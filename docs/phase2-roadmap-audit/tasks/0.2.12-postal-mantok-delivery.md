# 0.2.12 — Postal Mantok Delivery

## Goal

Let a player send one eligible manatka to a known recipient without requiring same-location presence, using explicit recipient opt-in, a delivery fee and the same reservation/replay guarantees as Safe Gifting.

## Scope

Base this on `docs/tasks/0.2.x-postal-mantok-delivery.md`.

- Sender selects one eligible owned stack unit.
- Sender selects a known recipient through a privacy-safe selector.
- Sender previews item, recipient and delivery fee.
- Recipient can accept or decline.
- Accept moves exactly one item transactionally.
- Decline/expiry/cancel release reservation and replay canonical state.
- Shynok sale, Mantok Chest, Munchkin barter, remort and safe gifting respect live mail reservations.

## Non-goals

- No item-for-item trade.
- No gold transfer between players.
- No anonymous mailbox or spam surface.
- No public exact-location or online-status disclosure.
- No market, auction, buyback or resale.
- No quest progress, referral reward or power bonus.

## Open decisions to settle first

- Recipient discovery: recent interaction, manual character code, saved known contacts, or another privacy-safe route.
- Fee formula and timing.
- Expiry and inbox visibility.

## Acceptance criteria

- One sender cannot reserve the same item stack in two live mail/gift flows.
- Recipient must opt in.
- Duplicate/stale callbacks cannot duplicate item or fee.
- Expired/declined delivery cannot leak location or online status.
- All item safety tags and protected states apply.

## Tests

- reservation concurrency;
- stale preview/content drift;
- accept/decline/expiry/cancel replay;
- fee spend/refund decision;
- privacy-safe recipient selector;
- sale/chest/barter/gift/remort reservation regressions.

## Manual QA

1. Send one eligible item to a known recipient.
2. Accept from recipient.
3. Replay accept.
4. Decline and expire separate deliveries.
5. Try protected/equipped/story/reserved items.
6. Confirm no exact recipient location is revealed.
