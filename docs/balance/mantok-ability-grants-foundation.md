# Mantok Ability Grants Foundation Balance Notes

This slice adds choices, not a broad power spike.

- Level band: first grant manatky stay inside current `9..13` content.
- Rarity and binding: every live grant item is epic, soulbound and effect-bearing equipment.
- Action economy: gear actions consume the normal committed action on each live surface: the persistent PvE turn, the party-boss/Big Barrel participant action or the turn-based duel choice. No hidden proc, free turn or extra action ships here.
- Cooldowns: gear cooldowns are ability-keyed and do not mirror native class-skill cooldown state. Active keyboards hide gear actions while mana/cooldown gates block them, and callback gates still reject stale or forged blocked presses without spending resources.
- Borrowed actions: borrowed Bard/Priest/Bureaucramancer/Varenyk/Drantohor/Molfar-style actions are weaker than native identity actions through smaller damage, higher cost, longer cooldown or smaller support.
- Bleed: current bleed is tiny visible chip damage, refresh-only and capped by remaining hero activations. It can finish combat, but only status-terminal kills suppress an extra monster response.
- Duels: turn-based duels may use eligible gear actions, but quick duels remain instant and ignore gear-action buttons. The slice adds no item theft, wager, extra PvP reward or quick-duel gear expansion.
- Party boss: Big Barrel gear support can reduce the incoming boss hit or restore a small amount of HP, but it does not change boss rewards, loss cooldowns, raid eligibility, one-use item economy or settlement ledgers.
- Achievements: the first-use gear-action achievement is rewardless and grants no XP, gold, items, title power, stats, combat power, loot odds or quest progress.
- Yeger cloak: the cloak is a documented service-perk marker only in this slice. It does not unlock dense bandages, field kits or Yeger boards.
- Loot: grant items are low-weight additive monster loot entries and do not replace base, trophy, coverage or set-piece loot.

Open balance follow-up: run simulator probes after manual QA confirms the first gear actions feel useful but not mandatory.
