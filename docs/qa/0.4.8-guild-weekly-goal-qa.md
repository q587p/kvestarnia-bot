# 0.4.8 Guild Weekly Goal — Manual Telegram QA

Status: pending on the exact PR head. This checklist is not evidence that QA,
deployment or production enablement happened.

## Local setup

Use an isolated non-production bot and four accounts. Phase A uses three members
of one active guild plus one nonmember. Phase B restores a clean QA snapshot and
uses two active guilds with two members each, so ties and pagination can be
observed without introducing any extra player account. The checkout `.env` needs:

```env
NODE_ENV=development
DEV_GRANT_COMMANDS_ENABLED=true
GUILD_FOUNDATION_ENABLED=true
LEFT_PASSAGE_PARTY_ATTACK_ENABLED=true
GUILD_WEEKLY_GOAL_ENABLED=true
```

Refresh the isolated snapshot with `refresh-local-bot.cmd`, then verify
`status-local-bot.cmd`, its exact SHA, polling-ready log and `/ready` before QA.

## Matrix

1. Open `/guild` as each Phase-A member. Confirm the same Kyiv/Holocene period,
   `0/13` progress, `0` Glory, the current weekly place, equal-contribution
   wording and no promised combat/reward bonus.
2. Form an ordinary left-passage party with two guildmates and the third member
   acting as support/healer/guard. Win once. Confirm progress becomes `1/13` for
   every guild viewer and support is not ranked below damage or named publicly.
3. Repeat the terminal callback, reopen the result, restart the bot and reopen
   `/guild`. Confirm the expedition still counts exactly once and Glory stays `0`.
4. Have one participant leave or be removed after the terminal timestamp, then
   run `/dev_guild_weekly`. Confirm the frozen contribution remains and no XP,
   gold, item, combat settlement or Chronicle duplicate appears.
5. Delete/recreate one contributor Character after the terminal receipt but
   before completion. Use `/dev_guild_weekly finish`; confirm `13/13`, exactly
   `13` Glory, one ordinary completion Chronicle row with `+13 Слави`, and one
   rewardless notice on each contributor's current Character. If a contributor
   has no Character, recreate it, open `/guild`, and confirm the pending notice
   arrives exactly once.
6. Repeat `finish`, terminal callbacks, scheduler repair and a bot restart.
   Confirm the same completion time, `13` Glory, Chronicle row, achievements and
   notices remain exact-once across remort/restart.
7. Open `📜 Книга слави` in `🪺 Гніздо ґільдій`. Confirm `✨ Слава` and
   `🏁 Першість` rank guilds only, are read-only, five rows per page and show only
   escaped active crest/name/aggregate. Confirm no player, roster, role,
   Telegram identity, location, token, contribution or exact completion time.
   Confirm both views and the private guild hub explain that only the weekly
   `13/13` grants `+13 Слави`, while daily quests and personal activities grant
   none. Complete or replay `Корчмарський обхід`; its reward card must preserve
   the XP/gold/item list and show the same source boundary only while the weekly
   feature is enabled.
8. From outside the Guild Nest, replay the board callback. Confirm it explains
   the location requirement and offers a safe return route. As the nonmember at
   the Nest, confirm the Book button and both public boards remain readable but
   show no own-guild appendix.
   On the same nonmember account, open `Мій код запрошення`: confirm the private
   code-management screen is edited first and a separate compact forwardable
   card arrives below it with the raw link and one open button. Forward only that
   card and confirm it contains no reserve-code, copy, regeneration or back text.
9. Disable `GUILD_WEEKLY_GOAL_ENABLED`, refresh the isolated bot and complete
   another ordinary party expedition. Confirm ordinary party play still works,
   the weekly/Glory hub section and Book entry are absent and no new weekly or
   Glory receipt appears. Re-enable it and confirm older evidence returns unchanged.
10. As the nonmember, form and finish an ordinary party expedition. Confirm the
   weekly goal does not block or redirect ordinary access and no guild progress
   is credited.
11. Cross Monday `00:00 Europe/Kyiv` with a Sunday terminal left unreconciled.
   Restart and repair on Monday. Confirm Sunday is credited to the old immutable
   `1YYYY-Www` period while the new week remains separate at zero.
12. In Phase B, complete both guilds with the same all-time Glory total. Confirm
   `✨ Слава` gives the same visible place to both; equal rows use stable
   name/id ordering without fabricating a better rank.
13. Seed at least seven active guild rows in the isolated QA database. Move
   previous/refresh/next across `✨ Слава`; confirm exactly five rows, clamped
   ends and the viewer guild's own place when it is outside the visible page.
14. For `🏁 Першість`, complete one guild earlier and leave the other incomplete.
   Confirm completed guilds come first by canonical completion order, then
   incomplete guilds by progress descending, without showing timestamps.
15. Give two incomplete guilds equal progress. Confirm they share one visible
   place; verify five-row overflow, deterministic paging and off-page own place.
16. Open `📜 Хроніки Квестарні`: the ordinary completion must appear in `Усе`
   and `Пригодники`, and must be absent from `Важливе`.
17. Accumulate three and then thirteen distinct completed contributor periods
   with the QA clock. Confirm the cumulative rewardless milestones arrive once
   per User/current Character and never expose or rank individual contribution.
18. Confirm there is no first-place achievement, material winner reward, Glory
   spending action, guild XP/level tree, capacity change or combat-stat change.
19. From a restored isolated snapshot, make A an ordinary contributor to B's
   completed guild period, then confirm A's `/dev_delete_account ПІДТВЕРДЖУЮ`.
   Confirm B still sees the same completed period, Glory, Chronicle and own
   achievement notice; A's next `/start` creates a fresh identity with no old
   weekly notice, contribution or pending claim.
20. Restore again with A as founder of a guild that owns completed weekly
   evidence. Confirm A's account reset succeeds, removes that guild/weekly
   evidence, and leaves the ordinary party/combat result reopenable without a
   credited-without-receipt error. Unrelated Guild B and its evidence must remain.

Record the exact SHA, accounts/roles, flag values, runtime snapshot, performed
steps and every omitted case. Keep migration deployment and production rollout
