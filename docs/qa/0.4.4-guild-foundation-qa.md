# 0.4.4 Guild Foundation QA

Status: automated correction gates pass. Manual three-account Telegram QA is
pending on the final exact PR #190 head.

## Runtime setup

Use the isolated local bot and three private test accounts. In checkout `.env`:

```env
NODE_ENV=development
GUILD_FOUNDATION_ENABLED=true
```

Run `refresh-local-bot.cmd`, then `status-local-bot.cmd`. Record the snapshot SHA
and copied runtime flag. Account A needs an eligible founder Character (level 5,
or level 3 after a remort) and at least 93 gold; B and C only need current
Characters. `/dev_guild_gold` may prepare A outside production. It must never
register, appear or mutate under `NODE_ENV=production`, even with the flag true.

## Three-account matrix

1. A previews `/guild_create 🛡️ Назва | короткий опис`; replace the preview,
   reuse the old confirmation, confirm twice and restart the bot between clicks.
   Expect one forming charter, one 93-gold debit, a non-refundable warning and a
   seven-day founder cooldown. Insufficient gold and expired preview spend zero.
2. B races an equivalent normalized name; another eligible account races a
   different name as the same founder. Expect one name winner where applicable,
   one founder charter within seven days and no extra debit. Check unsafe mixed
   script, reserved name, noncatalog crest and 94-grapheme description failures.
3. B creates `/guild_invite_code`; A uses `/guild_invite КОД`. Block the bot for
   B once: A must see that Telegram delivery is unconfirmed and B must recover
   the durable invitation through `/guild`. Inspect all cards for absence of
   token, Telegram identity, exact location and online/activity time.
4. B declines. Verify the same guild-target pair is blocked for seven days even
   through another officer. Verify 93-hour expiry, three incoming cap and three
   new invites per actor/rolling 13 minutes with canonical remaining waits.
5. Accept as B. The charter becomes active only now; A gets the founder event and
   B the join event once. Race two guild accepts for C and race the final eighth
   slot: one membership/slot wins. Every other incoming invitation terminalizes.
6. A edits crest/description, promotes/demotes B and C, checks the two-officer
   cap, and offers leadership. Only the nominee may accept. Officers can invite
   and cancel only invitations they created while still officers; demotion,
   kick or leave removes that authority. Old versioned buttons become stale.
   With two current members sharing one exact Character name, the role command
   must show two distinct numbered selectors and each must target its own stable
   membership.
7. Confirm a multi-member leader cannot leave or disband. After accepted
   transfer the old leader may leave. Sole leader disband succeeds and does not
   remove any existing party/combat. Do not infer a successor from activity.
8. Remort a member and confirm membership/leadership persist while current-life
   party/combat cleanup remains unchanged. During a recruiting party, `/restart`
   for leader and member must be blocked even without a combat lease. Active
   group combat also blocks it. An expired generic recruiting residue must not
   block restart, while ordinary unexpired, Big Barrel and left-passage
   automatic-start contracts remain protected. After a genuinely safe
   restart/recreate, the User returns to the same guild role.
9. Start a real ordinary left-passage or Big Barrel recruiting `PartySession`
   first, then open `/guild_party`. Confirm five-row pagination, ordinary invite
   copy, `joinSource=guild`, the new bottom-most canonical party card and its
   correct message reference. A nonmember still joins through an ordinary route.
   With no eligible live party, `/guild_party` creates nothing.
10. Race a guild leave/kick against the picker callback. The target must receive
    no guild-branded disclosure; stale callback recovers safely. Existing
    `PartySession` and `GroupCombatSession` rows stay unchanged.
11. With at most five active members, create at least six pending outgoing
    invitations. Navigate every page: members must not repeat, all invitations
    and cancellation actions remain reachable, and previous/refresh/next keep
    the displayed page. Run an exact-name role action while invitations overflow.
12. Keep an old guild-party invite button, set `GUILD_FOUNDATION_ENABLED=false`
    and refresh, then press it. It must resolve no recipient, send no Telegram
    invitation, publish no canonical party card and append no guild audit. `/guild` still reads safe
    state; nonleader leave, nominee acceptance and sole disband remain usable.
    Formation, invitation/accept, role/profile and guild-party writes are blocked.
    Restore the local value only if more QA is required.
