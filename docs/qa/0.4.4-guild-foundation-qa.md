gate passes with 4,420 unit and 720 integration tests. Manual three-account

## Runtime setup

Use an isolated local bot and three private test accounts. In checkout `.env`:

```env
NODE_ENV=development
GUILD_FOUNDATION_ENABLED=true
```

Then run `refresh-local-bot.cmd` and `status-local-bot.cmd`. Record the candidate
SHA and confirm the copied runtime `.env` contains the exact flag above. Each
account needs a current character; account A needs at least 587 gold. Use
`/dev_guild_gold` on A if needed. The helper must not register, appear or mutate
under `NODE_ENV=production`, even when the guild flag is true.

## Three-account matrix

1. A previews `/guild_create 🛡️ Назва | короткий опис`. Repeat the confirmation
   button and confirm exactly 587 gold is spent once and the same guild replays.
2. B attempts the same name with whitespace/case differences. Expect a
   uniqueness refusal and no gold spend. Try a reserved name, non-emoji crest,
   unsafe markup and overlong text; each fails without mutation.
3. A invites B. B declines; A reinvites after the shown wait; B accepts. Reuse
   every old button and confirm only a terminal/stale response. Repeat against
   C and inspect that no card exposes an invite token, Telegram identity, exact
   location or online timestamp.
4. Race A and another guild invitation for C. Exactly one accept may create
   membership. Replayed callbacks must not move C or emit a second join result.
5. A promotes/demotes B, transfers leadership to B, then reuses old role and
   transfer buttons. Confirm permissions and deterministic current state. A
   leaves and rejoins through a fresh invitation.
6. Remort one member and restart the isolated bot. Membership and safe display
   remain; the old-life party/combat state is neither adopted nor weakened.
7. From `/guild`, create the ordinary temporary party. Confirm only eligible
   guild members receive the private notice, a nonmember can still join through
   an ordinary allowed party link, and repeating `/guild_party` does not resend
   notices for the same live party.
8. With an ordinary `PartySession` and then a `GroupCombatSession` active, test
   leave, kick, leadership transfer and guild deletion. Neither session may be
   deleted or reassigned. A leader with another active combat lease cannot
   create a new ordinary party.
9. Set `GUILD_FOUNDATION_ENABLED=false`, refresh and confirm the real guild
   handlers/callbacks/dev helper are absent while the pre-release `/guild`
   placeholder remains harmless. Restore the local flag only for more QA.
