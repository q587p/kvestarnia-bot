# 0.4.5 — Guild Cosmetic Progression

Status: proposed, data-gated after at least one observed `0.4.4` weekly period.

## Goal

Turn repeated small-group participation into visible guild history and cosmetic
milestones without combat power, shared item custody or FOMO pressure.

## Activation gate

Before implementation, review privacy-safe `0.4.3`/`0.4.4` participation,
completion, repeat-party and timeout data. Retarget or defer this task if the
weekly goal does not produce a healthy repeated social ritual.

## Scope

- One small deterministic guild XP/level curve from existing exact-once weekly
  completion/contribution receipts.
- Bounded cosmetic milestones: crest frames/markers, profile line, Chronicle
  recap or rewardless titles as separately approved.
- Read-only guild history/next-milestone view with no hidden odds.
- Replay-safe backfill from canonical receipts, period repair and remort/member
  boundary policy.
- Privacy-safe aggregate metrics, flag/kill switch and staged rollout.

## Non-goals

- Combat stats, resource recovery, reward multipliers or paid power.
- Guild bank/items/gold, crafting, market or trade.
- Guild boss, wars, territory, season pass or infinite level grind.
- Retroactive credit from noncanonical chat/activity noise.

## Acceptance criteria

1. XP derives only from canonical eligible weekly receipts and is exact-once under
   retries, backfill, repair and concurrent period settlement.
2. Curve and caps are documented, bounded and simulation-tested; no individual
   member can create an uncapped farm or participant-count multiplier.
3. Every milestone is cosmetic/social only and has explicit achievement, title,
   Lore/Chronicle and copy decisions.
4. Join/leave/kick/remort cannot duplicate or remove already-earned guild history;
   personal history/privacy remains separate.
5. Backfill is idempotent, resumable and produces the same total as live receipt
   application without granting gameplay rewards.
6. UI shows current level/progress/next milestone compactly and does not expose
   exact private contribution timestamps or shame inactive members.
7. Kill switch hides fresh progression writes/UI as documented while ordinary
   guild membership, party and expedition flows remain intact.
8. Rollout proceeds only after recorded metrics, migration/restore, automated
   checks and three-account Telegram QA.

## Relevant files / search terms

- Guild weekly contribution/completion receipts and period helpers.
- achievement/title provenance, Chronicle privacy and cosmetic presentation.
- replay-safe backfill/repair patterns, flags and release-state ledger.

## Focused tests

- Curve/cap simulation and exact-once live/backfill parity.
- Period settlement, concurrent repair and membership/remort boundaries.
- Cosmetic-only invariant, privacy/logging and kill-switch isolation.
- Migration, `npm run check`, `git diff --check`.

## Manual Telegram QA

Use a three-member guild across prepared historical/current weekly receipts.
Open progress, cross milestones, retry/backfill/restart, change membership/remort
and toggle the flag. Confirm no combat/resources/items/rewards change.

## Release surfaces

- Version/changelog and spoiler-light Ukrainian news.
- Task/QA/design/balance curve, achievement/title/Lore/Chronicle decisions,
  release ledger, playtesting and compact AI context.
