# 0.4.3 — Party Expedition MVP

Status: proposed first production consumer of the generic group-combat runtime.

## Goal

Ship one authored, replay-safe 2–3 player versus 2–3 enemy expedition that gives
a small reason to regroup without replacing or inflating the solo loop.

## Scope

- One canonical location/quest/lore entry with clear eligibility and party flow.
- One authored encounter using only `0.4.1`-proven actions/targets/statuses.
- Tuned 2-player and 3-player variants within the same rules version.
- Idempotent per-player resource and capped reward settlement.
- Contribution policy that includes support and inactivity guardrails.
- Bounded journal/recap, participant result cards and safe Chronicle event.
- Rewardless achievements as explicitly approved; no hidden reward hooks.
- Simulator/balance report, funnel/repair/delivery metrics, default-off flag,
  kill switch, rollback and staged Telegram rollout.

## Non-goals

- Guild-only access or weekly guild progression.
- Rare economy-defining loot, unlimited farming or participant-count multiplier.
- Big Barrel migration, raid finder, >3×3 or new chat platform.
- New general quest engine or broad monster/item catalog expansion.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Eligible 2/3-player parties can discover, preview and start the encounter;
   blocked players get canonical reason/timer without partial state.
2. Two- and three-player tuning has a documented simulator envelope across
   classes/races and does not require one mandatory support class.
3. One terminal plan settles participant resources and capped rewards exactly
   once. Partial retries converge; duplicate callbacks/restarts cannot duplicate
   XP, gold, items, quest progress, achievements or Chronicle rows.
4. Contribution credit covers damage/heal/guard/control/damage taken/actions and
   excludes a zero-action passenger without publicly shaming them.
5. Reward budget is per encounter, not per enemy or unbounded participant count,
   and is compared with current solo/hour and Big Barrel rewards.
6. Restart/remort/timeout/repair/Telegram failures preserve combat and settlement
   invariants. Kill switch blocks fresh starts but lets active sessions finish or
   follow an explicit safe policy.
7. `🗺️ Квести`, location routing, Lore Board/canon and achievements catalog have
   explicit updates; player copy remains compact Ukrainian and avoids hidden odds.
8. Funnel, timeout, repair, delivery and settlement metrics contain no message
   content or individual public ranking.
9. Automated suites, simulations, migration/restore and 3-account Telegram QA
   pass before production flag enablement; observation and rollback are recorded.

## Relevant files / search terms

- Group combat session/participant/action/settlement.
- quest overview/location/lore/activity/achievement integration.
- reward budget, effective resources, loot idempotency and Chronicle privacy.
- flags, release ledger, playtesting and simulator commands.

## Focused tests

- Eligibility/start and 2/3-player authored encounter domain fixtures.
- Terminal settlement replay, participant retry and reward budget invariants.
- Restart/remort/timeout/repair and kill-switch behavior.
- Quest/lore/achievement/activity exact-once integration.
- Simulator matrix, migration, `npm run check`, `git diff --check`.

## Manual Telegram QA

Use three accounts and varied roles. Discover, form 2/3-player parties, play
win/loss/timeout, exercise support contribution, restart during settlement,
repair one Telegram card, toggle kill switch and verify quest/lore/Chronicle/
reward state. Record flags, SHA, runtime and unperformed cases.

## Release surfaces

- Version/changelog, player news with one short in-world paragraph.
- Task/QA/design/balance report, quest/lore/achievement decisions, playtesting,
  release-state ledger and compact AI context.
