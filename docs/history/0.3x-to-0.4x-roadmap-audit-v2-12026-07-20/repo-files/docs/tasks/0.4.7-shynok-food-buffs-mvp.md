# 0.4.7 — Shynok Food Buffs MVP

Status: proposed; not implementation-ready until the exact first-menu catalog and
status interaction matrix are accepted in this task.

## Goal

Let a player buy one short, funny Korchma meal as a gold sink and receive one
truthful preparation effect without turning food into a required win-rate tax.

## Design cutline

Older docs disagree between one and up to five simultaneous food buffs. The MVP
uses **one active food buff total**. A future evidence-backed task may widen that
cap. Food bought and eaten now is separate from carried consumable manatky.

Start with at most three authored meals. Each definition owns its price, exact
effect, duration/consumption boundary and supported modes. Use one canonical
effective-summary/status contract; do not calculate food power in presenters.

Food owns a separate current-life status/order model. It must not reuse the
one-row `CharacterDrinkState`, evict an active drink, or overload Sated,
Inspiration or Priest blessing storage.

## Activation gate

Before implementation, amend this task with the exact two or three meals: stable
content id, Ukrainian name, price, numerical effect, duration/consumption rule,
supported modes, replacement behavior and current/max HP/mana semantics. Add an
interaction table for drinks, `😋 Ситий`, Inspiration, Priest/greeting statuses,
remort and every supported combat mode. Include no-food simulations and reject
unused backlog candidates explicitly. Codex must not invent this catalog while
implementing persistence.

## Scope

- Compact Shynok food menu, detail and explicit purchase confirmation.
- Replay-safe purchase order: gold spend and active-status create/replace in one
  transaction, with current-life, price/content and replacement fingerprint.
- One food-owned active status per current life, visible expiry/next-fight
  wording, explicit replace confirmation and remort cleanup.
- Effects smaller than good equipment of the same level and covered by no-food
  baseline simulations.
- Supported solo/duel/PartyBoss/GroupCombat start, turn, timeout and release
  behavior according to each definition.

## Non-goals

- Coffee cooldown cycles, five-buff stacking, take-away items, item crafting,
  broad shop/market, XP/gold/loot multipliers or paid mandatory power.
- Hidden random effects in the MVP.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Insufficient-gold, duplicate, stale-price/content/current-life and concurrent
   replace confirms do not double spend or overwrite a newer status.
2. One active-food invariant holds; UI states exactly what is replaced and when
   the new effect ends or is consumed.
3. HP/mana maxima and current-resource changes are explicit, capped and tested;
   expiry/release cannot heal, kill or duplicate a pulse accidentally.
4. Every advertised combat/check mode consumes or ignores the status exactly as
   documented under restart, timeout, remort and terminal replay.
5. Balance simulations preserve acceptable no-food play; food creates no reward
   faucet and no guild/party requirement.
6. Migration/restore, focused/full checks, Telegram QA, staged flag and release
   ledger evidence exist before rollout.

- Accepted catalog/price/effect/consumption tables and no-food simulations.
- Purchase/replace duplicate, stale and concurrent transactions.
- Drink/status isolation plus HP/mana max/current expiry, timeout/flee/restart/
  remort and cross-mode parity.

## Manual Telegram QA

Buy each accepted meal, replay purchase, replace the active meal while a drink
and other statuses exist, enter/finish/flee/timeout every supported fight mode,
cross expiry/remort and confirm no hidden refill, eviction or reward change.
