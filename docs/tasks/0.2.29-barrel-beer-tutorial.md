# 0.2.29 Barrel Beer Tutorial

## Goal

Add a small level 2-5 newbie tutorial quest, `Бочка, або Туди і звідти`, that teaches players to find `Бочка`, complete the beginner solo Barrel raid, set out a beer round, drink beer and return to the quest table while the beer effect is active.

## Scope

- Add the quest as an extra quest-table option from levels 2 through 5 inclusive.
- Grant a replay-safe 39 gold stipend once on accept.
- Track accepted, visited Barrel, completed beginner solo Barrel raid, post-raid Shynok or legacy Tavern beer round offered, beer drunk and active-beer turn-in progress with existing daily-action state.
- Complete for small level-scaled XP and `Перстень Пивовладдя`.
- Unlock the rewardless first-completion achievement `achievement.quest.barrel-beer-tutorial`.
- Use the current `accessory` equipment slot because the repo has no separate `ring` slot.
- Reset quest progress on remort/current character life, matching the default character-scoped tutorial-state rule.

## Non-goals

- Do not add a new location, raid, beer, effect, inventory or equipment-slot system.
- Do not change beer duration or Shynok drink balance.
- Do not replace or block `Льохова справа`.
- Do not add a dev helper: this quest uses existing `/dev_*` routes for leveling/resetting and existing Barrel/Shynok actions; adding a quest-specific mutation helper would be redundant for the gate.
- Do not update `📖 Перекази`: the shipped behavior teaches an existing location/effect and does not change current lore-board facts.

## Acceptance criteria

- The quest appears from levels 2 through 5 and not below level 2 or for fresh level 6+ characters.
- Characters who accepted at levels 2-5 can still complete after leveling past 5.
- Accepting grants exactly 39 gold once and cannot be farmed by replaying accept.
- Completing only the Barrel raid is not enough.
- Having beer effect without post-accept route progress is not enough.
- Drinking beer without buying/setting out a post-raid round is not enough.
- Full post-accept route can complete while beer effect is active.
- If beer expires before turn-in, the quest keeps route and round progress and asks the player to drink beer again.
- Completion grants level-scaled newbie XP and the weak level 2 accessory once.

## Manual Telegram QA

- Start as a level 2 character.
- Open the quest table and confirm `Бочка, або Туди і звідти` appears next to `Льохова справа`.
- Accept the quest and verify the accept text plus 39 gold stipend.
- Navigate to `Бочка`, complete the beginner solo Barrel raid, then go to Shynok.
- Buy/set out a beer round, drink beer and return to the quest table while the effect is active.
- Turn in and verify scaled XP plus `Перстень Пивовладдя`.
- Repeat the near-fail path where beer expires before return; verify route and round progress remain and the retry text points back to beer.
- Confirm a fresh level 6+ character cannot newly take the quest.
