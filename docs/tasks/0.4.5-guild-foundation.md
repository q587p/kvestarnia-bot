# 0.4.5 — Guild Foundation

Status: PR #190 extends the default-off foundation with exclusive emoji crests
and forwardable invitation cards, while keeping the global guild row out of
newly rendered main keyboards. Exact-head CI evidence is maintained in the PR;
three-account Telegram QA, merge, deploy and the production flag remain pending.

Design: `docs/design/guilds-and-party-progression.md`

## Goal

Ship a durable, privacy-safe social shell for a small guild, give it the
canonical in-world discovery place `🪺 Гніздо ґільдій`, and reuse the ordinary
temporary `PartySession` contract without shared economy, progression or
combat power.

## Accepted contract

- Founder eligibility is level 5+, or level 3+ after at least one remort. Joining
  is free and has no progression gate.
- One replaceable creation preview per User lasts 13 minutes. Confirm debits 587
  gold exactly once, consumes a durable rolling seven-day founder cooldown,
  creates a seven-day `forming` charter and leader membership, and terminalizes
  all incoming invitations. The fee is non-refundable.
- The first second distinct User activates the guild. Only activation records
  `guild.created`; the founder and joiner achievement events use that durable
  activation result. Forming expiry closes memberships and invitations.
- Historical names remain stored. A separate reservation key releases at
  forming charter expiry plus 23 hours, or active disband plus 30 days.
- Names are 3–32 graphemes with canonical normalization and mixed-script/basic
  safety checks. Description is 0–93 graphemes. Each forming/active guild owns
  one exclusive emoji crest: either one of the 13 catalog choices or one custom
  emoji proposed through the guided reply. Terminal guilds release the
  reservation. `GUILD_INITIAL_MEMBER_CAPACITY` starts every guild at 8 active
  members and `GUILD_MAX_MEMBER_CAPACITY` fixes the absolute expansion boundary
  at 13; exactly one leader and at most two officers are enforced. This release
  ships no expansion action, price, entitlement or persisted capacity field, so
  current admission and every `n/8` surface remain on the initial boundary
  without a new migration.
- Target-bound opt-in codes replace global player-name lookup. Invite TTL is 93
  hours, with one live guild-target pair, at most three incoming invitations,
  at most three new invitations per actor per rolling 13 minutes, and a
  seven-day guild-target decline cooldown.
- `🪺 Гніздо ґільдій` at `Спуск до Низу` is the canonical public discovery
  entry. New persistent main keyboards do not render a global guild button and
  retain the canonical three-row composition with `👀 Хто поруч` beside
  `📖 Допомога`.
  Private `/guild`, target-bound deep links, invite responses and character-card
  shortcuts remain location-independent recovery paths. Creation explains eligibility
  and cost first, then asks for an available catalog crest or one custom emoji,
  name and optional description in separate steps before preview/confirm.
  Private Telegram share links invoke the
  canonical target-code invitation flow; accept/decline remain explicit
  target-side buttons and notify the inviter once. The personal invitation is a
  forwardable card with 13 distinct Ukrainian texts; `🎲 Згенерувати інший
  текст` changes only the share copy and never rotates the live link/token.
  The actionable private deep link appears in the card body so forwarding works
  without a separate copy action. Its instructions use separate mobile-readable
  paragraphs and emphasize the real `✅ Долучитися` / `✖️ Відхилити` buttons.
  Commands remain fallback routes.
- Leader may replace a custom emoji, switch between custom and an available
  catalog crest, or use `🔁 Лишити чинний емоджі` to edit only the description
  while preserving the exact current crest. Leader may also change roles, kick,
  offer leadership and sole-member disband. Current officers may invite and cancel only their own
  invitations. Leadership changes only after nominee acceptance. A
  multi-member leader cannot leave or disband; there is no inferred successor.
- Profile and role management are button-first: the leader opens `Учасники`,
  selects a stable membership row and then an authorized action. Duplicate
  Character names remain independently selectable. A one-page member list
  omits redundant `1/1` text and keyboard controls; real overflow keeps the
  current/total page indicator and navigation. An active member also sees `🏰
  Ґільдія` on the character card, including while its charter is forming.
- The private hero card shows the current forming/active guild's escaped crest
  and name below the title. `👀 Хто поруч` may prefix an already-visible active
  Character with the escaped active-guild crest, but exposes no guild name,
  role, membership id, Telegram identity or additional location/activity data.
- An active leader may invite a same-location nonmember from `👀 Хто поруч` only
  when that target already minted a live personal opt-in. The five-row picker
  hides redundant one-page counters and revalidates presence, leader authority,
  membership and target-bound consent before canonical delivery.
- Membership and leadership belong to `User` and survive remort and safe
  Character recreation. Live party/combat contracts still block `/restart`.

## PartySession and privacy boundary

- `/guild_party` is only a five-row picker for an already-existing eligible
  gameplay `PartySession` in recruiting state. It never creates a generic lobby.
- Every non-ready picker result retains `🏰 До ґільдії`, so the explanatory
  no-party/eligibility card cannot remove the only guild navigation control.
- Delivery reuses the ordinary invitation, `joinSource=guild` attribution and
  canonical party-card sender. Ordinary nonmember routes stay available.
- Guild membership/version and party eligibility are revalidated before
  delivery. Guild leave, kick, disband and safe restart do not mutate or own an
  existing `PartySession` or `GroupCombatSession`.
- Roster, invitations and audit expose no exact location, activity/online time,
  Telegram identity or invite token. Candidate, roster and invite pages contain
  at most five rows and callbacks stay within Telegram limits.

## Guild Nest and public directory

- The Nest is a low side chamber off `Спуск до Низу`, before `Ярус I`. It has a
  short landing card, a separate rules card, viewer-aware actions and an
  explicit `↩️ До Спуску` route. While the feature is enabled, the Spusk card
  itself mentions the low side passage, chamber, crests and postal slots so
  the button has an in-world signpost. Tavern code owns only the entrance; guild
  presenters/services own all stateful Nest and directory UI.
- It intentionally shares `PRESENCE_LOCATION_KORCHMA_DEEP`. Opening the Nest or
  directory renews neutral Spusk activity without moving the User or changing
  the current-location reply keyboard. Public reads require the viewer to be at
  that location; private hub, deep-link and recovery flows remain independent.
- The directory is a separate five-row DTO/query containing only active guild
  id, escaped emoji crest/display name and live current-member count. Ordering is
  normalized-name then stable id. Forming/expired/disbanded rows, rosters,
  leaders, roles, membership ids, Telegram identity, tokens, audits, timestamps,
  location, rankings and direct applications remain private.
- Public names/descriptions and custom emoji crests reuse canonical validation
  and HTML escaping.
  The repository has no general UGC reporting system, so this slice does not
  invent a parallel report workflow: rollout stays default-off, existing
  operator support handles abuse, and a future repository-wide moderation
  design owns any player report action.
- An existing member cannot mint or replace an invitation opt-in token. The
  repository validates membership transactionally and clears stale opt-in
  state when membership is created. Rejection stays privacy-uniform to others.

## Crest persistence boundary

- `Guild.crestReservationKey` is nullable and unique. Creation/profile
  transactions terminalize the specific overdue owner before claiming a crest;
  they do not depend on the bounded global cleanup page. UI availability is
  advisory, while the unique transactional claim decides create/create,
  create/edit and edit/edit races. A losing founder spends no gold, receives no
  cooldown and creates no charter or active preview side effect.
- Custom crest input accepts exactly one genuine Unicode RGI emoji sequence
  through the exact bot
  ForceReply and never accepts a photo, document, sticker, URL, text label or
  multiple emoji. Ordinary and subdivision/tag flags, keycaps, skin-tone and
  valid ZWJ sequences remain valid; lone regional indicators, arbitrary
  pseudo-ZWJ constructions and text decorated with variation selectors do not.
  The player-visible normalized sequence retains its presentation selectors
  (`❤️`, `👩‍⚕️`), while selector-less/FE0F aliases canonicalize only to a
  separate stable reservation key; explicit FE0E text-presentation input is
  rejected and catalog aliases still render the catalog's canonical form.
  Availability is advisory in the picker and authoritative in the
  creation/profile transaction. Occupied input returns a short recovery and
  another emoji prompt. Unsupported media or link input receives another exact
  prompt only after flag, membership/authority, live status and expected-version
  validation; stale/forged replies are inert.
- The existing additive custom-crest storage migration is retained unchanged
  for branch compatibility, but 0.4.5 exposes no image-upload, image-view or
  Telegram-media mutation route. Saved pre-release media references are neither
  displayed nor copied into player text, callbacks, audit data or directory DTOs;
  legacy photo prompts and callbacks recover inertly through the emoji menu.
- `prisma/migrations/20260806120000_guild_custom_crests` is additive and has a
  paired rollback. The original `20260802230000_guild_foundation` migration is
  unchanged. Migration smoke preserves User, Character, PartySession and
  GroupCombatSession rows across apply, rollback and restore. Deployment must
  preflight that no two existing forming/active rows already share a catalog
  crest; the unique-index migration intentionally stops instead of silently
  choosing an owner for invalid pre-release data.

## Rollout and non-goals

`GUILD_FOUNDATION_ENABLED` remains false by default. Disabled mode preserves
rows and safe reads, nonleader leave, accepted leadership transfer and
sole-member disband, while blocking formation, acceptance/new invitations,
roles, profile changes and guild-party invitations. `/dev_guild_gold` exists
only outside production.

No bank, shared gold/items, trade, market, guild XP/levels, buffs, weekly goal,
boss, chat, alliance, war, territory, PvP or guild-owned party/combat state is
part of 0.4.5. Quests do not change. `📖 Перекази` adds the Nest under `⬇️ Низ`
and points the charter entry to it without promising a bank, shared party or
combat ownership. The rewardless create/join achievements remain authoritative;
opening or browsing the directory is not a meaningful achievement and adds no
new definition. No quest is added.

Public/private recruitment policy, announcement/contact fields and directory
applications are future design work. The 0.4.5 directory remains read-only and
joining remains target-bound through a recipient's private opt-in.

## Production-enable blocker

An abandoned multi-member leader has no presence-derived automatic successor.
Before target enablement, the release operator owns a runbook decision: either
keep the flag off or use an audited operator-only recovery with an explicitly
approved inactivity threshold and nominee/consent procedure. No such operator
mutation is shipped in 0.4.5.
