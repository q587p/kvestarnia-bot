# 0.4.5 — Guild Foundation

Status: proposed small social shell after the first production GroupCombat consumer.

Design: `docs/design/guilds-and-party-progression.md`

## Goal

Let 3–5 friends create a durable small guild identity and repeatedly form an
ordinary temporary party, without adding shared economy or combat power.

## Scope

- `Guild`, `GuildMember`, durable invite and audit persistence with safe indexes.
- Normalized unique display name, emoji crest and bounded short description.
- Previewed replay-safe guild creation gold sink.
- Invite/accept/decline/cancel, join/leave, leader transfer and officer/member
  role changes.
- One-guild membership policy with explicit same-life/remort identity.
- Guild roster without exact online/location tracking.
- Create a normal `PartySession` and privately invite eligible guild members.
- Rate limits, stale callback safety, combat-flow compatibility, moderation-ready
  audit and default-off rollout flag.
- Read-only privacy-safe funnel counters and Telegram QA.

## Non-goals

- Guild bank, shared items/gold, trade or market.
- Guild XP, weekly objective, boss, buffs or reward power.
- Guild chat, alliances, wars, territory, PvP or matchmaking.
- Making guild membership required for party combat.
- Coupling Guild rows to active GroupCombat rows.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Eligible player sees creation preview and pays the exact configured sink once;
   duplicate/stale confirm replays the same guild without a second charge.
2. Unicode/name normalization, length, reserved-name and uniqueness rules are
   server-owned and race-safe; two concurrent creates cannot claim one name.
3. Invites reveal no private location/online state and cannot spam indefinitely.
   Accept/decline/cancel/expire is replay-safe and one player cannot join two
   guilds through races.
4. Leader/officer/member permissions are enforced transactionally. A guild never
   becomes leaderless; leave/delete flows require explicit transfer or safe
   deterministic succession.
5. Remort preserves guild membership but refreshes current character-life display
   safely; active party/combat remains tied to its frozen life.
6. Guild leave/kick/restart cannot cascade-delete an existing PartySession or
   GroupCombatSession. Active combat blockers remain authoritative.
7. Creating a party from guild UI calls the ordinary PartySession contract; a
   nonmember may still join through normal allowed routes and the guild owns no
   combat state.
8. Roster shows only safe identity/role data, not exact activity timestamp or
   location. Audit/logs contain no invite token or private Telegram identity.
9. New player-visible actions have explicit rewardless achievement and lore/
   quest decisions; create/join copy follows Ukrainian style and shows timers.
10. Flag defaults off, migration/rollback is proven and manual 3-account QA is
    recorded before target enablement.

## Relevant files / search terms

- User/Character life identity, remort and restart.
- PartySession invitation/privacy patterns and normalized presence identity.
- transfer/postal invite dedupe/audit patterns.
- gold mutation/receipt helpers, callback token conventions and environment flags.
- achievements catalog, Lore Board, Chronicles and quest overview decisions.

## Focused tests

- Normalization/uniqueness and concurrent create/accept races.
- Gold exact-once, invite lifecycle, role permissions/succession and remort.
- Guild leave/kick/restart while party/group combat exists.
- Privacy/logging, callback bounds/staleness and production flag isolation.
- Migration, `npm run check`, `git diff --check`.

## Manual Telegram QA

Use three accounts: create, duplicate name, invite/decline/reinvite/accept, role
changes, leadership transfer, leave/rejoin, remort, blocked combat transitions
and forming a normal party. Inspect privacy, gold, stale buttons, timers and
feature off/on. Record target flag and rollback evidence.

## Release surfaces

- Version/changelog and spoiler-light Ukrainian news.
- Design/task/QA, setup/playtesting, roadmap and compact AI context.
- Decide one or two rewardless achievements (for example first create/first join)
  without power. Add Lore/Chronicle copy only if it introduces canonical guild
  terminology; do not invent a guild boss or season promise.
