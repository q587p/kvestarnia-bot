# 0.4.7 — Universal Battle Statistics, Richer Korchma Rounds, and Manatka Dismantling

Status: implementation complete in PR #196 from merged `0.4.6`
(`origin/main` at `6b5ab40a` when the task branch was created). Automated
validation is complete; Manual Telegram QA, merge and deployment remain
separate and unclaimed.

## Goal

Make truthful terminal battle statistics universal, turn the Daily Korchma
Round into a meaningful deterministic daily reward, and add a safe one-unit
forge dismantling sink for canonical equippable manatky.

## Scope

### Universal battle statistics

- Preserve GroupCombat as the golden eight-dimension implementation and extend
  the separate terminal-only `📊 Статистика` card to persistent solo PvE,
  Training Doppelgänger, PartyBoss/Big Barrel, turn duels, quick duels and the
  starter Mimic-Shawarma probe.
- Keep the canonical order: damage dealt, actual healing, actual prevention,
  successfully applied control, damage taken, committed actions, committed
  specials and committed defensive turns.
- Generalize the shared presenter for partial persisted evidence: a supported
  zero renders as `0`; an unprovable value renders as unavailable and is never
  inferred from prose, authored values, current state, hidden odds or truncated
  logs.
- Persist versioned authoritative accumulators for solo/Training and PartyBoss;
  derive duel facts only from immutable action ledgers; freeze the starter probe
  payload in `DailyAction.resultJson`; show only already-public quick-duel facts.
- Terminal callbacks are read-only, participant-authorized, replay-stable and
  below Telegram callback/card limits. Active-fight clicks refresh the canonical
  card without settling or mutating combat state.

### Daily Korchma Round rewards

- Preserve the Kyiv-calendar daily identity and XP formula
  `2 * level + deterministicRandom(1..level)`.
- Set gold to `13 + level + deterministicRandom(1..level)`, grant exactly
  `13 * item.iskrokamin` and `1 * item.dense-bandage`, and disable the old
  quest Iskrokamin bonus for this claim.
- Add a deterministic identity-manatka branch: `13%` at baseline LUCK, plus
  one percentage point per full LUCK above `6`, capped at `23%`. LUCK affects
  only whether the branch opens; canonical `tavern_event` candidate weights
  choose the item without a second LUCK modifier. Force a
  featured drop after six consecutive completed reward rows without one; missed
  calendar days do not affect the streak.
- Select only a base `+0` equippable candidate with positive current class,
  race, canonical-title affinity or an explicit matching hard requirement.
  Never grant an incompatible fallback; persist `no-candidate` evidence and
  still settle the base reward safely.
- Freeze rules version, roll/branch, pity decision, selected item, matched
  identity kind and actual applied grants in the stored result. Legacy rows
  replay without top-up; repository-applied grants remain authoritative.
- Preserve Daily Korchma offer/step/reward rows and bounded featured-drop
  history across remort so same-day and seven-completion guarantees cannot reset.
- Extend a non-production-only Korchma helper to exercise miss, hit and forced
  seventh-completion branches without production registration or mutation.

Expected gold ranges are `17..19` at level 3, `22..29` at level 8 and `27..39`
at level 13.

### Forge manatka dismantling

- Add `♻️ Розібрати манатку` inside `✨ Чароковальня`, reusing its current
  location, level/remort, field-kit, callback and return-navigation contracts.
- Dismantle one owned canonical equippable weapon, armor or accessory per
  irreversible confirmation. Exclude resources, consumables, cosmetics, junk,
  unknown content, priceless/story/memory/sentimental/quest-critical items,
  equipped IDs, active reservations and protected last copies.
- Ordinary characters pay `5` gold. `class.mage`, `class.varenyk-mancer` and
  `class.bureaucramancer` pay `5` mana after canonical regeneration, with no
  gold fallback.
- Yield is
  `rarityBase + ceil(0.42 * nominalCumulativeIskrokaminUpgradeCost)`, where the
  rarity bases are common `1`, uncommon `2`, rare `3`, epic `5`, legendary `8`.
  Replay the real stepwise upgrade-cost function, including canonical set and
  legendary modifiers; item provenance does not affect yield.
- Paginate every eligible row with deterministic ordering. Preview exact item,
  level, quantity, yield, payment and resulting balance; preview reserves
  nothing.
- Confirmation uses one atomic transaction to recheck current life, access,
  regenerated resources, exact quantity, equipment/reservations, content/rules
  fingerprint, protection and yield, then writes one durable receipt, spends
  exactly once, removes one item and grants the frozen Iskrokamin output.
- Bind the compact opaque guard and receipt to remort life, item/base ID,
  upgrade, rarity/set fingerprint, expected quantity, yield, payment and rules
  version. Duplicate confirmation replays the receipt; concurrent last-unit
  confirmation has at most one success; old-life callbacks are stale.

## Integrity and balance decisions

- Statistics never affect rewards, settlement, quests, achievements, analytics
  or public activity. Existing GroupCombat and Big Barrel eligibility/economics
  remain unchanged.
- The featured-drop branch is `13%` at LUCK `6` or below and rises by one
  percentage point per full LUCK above `6`, capped at `23%`; the canonical
  candidate weights themselves remain LUCK-independent. The seven-completion
  guarantee is bounded by six prior rows and overrides the chance roll.
- Daily base grants and dismantling are exact-once under duplicate, concurrent,
  stale, restart and remort paths.
- Dismantling is a deterministic inventory/currency sink with no failure chance,
  bulk action, daily cap, buyback, provenance reconstruction or resale coupling.
- Every producer recognized by the shared active-reservation predicate was
  audited. Item-use preview/confirmation, gift creation/acceptance, postal draft
  confirmation, equipment writes, Mantok Chest creation/refresh/confirmation,
  Level Barter settlement and Shynok sale creation/refresh/confirmation use the
  same item-stack serialization seam with bounded retry and stable exhausted-
  contention outcomes. Restore-to-full is restricted to its canonical bandage,
  so it is domain-disjoint from dismantle-eligible gear, but it still uses the
  same protocol and stable `reserved` fallback.
- No new Chronicle event or achievement is added for statistics or dismantling:
  statistics are a read-only detail surface and dismantling is an irreversible
  inventory service whose repeated use should not create public spam. Existing
  shared inventory achievement behavior is preserved when applicable.
- Review `📖 Перекази`; update lore only if the visible forge contract makes the
  current in-world text inaccurate. No quest-overview change is expected because
  no quest state or route changes.

## Non-goals

- Leaderboards, lifetime combat analytics, ranking/shaming, hidden-odds
  disclosure, FightService rewrite or item-instance provenance.
- New currency, resale integration, Korchmar recycling, bulk dismantling or
  unrelated Old Altar/guild work.
- Retroactive reward top-ups or reinterpretation of legacy combat evidence.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Every shipped terminal combat surface exposes truthful replay-stable
   statistics with missing-versus-zero semantics and correct participant access.
2. Win/loss/flee/surrender/timeout/expiry/restart/stale/duplicate and legacy
   evidence paths do not mutate combat or rewards.
3. Daily level 3/8/13 rewards, exact item quantities, bounded 13..23% LUCK
   branch boundaries, hit/miss, forced seventh completion, reset after an
   actually applied featured grant, missed-day behavior and remort history are
   deterministic and exact-once.
4. All live class/race/title profiles have at least one identity-relevant
   candidate; corrupt no-candidate profiles settle safely and are observable.
5. Dismantling matches every formula row and canonical modifier path; payment,
   inventory decrement and Iskrokamin grant are atomic and replay-safe.
6. Every eligibility, equipped/reserved/protected-last-copy, stale fingerprint,
   quantity, life, access and resource branch is rechecked at confirmation.
7. Eligible rows are fully reachable through deterministic pagination; maximal
   names are HTML-safe and every callback/card remains within Telegram limits.
8. Selling, upgrading, equipment, chest runs, transfers, remort, rewards,
   public events and all existing combat settlement remain regression-covered.
