# 0.4.10 — Shynok Resale Listings

Status: proposed after inventory-volume and economy evidence.

## Goal

Keep eligible high-value units sold to Korchmar in a server-owned resale stock so
another player can buy them at the full canonical value.

This is not the already-shipped `0.1.24` player sale for a 42% rounded-up payout;
it is the deferred public resale shelf. Recycling follows separately in `0.4.11`.

## Scope

- On eligible sale, `goldValue >= 93` creates/increments an aggregate listing in
  the **same transaction** as current item consumption and 42% seller payout.
  A unique source-sale/line receipt prevents retry from intaking the unit twice.
- Listing identity uses item id, unit price and a semantic content fingerprint
  covering every sale/eligibility/gameplay-relevant field, not only the current
  narrow sale-selection fingerprint.
- Buy preview creates an opaque current-buyer-life purchase intent bound to the
  listing/fingerprint/price. Confirm atomically claims that unique receipt,
  decrements gold/listing quantity and grants inventory; terminal replay cannot
  buy another unit while stock remains.
- Quarantine content-fingerprint drift instead of selling changed content.
- Pagination, insufficient-gold/stale listing copy, audit and flag/kill switch.
- Shared destructive-inventory eligibility/reservation contract for the source
  sale path; no seller provenance or future royalty.

## Non-goals

- Player-set prices, auctions, bids, seller proceeds, item trade, item instances,
  Korchmar recycling or scheduler.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Use one inclusive threshold, `>= 93`, everywhere; preserve the existing sale
   payout calculation and seller result. Sale retry cannot duplicate intake.
2. Duplicate/concurrent buyers cannot oversell, double spend or double grant;
   one purchase intent has one terminal receipt/result.
3. Missing/drifted content quarantines a listing without mutating buyer gold.
4. Equipped/protected/story/priceless/reserved units cannot enter stock.
5. Aggregate stack identity is sufficient and documented; no per-copy mutable
   properties or seller provenance are promised.
6. Economy simulation, migration/restore, focused/full checks, multi-account
   Telegram QA and staged rollout evidence exist.

## Focused tests

- Sale+payout+listing-intake atomicity and duplicate source-receipt replay.
- Opaque purchase intent replay, two-buyer races, insufficient gold and seller
  payout regression.
- Semantic fingerprint drift/deletion quarantine, migration/restore and flag-off.

## Manual Telegram QA

Sell a qualifying item, replay sale, buy with another account, replay/race two
buyers, drift/remove content in local QA, toggle the flag and verify payout plus
inventory/listing quantities.
