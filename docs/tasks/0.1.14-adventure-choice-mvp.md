# 0.1.14 — Adventure Choice MVP

Ship `Adventure Choice MVP` as version `0.1.14`.

## Goal

Replace the old flat `/adventure` scene with a compact choice-driven loop: once per 93-minute period, a level 3+ player gets three deterministic tavern problems, chooses one, then chooses a resolution approach with different risk and reward. Keep the level 1-2 starter mimic-shawarma adventure available as the onboarding adventure before the new loop opens.

## Scope

- Add a generated adventure offer screen with three distinct choices per player and period from a broad tavern-problem pool that includes general, race-specific, class-specific and current-title-specific problems.
- Add three approaches for each problem:
  - safe: lower reward, low complication chance;
  - class-flavored clever/social/magical option: medium reward and risk;
  - risky: higher reward and higher complication chance.
- Route complications into the existing persistent solo fight path.
- Keep rewards conservative and idempotent through the existing daily-action reward ledger.
- Add Ukrainian Квестарня-style copy for offers, approaches, results, stale callbacks and safety states.
- Add stable per-problem icons to adventure offer rows and problem-choice buttons so the three-choice screen is easier to scan without changing callback payloads.
- Keep pre-choice approach copy qualitative: no exact XP/gold/item/odds spoilers before the player commits.
- Preserve the starter mimic-shawarma path for levels 1-2, including legacy action callbacks and idempotent daily-action rewards.
- Show starter mimic-shawarma from Quest Hub only while that starter adventure is actually ready; completed starter claims must not stay as active hub rows or buttons.
- Keep the Quest Hub focused on quest-table work: do not advertise the separate Fighting Corner in the table intro and do not show a direct Fighting Corner shortcut from the table.
- Add `/dev_adventure_reset` for non-production manual QA so the current 93-minute adventure claim can be cleared.

## Non-goals

- No new combat engine.
- No new migrations or schema changes.
- No shops, crafting, trading, broad loot economy or social combat expansion.
- No duplicate adventure sessions beside the existing daily-action claim and persistent fight protections.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Offer generation returns three distinct deterministic choices for the same player and period.
- The tavern problem pool is several times larger than the three visible choices.
- Every active race and class has at least three matching adventure problems, every current combo title has at least one matching problem, and a matching offer includes at least one personalized problem when available.
- Problem and approach callbacks parse safely, stay under Telegram callback length limits and reject stale/invalid payloads.
- Approach selection applies ordered risk/reward: safe < flavored < risky.
- A complication can start the existing persistent fight flow.
- Duplicate callback presses and reward replays do not grant a second reward.
- No-character, level-gate and active-fight states block the adventure safely.
- Level 1-2 players still reach the starter mimic-shawarma adventure from `/adventure` and Quest Hub instead of seeing only the level 3 gate.
- Completed level 1-2 starter mimic-shawarma claims do not remain actionable in Quest Hub.
- Offer/problem presenter text keeps choices visually separated, uses qualitative pre-choice approach copy, and formats the selected failed method without awkward quote wrapping.
- Adventure offer rows and problem-choice buttons show matching per-problem icons while callbacks still use compact problem ids.
- Quest Hub does not show Fighting Corner promo text or a direct Fighting Corner button from the quest table.
- Presenter output escapes player/content HTML.
- `/dev_adventure_reset` works only where dev reset commands are enabled.

## Manual Telegram QA

1. Use a level 1-2 character and confirm `/adventure` plus Quest Hub still show the starter mimic-shawarma adventure.
2. Repeat a starter shawarma action callback and confirm no duplicate reward.
3. Open Quest Hub after the starter shawarma claim is completed and confirm the shawarma row/button is gone.
4. Create or use a level 3+ character with no active persistent fight.
5. Open `/adventure` or the Quest Hub adventure button and confirm three problem choices with matching icons on rows and buttons.
6. Pick a problem, confirm three approaches with readable spacing, then resolve it.
7. Repeat the same callback and confirm no duplicate reward.
8. Use `/dev_adventure_reset` locally and confirm `/adventure` can show a fresh choice again in the same period.
9. Force an active fight and confirm `/adventure` routes to the active-fight safety state.
