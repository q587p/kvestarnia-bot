# 0.1.15 — Combat Lock and Battle Flow Polish

Resolved from `combat-lock-battle-flow-polish-task-draft (2).md` as the next shipped numbered release after Adventure Choice MVP.

## Goal

Make battle a real locked gameplay state, not a background activity. An unfinished combat session must block normal navigation, rewards, new activities, stale callbacks, and ordinary location changes server-side until combat reaches a terminal state.

Also polish battle flow by:

1. Making no-mana and on-cooldown combat attempts spend the hero turn and let the monster respond.
2. Adding short cooldowns to successful non-mana class actions so ordinary attacks remain meaningful.
3. Hiding mana-costing action buttons while current mana is insufficient, with future-safe reappearance if mana is restored during combat.
4. Adding a small monster-rest cooldown after three eligible monster fights.
5. Showing the existing `Порада дня` mechanic on battle start.
6. Moving level 3+ ordinary/problem fight starts to `Глибка`.
7. Keeping Yeger quest selection, help, and turn-in at the Barrel-side Yeger corner while moving active trail work to the outdoor hunt screen and preserving source-agnostic Yeger progress.
8. Showing a short health-recovered notice when passive regeneration first brings HP back to full during player-driven `/hero` or `/fight` flows.

## Baseline Preserved

- Adventure Choice MVP remains intact: level 3+ three-problem adventure offers, level 1-2 starter mimic-shawarma, adventure complication fights through persistent solo combat, `/dev_adventure_reset`, qualitative pre-choice copy, stable icons, and no direct Fighting Corner shortcut from the quest table.
- Adventure complication handoff rollback/idempotency is preserved: if a zero-reward adventure claim cannot hand off to persistent combat, the period is not consumed incorrectly.
- Completed starter mimic-shawarma lookups still do not create actionable starter presence/buttons.
- Personalized adventure copy keeps the declined-name or neutral-phrasing rule for dynamic race/class/title/item/monster/place names.
- Existing `Порада дня` mechanics and the `Лютнева Довгожителька` / `Бочка` tip are reused, not duplicated.
- The old free-retry no-mana behavior and copy were retired.

## Scope Delivered

### Central Active Combat Lock

- Added a central combat-lock middleware after presence routing and before gameplay handlers.
- Active persistent solo fights and active training doppelganger fights redirect normal commands/callbacks back to the active fight screen.
- Actual combat turn callbacks, `/help`, and `/version` remain allowed.
- Pending Friday Barrel raid protection keeps priority for callback blocks before combat-lock redirect.
- Blocked navigation does not overwrite presence with hall/table/bar/deep/front/inventory locations.

### Failed Action Turns and Skill Cooldowns

- Current-turn hidden/forged magic callbacks without enough mana now waste the hero action, run the monster phase, advance the turn, persist once, and show a short Ukrainian failure line.
- Current-turn hidden/forged non-mana skill callbacks while on cooldown use the same failed-turn semantics.
- Truly stale session/turn callbacks remain stale and idempotent.
- Successful zero-mana class skills now receive deterministic `3..5` hero-turn cooldowns from the skill stat plus a small luck effect.
- Cooldowns live in serializable combat state and tick only when combat time advances.
- Fight presenters show the remaining skill-rest turns with Ukrainian pluralization.
- Persistent and training fight keyboards recompute action availability on every render from current combat state.

### Monster Rest

- After three recent eligible ordinary `Глибка` monster fights, the next ordinary start is blocked for three minutes.
- Adventure handoffs are excluded from monster rest so a failed complication handoff still rolls back correctly.
- Training doppelganger and legacy/unmarked sessions are excluded.

### Battle Start Tips

- Newly started persistent battles show the existing `Порада дня` flavor line once at battle intro.
- Restores, lock redirects, stale callbacks, and turn renders do not spam the tip.
- The existing `raid.prep-hint` pool was expanded without creating a second combat-only tip system.

### `Глибка` and Yeger Surfaces

- Quest Hub routes level 3+ ordinary/problem fights through the exact button `До глибки`.
- Stale old quest-table fight callbacks open a fresh `Глибка` fight surface instead of mutating the old table message.
- `Глибка` owns the ordinary/problem fight difficulty/start UI.
- Yeger still sits by the Barrel for quest selection, target help, and turn-in.
- Active Yeger trail work moves outside: active quests show `Надвір` at Yeger and `До полювання` from the front-door screen.
- Yeger progress remains source-agnostic by matching monster type/tag through the existing persistent fight path.

### Health Recovery Notice

- Passive out-of-combat resource sync now reports a narrow `hp-full` recovery notice when HP crosses from below maximum to full.
- `/hero` and `/fight` show a short Ukrainian notification before the normal screen in reply mode, and prefix the edited screen in callback/edit mode.
- The notice points players back toward fights, duels, or other risky choices without changing HP math, mana recovery, combat state, or reward state.
- This remains lazy/player-driven; it does not add a durable background scheduler, proactive chat notification table, or healing item economy.

## Non-Goals

- No threat-streak or multi-enemy implementation.
- No mana-restoring manatky/items.
- No proactive background HP notification scheduler.
- No combat items or battle-escape item.
- No duel-combat runtime.
- No shops, crafting, trading, guilds, raids, Mini App UI, monetization, schema migration, or new production dependency.

## Focused Tests

- Domain combat tests cover action availability, no-mana failed turns, cooldown failed turns, and zero-mana skill cooldown length.
- Fight service tests cover persistent no-mana failed turns, monster-rest cooldown, stale turns, reward idempotency, adventure handoff safety, Yeger matching, and problem progress.
- Resource, hero, and fight service tests cover the one-shot HP recovery notice from passive regeneration.
- Fight command tests cover the separate recovery notice before the fight screen.
- Presenter tests cover failure copy and cooldown/fight rendering.
- Callback HTML tests cover central route behavior and stale quest fight safety.
- Training doppelganger service tests cover the shared combat semantics.

## Maintenance Notes

- Starter mimic-shawarma completion is still inferred from the current action history the lookup can prove. The current model has no durable once-ever completion marker for the old starter flow, so future work that needs lifelong starter completion should add an explicit persistence slice instead of guessing from absence.
- Loot Expansion v1 still normalizes generated legacy class/race/title ids at the adapter boundary in `src/content/lootExpansionV1.ts`. If the generated loot pack is regenerated with new legacy ids, update the alias/surrogate maps and equipability tests there; this release does not add a persistent title system.

## Manual QA Notes

1. Create a level 3+ character, take a Korchmar problem in the Shynok, then open Quest Hub and press `До глибки`.
2. Start a fight from `Глибка`; confirm `Порада дня` appears only on the new battle intro.
3. While a fight is active, press hall/table/front/bar/inventory/adventure/Yeger callbacks from older messages; confirm they return to the active battle instead of mutating state.
4. Use a mage with insufficient mana and an old skill callback; confirm the monster acts and the turn advances.
5. Use a zero-mana skill twice from a stale/current-turn hidden button path; confirm the second attempt wastes the turn while the skill remains on cooldown.
6. Finish three ordinary eligible `Глибка` fights quickly; confirm the next ordinary start shows the short monster-rest block.
7. Open Yeger by the Barrel, take the quest, go `Надвір`, and confirm trail actions still count matching unquiet monster progress from eligible persistent fights.
8. Leave a character below full HP until passive recovery would fill HP, then open `/hero` or `/fight`; confirm the recovery notice appears once and the normal screen still follows.
