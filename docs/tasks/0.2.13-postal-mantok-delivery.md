# 0.2.13 - Postal Manatka Delivery MVP

## Goal

Ship `📮 Пошта Квестарні`: a narrow paid courier delivery that lets a sender offer a bounded package of eligible manatky to a known recipient without requiring same-location presence. Confirmed packages leave the sender inventory for postal custody, and the recipient must explicitly accept before items enter their inventory.

## Scope

- Known recipients come from previous completed item-transfer history, accepted/active/resolved duel history, or explicit Bard performance reactions (`applauded`/`tipped`) in either direction.
- A postal package may contain 1 to 5 distinct `itemId` stack types.
- Each selected type may contain 1 to 93 units, capped by currently owned, eligible, unreserved quantity.
- Package editing uses server-owned draft tokens; callback data never encodes item lists.
- Confirm charges the sender fee, moves every selected quantity out of the sender inventory into postal custody, and turns a draft into one live pending postal reservation per sender.
- Acceptance is all-or-nothing and replay-safe: every line enters the recipient inventory once, or none enters.
- Delivery fee is charged immediately at confirmation/send time; if the sender lacks gold, the package stays a draft and no items are moved.
- Fee formula is internal and tested: `5 + selected type count`.
- Pending postal links expire after one week.
- Decline, cancel and expiry return postal-custody items to the sender once, while stale callbacks release or replay the canonical postal state.
- Sender or receiver remort cancels pending postal custody without delivery: custody items return to the original sender once, and the sent delivery fee is not refunded.
- Existing one-unit same-location Safe Gifting keeps its old flow.

## Non-goals

- No item-for-item trade.
- No gold transfer between players.
- No market, auction, buyback, resale, royalties or dynamic pricing.
- No anonymous inbox, public recipient search, exact-location disclosure or online-status leak.
- No package larger than 5 distinct manatka types.
- No per-type quantity above 93.
- No unbounded cart, bulk dump or send-all shortcut.
- No XP, quest progress, power bonus, achievement reward, donor perk, party/raid/guild/combat work or Mini App UI.

## Acceptance criteria

- Sender opens `📮 Пошта Квестарні`, sees known recipients from explicit social history, and recipient rows do not reveal current location or online status.
- Sender can select 1..5 eligible owned manatka types and 1..93 units per selected type.
- Preview shows recipient, each line, selected quantity, exact delivery fee, fee formula and expiry.
- Confirm rejects empty packages, duplicate item ids, more than 5 types, quantity 0/94, over-owned quantity, reserved, equipped, missing, unknown or drifted stacks.
- Confirm rejects insufficient sender fee gold before the package is sent.
- Recipient sees full package summary and accepts or declines explicitly.
- Accept delivers all postal-custody package lines exactly once; the sender fee has already been paid.
- Confirm blocks stale, missing, under-quantity, newly equipped or reserved sender stacks before postal custody begins; explicit postal packages may include owned protected/priceless/story/memory/sentimental/soulbound/trade-blocked stacks such as bandages because the sender selects them manually. Accept still blocks content drift before delivery to the recipient.
- Safe Gifting, Shynok sale, Mantok Chest, level barter, item use and remort cleanup respect live postal reservations. Sender remort restores sender-debited postal custody to the remorting sender after the remort inventory rewrite; receiver remort restores it to the original sender and never delivers into the receiver's new life.

## Manual Telegram QA

1. Use two accounts with a completed accepted gift relationship.
2. Open `📰 Дошка корчми`; verify it is a location card with `📰 Вісти`, `🎁 Подарувати манатку` and `📮 Пошта Квестарні`, while `🍻 Шинок` no longer shows gift or postal buttons. From `📰 Вісти`, verify `⬅️ Назад` returns to `📰 Дошка корчми`.
3. Open `📮 Пошта Квестарні` while the recipient is not nearby; verify recipients from accepted gift, duel and explicit Bard reaction history appear without location/online details.
4. Send one eligible manatka type with quantity 1; verify package, fee formula, immediate fee charge and acceptance copy.
5. Send five distinct eligible types, including one quantity above 1 and one quantity 93 if local data can create it.
6. Try a sixth type, quantity 0, quantity 94 and over-owned quantity; verify each is blocked without mutation.
7. Accept as recipient; verify all lines move once from postal custody to the recipient and no extra fee is charged from either player.
8. Cancel, decline and expire a pending package; verify items return to the sender while the already paid delivery fee is not refunded.
9. Replay accept/decline/cancel/old draft buttons.
10. Send a bandage or other trade-blocked/one-use stack explicitly; verify it can be packaged, leaves inventory on confirm and delivers on accept.
11. Try equipped/reserved/drifted stacks and insufficient sender gold at confirmation.
12. Reopen `📮 Пошта Квестарні`; verify recipients, packages in transit and completed history appear without location/online details, with pagination when enough rows exist.
13. Remort the sender of a pending postal package; verify custody returns to that sender once, recipient gets nothing and the sent fee is not refunded.
14. Remort the receiver of a pending postal package; verify custody returns to the original sender and never enters the receiver's new life.
15. Verify nearby Safe Gifting still works and does not list postal-only trade-blocked stacks such as bandages.

## Release-candidate UX polish

- Adventure Choice, starter shawarma and cellar mouse method-choice cards were shortened after playtest feedback: the main card lists method names only, and `💡 Підказка` opens the previous qualitative details without completing the scene. Detail screens italicize explanation/chance lines and show `⬅️ Назад` to return to the compact card. Daily Korchma round scene cards also list their local actions in the compact card and show optional action descriptions behind `💡 Підказка` / `⬅️ Назад`. The cellar mouse start now sends the intro scene separately from the action card. The Korchma hall removes the repeated name/title header and asks with the character name only. Onboarding completion now combines the created-title note with `🍺 Квестарня відчинена.` and suppresses the redundant `Ви вийшли надвір.` movement notice. The front-door arrivals/memorial copy is one compact paragraph, and the Munchkin paragraph/button stays hidden until level 3. The Korchma board is now a location card: `📰 Вісти` opens the existing news list and returns with `⬅️ Назад`, while Safe Gifting and `📮 Пошта Квестарні` live there instead of in the Shynok.
- Active cosmetic titles now use explicit short labels from `src/content/cosmeticTitles.ts` instead of falling back to full achievement names. The catalog covers every current enabled achievement grant id plus disabled placeholders, and validation rejects missing ids, duplicates, overlong labels, sentence punctuation and English reward/power terms.

## Achievement note

Postal delivery completion currently reuses the durable `item_transfers` ledger but does not add separate postal-specific achievement triggers in this slice. The existing gift sent/received achievements remain valid for Safe Gifting; separate first postal sent/received achievements are deferred until achievement recalculation has explicit postal trigger keys rather than overloading gift counters.
