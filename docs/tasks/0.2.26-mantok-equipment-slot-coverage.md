# 0.2.26 - Mantok Equipment Slot Coverage

## Goal

Fill the equipment-slot foundation with real manatky so every canonical slot has enough useful items, existing generated gear lands in the slot implied by its name/tags, and class/race/title identity gear is enforceable rather than flavor-only.

This is a content-stabilization pass after `0.2.23` and `0.2.24`: it should not redesign equipment persistence, combat formulas, shops, item instances or reward settlement.

## Scope

- Add authored equipment coverage manatky for all canonical equipment slots: `weapon`, `offhand`, `head`, `chest`, `legs`, `accessory` and `tool`.
- Keep slot counts reasonably even: at least five coverage items per slot and no authored coverage slot count spread above ten.
- Add at least two class-restricted items for every current class.
- Add at least two race-restricted items for every active race.
- Add at least two title/path-bucket restricted items for every current synthetic title bucket used by Loot Expansion v1.
- Route existing/generated head-like armor names to `head`, leg/boot/pants names to `legs`, shield/buckler names to `offhand`, and bonus-bearing tool-like gear to `tool`.
- Add `twohand` tags to logical bows, spears, staves, crossbows and similar two-handed weapons.
- Add `offhand` tags to logical daggers, bucklers, shields, parrying tools and second-hand items.
- Enforce authored coverage class/race/title gates in the same equip preview/equip flow as generated Loot Expansion gates.
- Keep all new player-facing item names/descriptions in Ukrainian and within the existing humorous Kvestarnia voice.

## Non-goals

- No new equipment slots beyond the seven canonical slots.
- No Prisma migration or stored item-instance schema change.
- No shop, Mantok Chest, Shynok sale, Munchkin barter, postal/gifting or remort rewrite.
- Reward/drop integration is limited to existing monster loot candidates and existing reward settlement paths.
- No broad combat stat formula rebalance.
- No hidden procs or unpreviewed power effects.
- No replacement of the generated Loot Expansion v1 source pack.

## Acceptance criteria

- `src/content/mantokEquipmentCoverage.ts` contributes valid `ItemContent` rows to `items`.
- Every canonical equipment slot has at least five authored coverage manatky.
- Authored coverage slot spread is at most ten between the least-filled and most-filled slot.
- Every current class and active race has at least two restricted authored coverage manatky.
- Every coverage title/path bucket has at least two restricted authored coverage manatky.
- Restricted authored coverage manatky fail equip preview/equip for the wrong class, race or title bucket and pass for matching characters.
- The `common_title` coverage bucket is explicitly common-bucket gear and is not a dead title gate for ordinary current characters.
- Authored coverage manatky are reachable through existing monster loot/reward paths; restricted gear may still drop for mismatched profiles, but equip preview/equip keeps the actual wearing gate intact.
- Existing fight and Yeger/hunt reward paths respect typed loot candidate weights while keeping deterministic replay behavior.
- Generated Loot Expansion gear uses name/tag heuristics for head, legs, offhand shields, tool-like gear, offhand tags and twohand tags.
- Existing `item.loot-v1-*` ids, enhancement ids and generated item counts stay stable.
- Existing starter, consumable, trophy, protected, trade-blocked and one-use behavior stays unchanged.
