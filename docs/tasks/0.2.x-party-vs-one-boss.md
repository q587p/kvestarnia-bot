# 0.2.x - Party Vs One Boss

## Goal

Ship the first production party-combat proof after the temporary party/session foundation: one party, one boss-like enemy, short deterministic rounds and conservative rewards.

This is a bridge between the temporary party foundation and real multi-player raid content such as Senior Barrel Brother.

## Scope

- Reuse the party/session foundation instead of introducing another social grouping model.
- Allow a small party to start one boss encounter through an explicit opt-in flow.
- Keep the encounter to one shared enemy target.
- Resolve party actions through deterministic, testable combat/application logic.
- Support restart-safe session recovery, stale callback handling and exactly-once settlement.
- Show qualitative preparation/risk language before commitment; exact rewards appear only after resolution or in internal docs/tests.
- Preserve existing solo/newbie combat routes and all starter fallback paths.

## Non-goals

- No targetable adds.
- No broad party-vs-many runtime.
- No guild, market, crafting, Mini App or raid scheduler system.
- No Senior Barrel Brother production content in this slice.
- No new reward faucet without explicit balance review.

## Acceptance criteria

- Temporary parties can enter a one-boss encounter without corrupting solo fight state.
- Concurrent callbacks cannot double-spend actions, duplicate rewards or revive closed sessions.
- AFK/stale participants resolve through a predictable conservative fallback.
- Existing one-player combat and shipped duel/social flows keep passing their current tests.
- The UI clearly distinguishes temporary party combat from real raids.

## Focused tests

- party session start/leave/expiry/recovery interactions from the foundation;
- one-boss turn ordering and settlement;
- stale/duplicate callback idempotency;
- solo combat regression;
- reward exactly-once behavior.

## Manual Telegram QA

- Create or join a temporary party.
- Start a one-boss encounter with the minimum supported party size.
- Let one participant act, one participant miss a turn, and one participant use a stale button.
- Finish the encounter and verify rewards/messages are settled once.
- Reopen normal solo combat and starter flows afterward.

## Release surfaces

- `CHANGELOG.md` for exact mechanics and QA notes.
- `news.md` only if this becomes player-visible; keep it qualitative and avoid exact reward/odds spoilers.
- `docs/ai/context.md` if this changes the current playable scope.
- PR body must state that real raids and Senior Barrel Brother remain future work.
