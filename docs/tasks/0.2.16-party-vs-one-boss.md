# 0.2.16 - Party Vs One Boss MVP

## Goal

Ship the first production-proof bridge from temporary party recruiting to real raids: one existing party, one shared boss-like target, short deterministic rounds, durable state, stale callback safety and no production Senior Barrel Brother content.

## Scope

- Reuse `PartySession` / `PartyParticipant` as the recruiting and roster source.
- Start the proof only from a valid recruiting party through a dev/flag-gated control.
- Freeze the roster at start and acquire one `ActiveCombatLease(kind = "party-boss")` per active participant.
- Persist active one-boss state in `party_boss_sessions` and per-turn actions in `party_boss_actions`.
- Allow each active participant to submit one compact action per turn: attack, defend, class skill or race action.
- Resolve all submitted actions immediately when everyone has acted; otherwise dev/manual timeout force-resolves the current turn and fills missing actions as deterministic defend.
- Keep the runtime fight active across turns until the boss reaches 0 HP, all active participants are knocked out or lifecycle cleanup cancels it.
- Terminalize once, store the result, release leases and clear live party keys.

## Non-goals

- No Senior Barrel Brother production route.
- No level-8 Barrel replacement.
- No serious raid rewards, trophies, affinity spotlight, loot table, beer success gate or scheduler.
- No party-boss item actions yet: combat bandages/one-use manatky stay out of this proof and need explicit production raid combat-item rules later.
- No targetable adds, target switching, watcher stacks, marks, enrage or party-vs-many runtime.
- No permanent party, guild, matchmaking, public roster, group-chat requirement, Mini App, market, trade or crafting.
- No broad combat engine rewrite.
- No hidden runtime round cap; the 13-round horizon is only for deterministic simulation and balance probes.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A recruiting temporary party can start one one-boss proof under the dev/flag path.
- Roster, participant resources and boss state persist in database rows.
- Each participant can submit at most one action per turn.
- Duplicate and stale callbacks replay canonical state without double mana, cooldown, RNG or contribution mutation.
- Timeout fallback deterministically fills missing actions with defend, and the dev/manual timeout control does not wait for the normal 23-second deadline.
- Boss HP and terminal state survive restart through `state_json` / `result_json`.
- Turn count keeps incrementing beyond the old five-turn proof cap while the boss and at least one participant remain alive.
- Terminal settlement is exactly-once and releases `party-boss` leases.
- Remort during recruiting or active proof leaves no live membership or combat lease orphan; active proof remort cancels the proof without rewards.
- Existing solo combat, duel, postal, Shynok, Adventure, Daily Korchma, Barrel legacy and party recruiting behavior remain preserved.

## Manual Telegram QA

1. Enable local/dev party helpers (`PARTY_SESSION_DEV_HELPERS_ENABLED=true`) or run non-production.
2. Create a party with two or three accounts through `/dev_party`.
3. Join from another account through the deep link or private invite.
4. From the leader card, tap `🧪 Dev: бос-проба`.
5. Verify the start immediately sends private boss cards to the other active participants without manual refresh.
6. Submit one action from one participant, then submit the last missing action from another account and verify the next turn/result is pushed to the participant who acted earlier.
7. Tap old action buttons and verify canonical replay without extra damage or mana spend.
8. Use `⏱️ Dev: добити хід` to resolve missing actions, then verify `🔎 Оновити` stays usable inside the active party-boss combat lock.
9. Open `📜 Журнал` and verify it shows resolved round damage/actions without private HP/mana or Telegram ids.
10. If the proof reaches a sixth resolved turn while both the boss and at least one participant are alive, verify it remains active instead of losing because of turn count.
11. Finish the encounter and replay result cards.
12. Try remort/cancel/leave edge smoke where the local setup supports it.
13. During the active boss proof, open a bandage from the bag and verify it does not offer unsupported ordinary use or promise a missing combat item button.
14. During a normal solo fight, open a bandage from the bag and verify the combat-use button can replace the player's own stale item-use preview without falsely reporting another action.
15. During a normal solo fight with too little mana for a class/race action, verify the active fight card explains the mana shortfall instead of looking like the cooldown button failed to return.
16. During a normal solo fight, run `/dev_restore_mana` and verify it restores mana instead of being blocked by the combat lock.
17. Verify normal solo fight, duel, postal, Shynok and ordinary Barrel still work afterward.
