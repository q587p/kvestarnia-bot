# 0.2.10 - Active Cosmetic Title Selection MVP

## Goal

Make earned `CharacterCosmeticTitleGrant` records usable without adding power. Players can browse owned cosmetic titles, set one active title, clear it, and see the active cosmetic title on the character card and compact social identity surfaces.

## Scope

- Add the smallest persistence needed for one active cosmetic title per character.
- Add a `🏷️ Титули` route from the character or achievements surface.
- Render earned title grants with active state, title text and source achievement/provenance when available.
- Enforce server-authoritative ownership before selecting a title.
- Keep cosmetic titles separate from generated/current title identity used by gameplay routing.
- Handle duplicate, stale, unknown, disabled or malformed title callbacks safely.
- Show the selected active title on compact social identity rows/cards where names are already shown.
- Freeze active-title display data in duel participant snapshots where duel result history already freezes names.
- Preserve title grants on remort and avoid duplicate active-title state.
- Add a first-title-selection achievement hook only if it stays non-circular and rewardless; otherwise document the deferral.

## Non-goals

- No title abilities, combat buttons or title-based modifiers.
- No XP, gold, item, loot, stat, quest, Yeger, Shynok, remort, duel, raid, economy, supporter or paid advantage effects.
- No party/raid runtime, telemetry engine, title shop, market, bestiary collection or monetization feature.
- No rewrite of generated race/class/path/current-title identity logic unless a clear naming separation is needed.

## Acceptance criteria

- Characters with no title grants see a friendly empty `Титули` state and no broken buttons.
- Characters with title grants can select one active title, see it marked active, return to `/hero`, and clear it.
- Duplicate select/clear callbacks replay canonical state without duplicate rows, notifications, achievements or mutations.
- Selecting a title not owned by the character fails safely without exposing internals.
- Unknown/deleted/disabled title grant ids render safely or are ignored gracefully according to achievement archive conventions.
- Remort preserves title grants and does not create duplicate active-title state; old-life mutating callbacks stale safely if current callback conventions require it.
- `/hero`, nearby/presence rows, nearby duel target cards, duel invite/accept/result/rematch/share cards, gift target selection rows, Korchma arrival rows and duel-winner boards show the active cosmetic title compactly when present.
- Old duel result/share/rematch history keeps the stored title display; old rows without title data fall back to name-only.
- Unknown or disabled active title ids fail closed on public/social surfaces and do not expose raw ids.
- Existing achievement browsing, filters, recalculation and grouped unlock notifications still work.
- Achievement recalculation backfills current identity and identities selected during stored remorts; the very first pre-remort identity remains unrecoverable unless it is still current or a future durable snapshot exists.
- New callback data stays within Telegram limits and player-facing copy stays Ukrainian.

## Manual Telegram QA

1. Open `/hero` on a character with no title grants; verify `Титули` empty state is friendly.
2. Use an existing achievement/title grant path or local dev helper to create at least one title grant.
3. Open `🏷️ Титули`, select a title and return to `/hero`.
4. Replay the old select button; verify canonical replay and no duplicate mutation.
5. Clear the title and replay the clear button.
6. Try selecting a title from another character or stale/unknown payload; verify safe failure.
7. Remort locally and verify grants remain; verify old mutating title callbacks do not corrupt state.
8. Open achievements list, filters and recalculation after title changes.
9. Complete one Daily Korchma Round or open its relevant UI to verify no title-selection regression.
10. Confirm no XP, gold, item, stat, combat, quest, duel, Shynok, Yeger or remort effect changes from selecting a title.
11. Select a title on account A, open `👀 Хто поруч` / `/online` / `/look` from account B in the same location and verify A's title appears compactly.
12. Create, accept, resolve, replay and rematch a duel involving A; verify invite/result/share/rematch cards show the stored title and old result history stays stable after A clears the title.
13. Open gift target selection, the Korchma arrival board and duel-winner board; verify title display appears where documented and ordering/rewards do not change.

## Social display surface decisions

- Shipped title display: `/hero`, `/online`, `/look`, participants/presence rows, nearby duel target list/mode/create/target notification cards, duel invite/accept/result/share/rematch/terminal headers, gift target selection rows, Korchma arrival rows and duel-winner boards.
- Duel result and turn-based session snapshots store optional `activeCosmeticTitle` strings beside existing participant display names; replayed old rows without this field remain name-only.
- Public/social surfaces resolve only enabled catalog titles from `activeCosmeticTitleGrantId`; owner title management keeps archive-safe unknown rows.
- Nearby duel inline keyboard buttons remain name-only to keep Telegram buttons compact.
- Duel HP/vital rows, XP reward lines, gift terminal transaction receipts, Shynok generosity boards/offer notices and Bard performance notices stay name-only in this slice because they are cramped, transactional or already snapshot only names.
- No ranking, target filtering, duel formula, reward, combat, quest, Shynok, Yeger, remort or economy logic reads active cosmetic titles.

## Follow-up closure notes

- Active title state uses nullable `characters.active_cosmetic_title_grant_id`; title grants remain preserved across remort.
- The first-selection achievement ships as rewardless `achievement.journey.cosmetic-title-selected` and grants no cosmetic title to avoid a circular title-for-title loop.
- Title abilities, title combat buttons and title-based selectors remain deferred.
- Race/class achievement backfill now reads stored remort-selected identities where available; missing pre-first-remort identity snapshots are not guessed.
