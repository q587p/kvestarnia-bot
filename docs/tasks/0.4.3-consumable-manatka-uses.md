# 0.4.3 — Consumable Manatka Uses

Status: merged to `main` in PR `#188` (`253fa0df` merge evidence). Target deployment, production availability and manual Telegram QA remain pending; the merge is not evidence for those gates.

## Goal and boundaries

Activate every one of the twenty current `slot: consumable` items through an exhaustive typed catalog. Preserve the existing medical behavior, make the authored cellar items and generated `c001`–`c015` truthful to their fantasy, and keep consumption replay-safe on every shipped item-capable PvE surface.

This release adds no take-away purchase source; after the `0.4.4` bugfix insertion that remains `0.4.10`. Turn-based duels remain item-free. There is no broad `effect_id`/tag inference, food-buff subsystem, hidden proc, new combat-power progression, new party system or migration.

## Effect contract

- Every effect is immediate. There are no prepared effects, charges, expiry rows, cross-turn hidden procs or stackable buffs.
- Out of combat, `item-use-v5` settles passive regeneration, freezes item id/name/fingerprint, exact starting stack quantity, life/remort count, resource before/max/after values and the resolved typed random branch in a preview, then requires confirm. Confirm recalculates only the capped delta from that branch; it never rerolls. Any stack quantity drift stales the old preview. Cancel/expiry does not consume.
- Medical healing and `c001/c003/c009` apply `min(amount, effective maximum - current)`; `c001/c003/c009` are exactly 7/8/9. Field Kit uses the existing 93% floor. A callback that is already inapplicable consumes nothing and commits no combat turn. In a shared Party Boss or GroupCombat round, an item action that becomes inapplicable after an earlier actor is recorded as not used and the round continues, but its inventory unit remains and no item-use evidence, new item cooldown/limit marker or achievement is emitted.
- `restore-both` consumes only if at least one resource changes. Random-resource effects choose only a useful outcome in combat. Out of combat their stable unchanged-stack/life seed freezes one outcome; a full selected resource consumes nothing, and cancel/reopen cannot reroll without a real stack/life change.
- Paired healing requires the owner or a living ally to be injured. It heals the owner and the most injured eligible ally for up to 8 HP each; a lone injured owner receives exactly one 8-HP application, while full HP across every eligible target keeps the item. Party healing requires at least one injured living participant. Cleanse requires a canonical removable harmful status. Cooldown reduction requires at least one positive cooldown after that actor's ordinary committed-action tick; a collection containing only cooldown `1` ticks naturally to zero but keeps `c005`, creates no item-use evidence and, in solo combat, does not advance the turn. Conditional healing requires current HP at or below the threshold.
- Evade and 42% guard affect exactly the first eligible enemy response, in canonical enemy order, caused by the current committed item action. Eligibility requires a positive incremental mechanical delta after ordinary guard, Ward, Lament, Personal Protocol and other earlier protection: `c006` never commits for zero prevented damage, including responses of `0`, `1` or `2` damage. Evade commits only when the response would actually remove positive owner HP or apply a harmful owner-targeted on-hit consequence. A missed attack, an already-neutralized compiled rider or the mere presence of a potential plan component is not enough; when an authored response's established runtime would still apply a harmful rider at zero remaining damage, suppressing that rider is a positive delta. Pure non-damaging hostile actions remain outside evade eligibility. Evade preserves the enemy action/cooldown while suppressing only the selected owner's actual damage/riders; unrelated targets and every later response resolve normally. Solo keeps the original active turn when no eligible response exists, while Party Boss/Big Barrel and GroupCombat continue the shared round, keep the item and create no item-use evidence. The selected response and exact damage/prevention are stored for deterministic restart and journal replay. Critical damage is a fixed typed action, not a chance modifier.
- Party semantics apply in shipped Party Boss/Big Barrel and GroupCombat where the effect is representable. Cleanse is deliberately GroupCombat-only because Party Boss has no canonical removable harmful participant status. Duels expose no item action.
- Only exact ids map to behavior. Source tags, names and generated `effect_id` values remain descriptive inputs and cannot activate effects.

## Commit, reservation, remort and replay

- Inventory preview/confirm keeps the generalized `ItemUseOrder` reservation boundary. Completed orders replay their stored terminal result before current quest eligibility checks and without another decrement or effect; legacy completed HP snapshots, including Field Kit's `heal-hp-to-min-percent`, remain readable. Incompatible pending older rules and exact starting-stack drift stale terminally and release the preview for a clean reopen.
- Solo combat, Party Boss and GroupCombat keep their existing server-owned turn/action CAS. Duplicate callbacks, timeout overlap, retry and restart replay one stored result and cannot repeat consumption/effect. Solo guard/evade item attempts additionally derive their enemy action, hit, damage and canonical response from the immutable authoritative session id, character, monster, turn, item id and full combat snapshot. An unavailable action stores no fake turn, but the unchanged callback cannot obtain a free reroll after a duplicate tap or process restart; ordinary attack/skill randomness is unchanged.
- Every destructive commit rechecks the current-life quest-bottle gate inside its transaction. Party Boss and GroupCombat also recheck every queued item immediately before deterministic round reduction.
- Equipment, live item-use orders, transfers, Shynok sale, Mantok Chest and level barter remain destructive reservations. A committed combat use cancels only the actor's own pending preview for the same item through the existing authoritative path.
- Stacks follow the existing remort selection rule: up to five chosen item ids may carry; the rest reset. Pending/processing ItemUseOrder state is life-bound and cancelled/staled by remort. Immediate combat effects create no state to carry.
- A current-life Пінний Міраж quest bottle cannot appear or commit on any use surface before its quest is completed with `keep`; `turn-in` remains intact. A retained bottle after remort is not permanently locked merely because the prior-life daily-action ledger reset.

## Balance and economy risks

- The three common 7/8/9 heals are intentionally close and low; an item still costs one combat action and allows the response. The 23-point conditional/random outcomes sit on lower-weight tier-2/3 items and do not convert sale value directly into power.
- Paired 8 and party-wide 13 are bounded sustain, not permanent combat power. Their value scales with participant count, so monitor use/win/healing contribution by id, especially `c012` in Big Barrel and 3-person GroupCombat.
- Guaranteed evade can prevent more than a 42% guard against large hits; its scarcity is currently acquisition-limited. The authored cheese has no current production grant, while `c013` remains weight 60. Both evade rows and `c006` are bounded to one response, so enemy count does not multiply prevention. Do not add a purchase shelf here.
- `c008` deals 13 fixed damage and costs the turn; it must not inherit critical chance/multipliers. `c005` can accelerate strong abilities but reduces only cooldowns still positive after the normal action tick by one additional turn.
- Farming risk is inherited from broad Loot Expansion volume, especially multi-enemy GroupCombat. Monitor grants, owned stacks, uses, sales and win-rate delta per exact id before changing weights or adding purchases.
- Five-row GroupCombat pagination keeps every eligible owned stack reachable. Filters must remove inapplicable/full-resource/quest-locked items without silently truncating later rows.
- Reservation and duplicate-use risks are highest at preview-to-combat races, action replacement, timeout resolution and repeated Telegram callbacks; all destructive paths require authoritative ownership/quantity checks and exact-once replay.
