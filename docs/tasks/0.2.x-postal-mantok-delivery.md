# 0.2.x - Postal Manatka Delivery

Draft future task. Do not implement inside `0.2.0` Safe Gifting.

## Goal

Let a player send one eligible manatka unit to another known character without requiring same-location presence, by paying an extra delivery fee and keeping the same consent, audit and replay-safety guarantees as safe gifting.

Player-facing fantasy: Kvestarnia gets a small postal/courier service for gifts that should not depend on both players standing near the same table at the same time.

## Preconditions

- `0.2.0` Safe Gifting has shipped and survived two-account QA.
- Gift reservation, exact item selection guards and terminal replay behavior are stable.
- The design has an anti-spam and privacy review before implementation.

## Scope

- Sender chooses one eligible owned manatka stack unit.
- Sender chooses a known recipient through a privacy-safe selector, not public exact-location discovery.
- Sender previews the item, recipient and delivery fee before confirmation.
- The delivery fee is an explicit gold sink paid in addition to the item transfer.
- Recipient receives an opt-in delivery notice and may accept or decline.
- Accept moves exactly one unit transactionally and records an audit payload.
- Decline, expiry and cancel release the item reservation and replay canonical state.
- Terminal notices are best effort and duplicate-suppressed.

## Non-goals

- No item-for-item trade.
- No gold transfer between players.
- No market, auction, buyback or delivery resale.
- No anonymous spam mailbox.
- No public exact-location disclosure.
- No bypass for equipped, priceless, story, protected, sentimental or reserved items.
- No gift power bonus, referral reward or quest progress.

## Open design decisions

- Recipient discovery: recent interaction, friends, manual character code, or another privacy-safe identity surface.
- Delivery fee formula: fixed fee, item-value percentage, distance-flavored flat fee, or level-bounded fee.
- Fee timing: charged at send confirmation, accepted delivery, or partly refunded on expiry/decline.
- Inbox retention and expiry windows.
- Whether offline recipients can see the delivery from `/mail`, Shynok, `👀 Хто поруч`, or a neutral Kvestarnia surface.

## Acceptance criteria

- Sending by mail cannot create two live reservations for the same sender + `itemId`.
- Sender inventory remains unchanged until recipient acceptance, except for any explicitly committed delivery fee rule.
- Recipient must opt in; no silent item insertion.
- Decline, expiry and cancel do not move the item.
- Duplicate/stale callbacks replay canonical state and never duplicate the item or fee.
- Mail cannot be used to discover a recipient's current location or online status beyond the approved selector.
- Shynok sale, Mantok Chest, level barter, remort cleanup and safe gifting all respect live mail reservations.
