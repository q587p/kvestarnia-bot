# 0.3.1 - Turn-Based Duel Tournaments Rewards MVP

## Goal

Make turn-based duels socially rewarding beyond leaderboards by adding Korchma-funded daily, weekly and monthly tournament rewards.

## Scope

- Count only resolved turn-based duels.
- Exclude quick duels, Rogue retaliation quick duels, training, expired/cancelled/declined/stale sessions.
- Add period standings for Kyiv day/week/month.
- Add replay-safe reward claims per character/period.
- Rewards can include capped gold and manatky.
- Daily rewards are small; weekly rewards stronger; monthly rewards strongest.
- Pair-abuse caps prevent farming one opponent repeatedly.
- Public copy avoids shame for losses.

## Non-goals

- No wagers.
- No item loss.
- No player-funded prize pool.
- No public shame feed.
- No guild system.
- No Mini App.
- No broad duel rebalance unless a small cap is required.

## Acceptance criteria

- Duplicate claims replay stored rewards without paying again.
- Same-pair farming is capped or heavily downweighted per period.
- Rewards are Korchma-funded and bounded.
- Standings and claim cards explain what counted.
- Tournament rewards do not affect Rogue retaliation quick duels.

## Implementation notes

- Implemented as a tournament layer over resolved turn-based duel records; no new duel engine was added.
- Fixed Kyiv daily, weekly and monthly periods use stored claim rows keyed by character, period and period key.
- Repeated wins against the same opponent in a period score `3`, then `1`, then `0`; repeated draws score once.
- Rewards are top-three only and Korchma-funded: daily `42/23/13` gold plus `5/3/1` `Бинт відповідальної паніки`, weekly `93/42/23` gold plus `5/3/1` `Щільний бинт`, and monthly `587/93/42` gold plus `3/2/1` `Польова аптечка`.
- Tournament rewards are manual Korchma prize chests, not Postal Delivery and not auto-grants.
- Unclaimed prize chests remain visible for a bounded lookback: last 13 closed daily periods, 8 closed weekly periods and 5 closed monthly periods.
- Claim rows store reward snapshots, points, rank and result metadata for replay-safe old callbacks; replayed claim callbacks never duplicate gold, items or Latest Events rows.
- Latest Events publishes neutral completed-duel rows for quick and turn-based duels under `⚔️ Бої`, publishes successful tournament claims as `⭐ Важливе`, and never publishes tournament losses.
- Tournament cards keep Gregorian period keys for storage/callbacks but convert visible day/week/month labels to Holocene display dates.
- Turn-based duel starts now send a separate intro message with both frozen participants' levels/titles and the reused italic battle-start tip, while active action cards stay focused on the current turn.
- Active turn-based duel cards now reuse shared combat presenter helpers for compact HP/resource rows, with duel-specific natural action lines.
- Turn-based duel result cards expose a replay-safe combat journal from stored `DuelCombatAction` rows; active duel keyboards keep the journal hidden until combat is over.
- Rematch presses now send the targeted invite directly to the other participant when Telegram delivery is available.
- Follow-up performance hardening keeps the current PR responsive on Render Starter by adding slow hot-path timing logs for inventory/item-card/Mantok Chest/Charkokovalnia callbacks, building inventory list view data once per request, using item-content maps for hot lookups, skipping irrelevant item-detail equip/craft work, and making Mantok Chest auto-pick consume stack quantities without expanding large stacks into one object per unit.
- No new `/dev_*` helper was added because the feature has no new timer/session mutation path to accelerate; QA can use existing duel/dev data setup plus local DB clock/data snapshots for rollover checks.
- Lore Board update not required: this changes Korchma Fighting Corner mechanics and public recognition, not current in-world lore entries.

## Manual Telegram QA

- Complete several turn-based duels across two accounts.
- Open standings.
- Claim daily reward.
- Replay claim.
- Verify quick duel and Rogue retaliation do not count.
- Verify week/month pages and empty states.
- Verify daily/weekly/monthly visible period labels use Holocene years, for example `12026-07`, not `2026-07`.
- Verify pending prize chests show on the tournament card, old closed daily/weekly/monthly rewards inside lookback remain claimable, and claimed periods disappear from the pending block.
- Verify `🥊 Бійцівський куток` shows the tournament pending marker when prize chests are waiting.
- When a turn-based duel starts, verify the intro arrives as a separate message with both participants' levels/titles and an italic `Порада дня`.
- During an active turn-based duel, verify the active card uses compact combat-style HP rows, natural action lines and the 23-second turn hint, and does not show `📜 Журнал бою`.
- Open `📜 Журнал бою` from a turn-based duel result and verify it replays stored round lines without changing duel state.
- Press `🔁 Реванш` from a result and verify the other participant receives an immediate targeted invite card.
- Open `📜 Хроніки Квестарні` with `⚔️ Бої` and verify completed quick and turn-based duel rows appear as neutral duel-completion rows, while `⭐ Важливе` shows successful daily/weekly/monthly tournament reward claims.
- Open `Манатки`, switch filters/sorting/pages, open a junk item detail, open `♻️ До Дружньої Скрині`, run auto/manual selection, and open `✨ Чароковальня`; verify cards and buttons are unchanged while slow-path logs appear only for genuinely slow callbacks.
