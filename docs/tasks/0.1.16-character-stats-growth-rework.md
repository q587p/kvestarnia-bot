# 0.1.16 — Character Stats Growth Rework

## Goal

Make character numbers grow less misleadingly and less single-stat-heavy while preserving the current level budget.

## Scope

- Normalize `race.human-ish` to the active race budget: `+1 STR`, `+1 DEX`, `+1 CHA`.
- Add fixed derived hidden-path stat bonuses in the shared effective-stats layer:
  - `sun`: `+1 STR`, `+1 DEX`;
  - `moon`: `+1 INT`, `+1 DEX`;
  - `boundary`: `+1 LUCK`, `+1 CHA`.
- Keep `statsJson` unchanged; no migration or backfill.
- Replace primary-stat-only level growth with deterministic distributed growth:
  - total level stat budget remains `max(0, level - 1)`;
  - HP remains `+4` per gained level;
  - mana remains `+2` per gained level;
  - class profile, race bonus and hidden-path bonus bias allocation without adding extra level points.
- Change `/hero` from cumulative `Ріст:` wording to next-level forecast copy.
- Refresh `/help` roadmap copy so it no longer says loot or combat bookkeeping are still pending.
- Keep safe side surfaces, especially `/inventory` and item/equipment callbacks, available while an active fight is in progress.
- Preserve remort memory as 23% of previous distributed level growth per stat.
- Fix `Низ` passage reward scaling so XP, gold and broad loot profile power follow the effective monster level chosen by the passage difficulty.
- Add local `/dev_raid_stop` for manual Barrel raid QA without weakening production timers.

## Non-goals

- No XP curve, broad loot-table expansion, schema or migration changes.
- No exact race/path mechanical preview in onboarding or remort.
- No player-facing `sun`, `moon` or `boundary` labels.
- No broad balance retune; simulation follow-up can happen separately.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `Людисько` new/restarted/remorted characters use exactly `+1 STR`, `+1 DEX`, `+1 CHA`.
- Existing stored characters inherit hidden-path bonuses through effective stats without modifying `statsJson`.
- `/hero`, combat summaries and recovery paths read the same path-adjusted effective stats.
- Level 1 has 0 level stat points; level 2 has 1; level 12 has 11; level 13 has 12.
- Cumulative allocation is deterministic, monotonic and distributes one stat point per gained level.
- `/hero` shows `Зміна:` when a next level exists and does not show a fake next-level delta at cap.
- Remort memory preserves HP, mana and distributed stat memory without preserving old fixed path/race/class identity bonuses.
- Right/straight/left `Низ` passage rewards differ by the effective monster level: easier fights pay less, harder fights pay more, and repeated reward replay preserves the original values.
