# 0.3.4 - Quest Overview Route

## Goal

Turn the persistent `🗺️ Квести` button and `/quest` into a compact quest overview/journal route that works from safe non-combat states, plus ship the first tiny route quest that teaches players how to reach `📋 Стіл зі справами`.

## Scope

- Render a mostly read-only `🗺️ Квести` overview from the current quest snapshot.
- Show compact rows only for active/taken/current-period progress or claimable quest work.
- Hide retired starter rows, locked future quests, generic available untaken quests, completed/reward-claimed rows, and informational unlock hints, except explicit overview handoffs named below.
- After `Перший крок до столу` is completed, show the two starter follow-up rows in the same overview format: `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка`.
- After both `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка` are completed, show the next starter follow-up row in the same overview format: `Льохова справа`.
- When regular `Три справи` choice is ready, show `🪧 Три справи на найближчий час — три проблеми чекають вибору` in the overview with the same `Зроблено` / `Далі` / `Де` guidance.
- When the level 4+ cellar follow-up is relevant, show `Справа не до миші` in the overview for offered, roleplay-cooldown and bottle-turn-in stages.
- When the Charkokovalnia unlock asks for a `Польова аптечка`, show `Доступ до Чароковальні` in the overview with field-kit guidance for missing and already-owned kit states.
- `Льохова справа` method screens should show only 4 deterministic character-varied method buttons instead of the full 7-method spread.
- Italicize the repeated overview guidance labels `Зроблено:`, `Далі:`, and `Де:` so rows scan cleanly on Telegram.
- Each visible row should carry progress, what is already done when available, next-step guidance, and a location/turn-in hint in text.
- Show a compact empty state when no active/taken/claimable rows exist.
- Make `/quest` and the persistent `🗺️ Квести` button available outside Korchma for the overview, while keeping the physical `Стіл зі справами`, `v1:quest:list`, and Quest Hub archive behavior at the table.
- Keep the full Quest Hub table-gated: direct list/action callbacks outside Korchma still route through the existing `Квести видають усередині.` gate.
- Add the first current-life route quest, `Перший крок до столу`: enter Korchma, reach `Стіл зі справами`, then receive a replay-safe +1 XP completion and rewardless achievement.
- The first route quest completion service must fail closed until current normalized presence is `Стіл зі справами`; outside, hall, overview and stale non-table routes stay quiet and non-mutating unless the completion row already exists.
- Its completion message should tell the player that two more starter papers are now on the table, without revealing future rewards.
- Keep overview buttons navigation-only and minimal: only `📋 До Столу зі справами`. The table button may move through the normal place route and complete the first route quest when the table is reached; do not add per-quest direct route buttons from overview rows, and do not duplicate the main keyboard's always-available quest or location buttons.
- Preserve the existing Shynok beer-round mechanics, but ensure a successful round confirmation shows the shared `Корчмар поставив кухлі` result before any individual recipient `Випити` offer card is sent.
- While a Shynok round offer is still live for the current player, show a `🍺 Вам пиво!` shortcut next to `🍹 Напої для себе` on Shynok cards; opening it should replay the offer card with `Випити` / `Ні, дякую` without accepting or declining.
- In the Korchma hall, let a ready `📋 Стіл зі справами ✅` turn-in marker override other available-work `⚠️` markers, so completed table work is not hidden behind takeable papers.
- If the Charkokovalnia unlock is pending and the player already has a `Польова аптечка`, show green `✅` markers on the handoff route (`Доступ до Чароковальні`, `Надвір`, `У задвірок`, and `Чароковальня`) instead of the generic available `⚠️`.
- For level 4+ characters, completed quest turn-ins can grant a replay-safe `Іскрокамінь` bonus: 1 at 23%, 2 at 13%, or 3 at 5%. Cover current quest-like reward paths: Adventure choices, Daily Korchma Round final turn-in, Yeger boards, Barrel tutorial turn-in, problem quest turn-ins, Hunt Board contracts, cellar mouse errands, grownup cellar completion, and the Charkokovalnia field-kit unlock. Do not change ordinary combat reward rolls.
- Quest reward presenters that claim to show item grants must render fresh `Іскрокамінь` bonus grants from the just-created claim response and replayed bonus grants from stored `appliedItemGrants`, without rerolling or duplicating items.
- If `Льохова справа` becomes ready again after the mouse errand was already completed once, the overview should call it `не перший спуск` and tell the player to try the cellar again.
- When `Справа не до миші` is on its roleplay pause or paid-seal branch, keep its keyboard focused on the seal and hall return; do not show the old `Дошка полювання` shortcut from the era before the broader quest table existed.
- In quest overview prose, use `спуск до Низу` with lowercase `спуск` when it is not a button, title, or sentence start.
- Public level-up rows in `📜 Хроніки Квестарні` should omit the remort-life tag for base-life characters (`р0`) while keeping positive remort tags.
- `📜 Хроніки Квестарні` filter buttons should mark the currently selected feed with a neutral selected-state icon, not `✅`, so filters do not look like completed quest rows.
- `📜 Хроніки Квестарні` combat feed should record valid underdog solo-combat wins from both fresh persistent fight reward claims and later reward replay/recovery paths.

## Non-goals

- No broad quest engine, journal archive, schema, migrations, public feed rows, combat rebalance, Mini App, or Yeger/Adventure/Daily reward changes.
- No new `/dev_*` helper: the slice adds no timer, cooldown, random offer, pending session, retry, once-per-period gate, or wait rule.
- No production flag: the slice changes only safe navigation/overview surfaces and a one-time current-life tutorial quest.

## Acceptance criteria

- `🗺️ Квести` and `/quest` show a useful active/taken/claimable overview from safe states inside or outside Korchma and do not stamp quest-table presence by themselves.
- Locked, retired, completed/reward-claimed and generic available untaken quest rows stay out of the overview, except the explicit `Три справи на найближчий час`, `Справа не до миші`, and `Доступ до Чароковальні` handoffs.
- After the first route quest is completed, the overview shows `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка` with `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` guidance; they disappear again after their normal completed/retired states.
- After `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка` are completed and the cellar mouse quest is ready, the overview shows `Льохова справа` with `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` guidance.
- When the regular Adventure Choice offer is ready, the overview shows `Три справи на найближчий час` with stage guidance and text-only table direction.
- When `Справа не до миші` is offered, paused after a roleplay attempt, or ready to turn in with a bottle, the overview shows that current stage instead of hiding the adult cellar follow-up until it is already claimed.
- When `Доступ до Чароковальні` is pending, the overview says the elf-mage asked for a `Польова аптечка`, points players without one toward the Yeger hint, and points players with one back to Charkokovalnia.
- `Льохова справа` and its method-help card show 4 visible method buttons for the current character, not all 7 possible cellar methods.
- Overview `Зроблено:`, `Далі:`, and `Де:` labels render in Telegram HTML italics without italicizing the whole row.
- Empty overview states explain that new papers come from `Стіл зі справами`.
- No-character, active combat, turn-based duel, pending/active Big Barrel, active passage-search and stale callback blockers keep existing behavior.
- The first route quest appears as 0/2 before entering Korchma, 1/2 after entering, completes only when the character reaches `Стіл зі справами`, grants +1 XP once per current-life token, and resets after remort.
- Direct completion attempts away from `Стіл зі справами` return not-ready and do not write `quest.first-korchma.completed`, grant XP, or unlock the achievement.
- Completing the first route quest unlocks `achievement.quest.first-korchma` once without gold, items, title power or combat power.
- Overview buttons are navigation-only; per-quest locations are mentioned in text rather than offered as direct route buttons.
- Quest Table and archive callbacks remain compatible.
- Overview text is Ukrainian, compact, HTML-escaped and under Telegram callback-size limits.
- A completed Shynok beer-round confirmation edits the buyer's card before sending individual recipient offer messages, so the recipient sees the round as placed before acting on a cup.
- Live Shynok round offers remain reachable from the Shynok card through `🍺 Вам пиво!`, and that shortcut only opens the current offer card; drinking or declining still requires the explicit offer buttons.
- When the Barrel Beer Tutorial is ready to turn in and other available work still marks Shynok or the table, the Korchma hall shows `📋 Стіл зі справами ✅` rather than downgrading it to `⚠️`.
- When the Charkokovalnia unlock is pending and the field kit is already in the bag, the route markers show `✅` on `Доступ до Чароковальні`, `Надвір`, `У задвірок`, and `Чароковальня`; without the field kit they remain `⚠️`.
- Level 4+ quest turn-ins persist any bonus `Іскрокамінь` in the claim/result ledger, show it in the same item reward block as other manatky, and do not reroll or duplicate it on replay/stale callbacks.
- Daily Korchma Round fresh and replayed reward cards show stored quest `Іскрокамінь` grants consistently when that bonus was applied.
- Repeat-ready `Льохова справа` rows no longer say `перший спуск`; they use repeat-attempt wording when the cellar mouse errand has already been completed at least once in the current low-level loop.
- `Справа не до миші` roleplay pause / paid-seal keyboards no longer offer `Дошка полювання`; players can still use the hall and ordinary quest navigation.
- Problem-quest overview `Де:` guidance uses `спуск до Низу` in prose instead of title-casing it mid-sentence.
- First-life level-up rows in `📜 Хроніки Квестарні` no longer render `(р0)`; remorted lives still render positive tags such as `(р5)`.
- The selected `📜 Хроніки Квестарні` filter renders with `🔘`, including `Манатки`, and no selected filter uses `✅`.
- `⚔️ Бої` includes valid `combat.underdog_won` rows after fresh wins and after already-settled persistent fight reward replay/recovery, without duplicating rows on repeated views.

## Manual Telegram QA

1. New character outside Korchma: open `🗺️ Квести` or `/quest`; verify the overview opens instead of `Квести видають усередині.`, shows `Перший крок до столу` as 0/2, and does not mark table presence.
2. Press `🚪 Зайти в корчму` or route to the hall, then reopen `🗺️ Квести`; verify `Перший крок до столу` is 1/2, points to `Стіл зі справами`, and does not complete in the hall.
3. Press `📋 До Столу зі справами`; verify normal place movement reaches the table, the first route quest completes once with +1 XP, and a duplicate refresh/table open does not pay again.
4. After remort/current-life reset, repeat the route and verify the first route quest can appear again, stays incomplete until the new life reaches the table, and then completes once.
5. After `Перший крок до столу` completes on a level 1 character, open `🗺️ Квести`; verify `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка` appear with italic `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` labels, and the only inline button is still `📋 До Столу зі справами`.
6. Complete `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка`, then open `🗺️ Квести`; verify `Льохова справа` appears with italic `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` labels.
7. Open `Льохова справа` and its `💡 Підказка`; verify only 4 method buttons are shown for the current character, not the full 7-method spread.
8. Complete `Льохова справа`, wait/reset past its cooldown, then open `🗺️ Квести`; verify the row says `не перший спуск` and tells the player to go to the cellar again.
9. High-level character with regular `Три справи` ready: open `🗺️ Квести`; verify `🪧 Три справи на найближчий час` appears with `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` guidance.
10. Level 4+ character with `Справа не до миші` offered or paused after a roleplay attempt: open `🗺️ Квести`; verify the row appears and names the current stage.
10a. On the paused/adult cellar seal card, verify the keyboard shows `Купити пломбу` and `До зали`, but no `Дошка полювання` shortcut.
11. Level 4+ character holding the grownup cellar bottle: open `🗺️ Квести`; verify `Справа не до миші` appears as claimable and points to Shynok turn-in.
12. High-level character with Charkokovalnia unlock pending and no field kit: open `🗺️ Квести`; verify `Доступ до Чароковальні` says the elf-mage asked for `Польова аптечка` and points to the Yeger hint.
