# 0.3.4 - Quest Overview Route

## Goal

Turn the persistent `🗺️ Квести` button and `/quest` into a compact quest overview/journal route that works from safe non-combat states, plus ship the first tiny route quest that teaches players how to reach `📋 Стіл зі справами`.

## Scope

- Render a mostly read-only `🗺️ Квести` overview from the current quest snapshot.
- Show compact rows only for active/taken/current-period progress or claimable quest work.
- Hide retired starter rows, locked future quests, generic available untaken quests, completed/reward-claimed rows, and informational unlock hints.
- After `Перший крок до столу` is completed, show the two starter follow-up rows in the same overview format: `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка`.
- After both `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка` are completed, show the next starter follow-up row in the same overview format: `Льохова справа`.
- `Льохова справа` method screens should show only 4 deterministic character-varied method buttons instead of the full 7-method spread.
- Italicize the repeated overview guidance labels `Зроблено:`, `Далі:`, and `Де:` so rows scan cleanly on Telegram.
- Each visible row should carry progress, what is already done when available, next-step guidance, and a location/turn-in hint in text.
- Show a compact empty state when no active/taken/claimable rows exist.
- Make `/quest` and the persistent `🗺️ Квести` button available outside Korchma for the overview, while keeping the physical `Стіл зі справами`, `v1:quest:list`, and Quest Hub archive behavior at the table.
- Keep the full Quest Hub table-gated: direct list/action callbacks outside Korchma still route through the existing `Квести видають усередині.` gate.
- Add the first current-life route quest, `Перший крок до столу`: enter Korchma, reach `Стіл зі справами`, then receive a replay-safe +1 XP completion and rewardless achievement.
- Its completion message should tell the player that two more starter papers are now on the table, without revealing future rewards.
- Keep overview buttons navigation-only and minimal: only `📋 До Столу зі справами`. The table button may move through the normal place route and complete the first route quest when the table is reached; do not add per-quest direct route buttons from overview rows, and do not duplicate the main keyboard's always-available quest or location buttons.
- Preserve the existing Shynok beer-round mechanics, but ensure a successful round confirmation shows the shared `Корчмар поставив кухлі` result before any individual recipient `Випити` offer card is sent.
- While a Shynok round offer is still live for the current player, show a `🍺 Вам пиво!` shortcut next to `🍹 Напої для себе` on Shynok cards; opening it should replay the offer card with `Випити` / `Ні, дякую` without accepting or declining.

## Non-goals

- No broad quest engine, journal archive, schema, migrations, public feed rows, combat rebalance, Mini App, or Yeger/Adventure/Daily reward changes.
- No new `/dev_*` helper: the slice adds no timer, cooldown, random offer, pending session, retry, once-per-period gate, or wait rule.
- No production flag: the slice changes only safe navigation/overview surfaces and a one-time current-life tutorial quest.

## Acceptance criteria

- `🗺️ Квести` and `/quest` show a useful active/taken/claimable overview from safe states inside or outside Korchma and do not stamp quest-table presence by themselves.
- Locked, retired, completed/reward-claimed and generic available untaken quest rows stay out of the overview.
- After the first route quest is completed, the overview shows `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка` with `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` guidance; they disappear again after their normal completed/retired states.
- After `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка` are completed and the cellar mouse quest is ready, the overview shows `Льохова справа` with `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` guidance.
- `Льохова справа` and its method-help card show 4 visible method buttons for the current character, not all 7 possible cellar methods.
- Overview `Зроблено:`, `Далі:`, and `Де:` labels render in Telegram HTML italics without italicizing the whole row.
- Empty overview states explain that new papers come from `Стіл зі справами`.
- No-character, active combat, turn-based duel, pending/active Big Barrel, active passage-search and stale callback blockers keep existing behavior.
- The first route quest appears as 0/2 before entering Korchma, 1/2 after entering, completes only when the character reaches `Стіл зі справами`, grants +1 XP once per current-life token, and resets after remort.
- Completing the first route quest unlocks `achievement.quest.first-korchma` once without gold, items, title power or combat power.
- Overview buttons are navigation-only; per-quest locations are mentioned in text rather than offered as direct route buttons.
- Quest Table and archive callbacks remain compatible.
- Overview text is Ukrainian, compact, HTML-escaped and under Telegram callback-size limits.
- A completed Shynok beer-round confirmation edits the buyer's card before sending individual recipient offer messages, so the recipient sees the round as placed before acting on a cup.
- Live Shynok round offers remain reachable from the Shynok card through `🍺 Вам пиво!`, and that shortcut only opens the current offer card; drinking or declining still requires the explicit offer buttons.

## Manual Telegram QA

1. New character outside Korchma: open `🗺️ Квести` or `/quest`; verify the overview opens instead of `Квести видають усередині.`, shows `Перший крок до столу` as 0/2, and does not mark table presence.
2. Press `🚪 Зайти в корчму` or route to the hall, then reopen `🗺️ Квести`; verify `Перший крок до столу` is 1/2 and points to `Стіл зі справами`.
3. Press `📋 До Столу зі справами`; verify normal place movement reaches the table, the first route quest completes once with +1 XP, and a duplicate refresh/table open does not pay again.
4. After remort/current-life reset, repeat the route and verify the first route quest can appear and complete again for the new life.
5. After `Перший крок до столу` completes on a level 1 character, open `🗺️ Квести`; verify `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка` appear with italic `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` labels, and the only inline button is still `📋 До Столу зі справами`.
6. Complete `Підозріла шаурма` and `Новачкова сутичка`, then open `🗺️ Квести`; verify `Льохова справа` appears with italic `Зроблено`, `Далі`, and `Де` labels.
7. Open `Льохова справа` and its `💡 Підказка`; verify only 4 method buttons are shown for the current character, not the full 7-method spread.
8. High-level character with completed starter quests: open `🗺️ Квести`; verify starter shawarma/fight rows are absent.
9. Character with no other active/taken quests after starter follow-ups are completed/retired: verify the compact empty state points to `Стіл зі справами`.
10. Active Daily Korchma Round 1/2: verify the row appears with done step, next step and location/turn-in text, without a direct `До обходу` button.
11. Daily Korchma Round 2/2 turn-in-ready: verify the row appears as claimable and explains where to turn in.
12. Problem Quest in progress: verify the row appears with progress and turn-in guidance.
13. Problem Quest completed and reward claimed: verify the row disappears.
14. Yeger not started: verify no row.
15. Yeger in progress / claimable: verify the row appears with progress or turn-in guidance and no direct `До Єгеря` button.
16. Barrel Beer Tutorial completed, retired or merely available: verify no row; in progress / turn-in-ready still appears.
17. From the full Quest Hub, open the available Barrel Beer Tutorial paper; verify it shows a preview/confirmation card, does not grant the stipend or mark the quest accepted yet, and only `🛢️ Взяти записку` grants the accepted result with the 39-gold received line.
18. Buy a Shynok beer round for a nearby recipient; verify the buyer's card first changes to `Корчмар поставив кухлі`, then the recipient receives the separate beer offer with `Випити`.
19. Before accepting or declining that live offer, reopen Shynok; verify `🍺 Вам пиво!` appears next to `🍹 Напої для себе`, opens the same offer card, and does not drink or decline until an explicit offer button is pressed.
20. Verify the overview keyboard contains only `📋 До Столу зі справами`; it must not duplicate the main keyboard's `Квести`, refresh, or `До зали` routes.
21. Verify opening overview does not complete, claim, accept, start fights, start raids, move presence to `Стіл зі справами`, or expose per-quest route buttons such as `До Низу`, `До Єгеря`, `До бочки`, `До шинку`, `До льоху`, `До задвірка`, `До Корчмаря`, `До обходу`, or `До Трьох справ`.
22. Verify direct full Quest Hub list/action callbacks outside Korchma still use the existing outside gate.
23. Active solo combat, active turn-based duel, active/pending Big Barrel and active passage search: verify existing blockers win over overview.
24. Stale `v1:quest:overview` callback: verify it fails closed or routes to the current canonical overview without mutation.
