# 0.3.13 — Location-Safe Quest and Combat Bugfixes

## Goal

Ship a patch-first release that preserves world location across Bard and quest-combat flows, makes every valid duel completion advance the Fighting Corner quest exactly once, and repairs the related encounter scaling and Telegram quest-guidance defects.

## Scope

- Keep Bard Performance at its starting location while retaining Shynok-only house behavior for performances already in Shynok, and make the live card state that its next-performance wait applies to the current location.
- Carry normalized `User.lastSeenLocationId` through the Prisma daily-action claim into the Adventure result, then store and reuse it through combat start, redraw and terminal navigation. One shared Adventure callback presence policy treats `active-fight`, `combat-blocked` and `already-completed` as non-authoritative for `problem`, `problem-help` and final `approach`, so none can replace a durable combat location.
- Scale Adventure quest monsters to the current character level, then apply the canonical remort-aware combat derivation once in durable battle state.
- Count Quick Duel resolution at or after the persisted quest-acceptance timestamp while preserving shared replay-safe duel progress. Prisma coverage verifies the exact persisted equality boundary; the cause of a normal human-flow miss remains subject to Telegram QA.
- Render Doppelganger training comments as escaped HTML block quotes.
- State the full barrel-beer requirement consistently in the tutorial, clue and quest overview.
- Make `🗺️ Квести` show all three Fighting Corner objectives and all three current Korchma-round locations, crossing out each completed objective or scene while leaving unfinished entries visible.
- Emphasize the Fighting Corner rule card semantically: italicize the two quoted formulations and the nearby-challenge route, and bold the second- and third-rule labels.
- Make the first incoming-duel card offer `Details` or a real decline instead of a misleading first `Accept`; keep the sole player-facing acceptance action on the existing detailed confirmation card. Keep the author's status card role-correct with only cancel and refresh actions, redraw stale self-opened `Details` callbacks as that inert owner card, and preserve both players' current presence until a fresh final acceptance actually resolves or starts the duel.
- When final acceptance starts a turn duel, keep the visible Telegram order stable: an actual location transition first, then the separate announcement/advice card, then a newly sent combat card. Do not turn the old confirmation message into a combat card above the newer messages.
- Route `/duel` and every `v1:duel:*` callback through a neutral presence heartbeat that updates user activity only. Pending creation, rematches, details, warnings, gates, missing/expired/replayed results, refresh and decline preserve location, raid and adventure; only a fresh final `active` or `resolved` transition may apply the authoritative Fighting Corner payload.
- Keep exactly one canonical private turn-duel card per participant. Fresh sessions begin with null message references; accepted delivery records the new final card, stale owner refresh and repeated private deep links reuse it, and an atomic compare-and-set records any necessary fallback while disarming concurrent losing cards. Group deep links remain spectator-safe.
- Keep completed reward cards readable through shared presenters: XP/gold stay in the amount block, then a blank line separates every actual item grant under `Здобуто`; cover quests, the Barrel tutorial, fights, hunts, raids and tournament claims while preserving their contextual headings. The Fighting Corner soap reaction remains a separate paragraph before the amount.
- Let every current Nyz passage-search item slot rarely yield `Іскрокамінь` by reusing the bounded ordinary-fight bandage-slot replacement policy; a single search item slot grants either the bandage or Iskrokamin, never both, and remains deterministic on replay.

## Non-goals

- No new gameplay action, independent loot roll, monster family, schema, migration, dependency, rollout flag or broad combat refactor; only the existing passage-search bandage item slot gains the bounded Iskrokamin replacement already used by ordinary fights.
- No change to ordinary Niz fight location or non-quest monster selection.
- No new achievement definition, lore entry or `/dev_*` helper. Passage Search uses the standard reward achievement path, while the existing `/dev_reset_passage_search iskrokamin` fixture makes its next natural completed search deterministic for non-production QA without changing production loot.

## Acceptance criteria

- Bard start and response callbacks never assign Shynok when the player began elsewhere, and live cards describe the unchanged per-location cooldown as applying to `this location`.
- Quick and turn-based duel completions use the existing normalized quest-progress handler and remain exact-once on replay.
- Quest fights preserve front, yard and Quest Table origins through start, redraw, victory and flee navigation; starting the battle at any of those origins does not append a false `descended to the Korchma Cellars` movement notice after the combat card.
- Concurrent Adventure completion callbacks leave the winning Front or Yard fight presence authoritative when the loser reports `already-completed`, including when it discovers the committed claim during preflight before the winner starts the fight.
- Delayed or historical `already-completed` Adventure problem, help and approach callbacks preserve current presence; explicit quest navigation, ordinary selected problem/help navigation and newly completed non-fight Adventure results retain their existing Quest Table movement.
- `active-fight` and non-solo `combat-blocked` Adventure problem, help and approach callbacks perform no presence write.
- Adventure monsters use the current level; remort pressure is frozen once, does not stack, and its notice appears once at battle opening only when applicable.
- Doppelganger payloads contain a valid escaped `<blockquote>` under HTML parse mode.
- Before the Barrel round is offered, active instructions say to serve/buy beer for everyone and drink the player's own beer.
- After the Barrel round is offered but the player's mug remains undrunk, active instructions ask only to drink that mug.
- Fighting Corner progress crosses out completed training, Quick Duel and turn-duel objectives without hiding the remaining objectives; the claimable state shows all three crossed out.
- The Fighting Corner rule card renders both first-rule quotes and the `Хто поруч → Кинути виклик присутнім` route in italics, while `Друге правило:` and `Третє правило:` are bold labels under HTML parse mode.
- An active `Корчмарський обхід` states the two-of-three contract, lists the three actual offered locations, and crosses out only locations whose scenes are complete; turn-in guidance preserves that checklist and names the Quest Table separately.
- Initial open and targeted cards shown to invite recipients use `📖 Детальніше`, `🙅 Відмовитись` and `🔄 Оновити`; the author's own status card uses only `🧹 Скасувати виклик` and `🔄 Оновити`. Opening details does not accept the duel, only the detailed confirmation card offers `🤝 Так, прийняти`, and a stale self-opened Details callback redraws the owner card without changing duel or presence state.
- `/duel`, new open/nearby challenge creation, pending rematch creation, confirmation, refresh, resource warning, level gate, pair limit, busy, not-found, expired, replayed result and decline write only the neutral user heartbeat, never location, raid or adventure fields. Explicit Fighting Corner navigation still moves normally. A newly started turn duel clears the old confirmation keyboard and sends the transition (when needed), intro/advice and combat card in that exact order. Duplicate/concurrent final accepts do not edit the old confirmation into combat, do not overwrite the recorded participant reference and only refresh the already-recorded canonical card when one exists.
- Fresh and replayed Fighting Corner claims render `intro → soap reaction → Отримано → Здобуто`, with blank lines between those semantic blocks and no duplicate item rows. Other completed quest rewards, the Barrel tutorial, fights, hunts, raids and a newly claimed tournament prize use the same `amount → blank line → Здобуто` item-grant policy; previews and purchases do not pretend that an item was already granted.
- Safe and risky Nyz searches can persist one `Іскрокамінь` instead of an already-won bandage slot. LUCK cannot raise the shared replacement chance above its existing cap, repeated checks replay the stored item without another roll or grant, and the completed search card separates gold from `Здобуто: Іскрокамінь` with one blank line.
- Only the repository-winning Passage Search resolution emits `item.received`. Callback and scheduler completion paths deliver returned unlock notifications; stored replay and concurrent losers return no unlocks and cannot increment first-owned or cumulative item progress. Ordinary multi-bandage grants still expand quantity through the standard cumulative tracking contract.
- `/dev_reset_passage_search iskrokamin` clears the existing search fixture and makes the next completed natural search produce Iskrokamin in non-production only; it adds no production flag, command or loot mechanic.
