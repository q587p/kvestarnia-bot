## Goal

Add a private, restart-safe chat to Big Barrel Brother. Joined participants see the latest 13 player/system entries in recruiting, keep the same conversation during the active boss fight, and submit one short message through a precisely bound ForceReply without interfering with ordinary bot routing.

## Scope

- One feed per `PartySession → PartyBossSession` lineage.
- Authorized recruiting-card excerpt and one compact active chat card per participant.
- `/online` labels recruiting Big Barrel parties as an active gathering phase with canonical joined/capacity counts and does not contradict that state with an inactive-raid notice.
- Every accepted player post best-effort pushes a separate escaped HTML notification to every other current same-life raid participant; the durable shared feed remains canonical.
- `💬 Написати в рейд-чат`, exact durable ForceReply composer, fresh visible send confirmation and `/cancel_raid_chat`.
- Dedicated append-only entries, rate CAS state, separate chat revision, per-participant durable delivery/redaction dirty state and a safe Prisma migration.
- At most 93 grapheme clusters of normalized one-line plaintext; HTML escape only at render.
- Typed allowlisted system events for membership, leadership, preparation, start, high-signal resolved raid abilities and terminal state.
- Last-13 Kyiv-time rendering with bold player names and italic technical events, Telegram length guard, process-wide raid-chat delivery gate, `429 retry_after` persistence and restart recovery.
- Participant-only read/write lifecycle, 13-day terminal read-only retention, best-effort durable redaction after access loss.
- Existing Big Barrel production gate only, non-production timer/cap helper, tests and release/docs updates.

## Non-goals

- Global/guild/public chat, Telegram group bridging, matchmaking or trading.
- A durable per-entry push-delivery ledger or retry queue.
- Edit/delete, replies/threads, reactions, attachments, links, reporting/moderation UI, mute or old-history pagination.
- Ordinary combat-journal duplication.
- Rewards, XP, gold, loot, contribution or achievements for chat activity.
- Big Barrel balance, eligibility, timing, settlement or existing ability-achievement changes.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Two joined players exchange short posts and see one shared newest-13 feed, oldest→newest, with frozen character names and `Europe/Kyiv` timestamps. Every accepted player post also reaches every other current participant immediately as a separate escaped blockquote notification; a recipient failure cannot roll back the stored post. Player names are bold while technical system-event sentences are italic. `/online` identifies the gathering as recruiting and shows the canonical joined/capacity count such as `2/8`.
2. Invite viewers, outsiders, former/removed participants and old remort lives receive no fresh authorized read/write. Access loss durably schedules old-card redaction; Telegram's unavoidable already-seen/permanent-edit limitation is handled and documented exactly as the design states.
3. The same feed continues into active combat through persistent compact chat cards, survives restart, remains readable and writable for knocked-out final-roster participants while the boss fight is active, and becomes final-roster read-only for 13 days before redaction/cleanup.
4. Exact bot-authored prompt binding survives restart and consumes one valid reply once. Acceptance sends a fresh visible confirmation even if the old prompt cannot be edited. Commands/menu text/other prompts/non-replies/media continue to existing handlers. Invalid or rate-limited input can be corrected through a newly bound prompt.
5. Input normalization preserves valid composed emoji, blocks defined controls/bidi/link entities, counts 93 graphemes correctly and stores no HTML. Full cards stay within Telegram's 4096 limit; a pruned view says `останні N із 13`.
6. Player entries, composer consumption, 3-second author cooldown, fixed 42-per-93-second lineage window, duplicate-body policy and chat revision are transactionally/CAS correct under retries and concurrency. Hard storage cap is 130.
7. Join/leave/leader/preparation/start/allowlisted resolved-ability/terminal events appear exactly once and only after the winning canonical transition. Replaceable queued choices, readiness, failures, ordinary actions and journal details produce none.
8. Chat-only writes never bump gameplay session/boss versions or alter raid state, resources, rewards, contribution, achievements or action resolution.
9. Per-participant desired/rendered/redaction state is durable. A bounded startup worker resumes post-commit fanout after restart, repairs every participant reference, coalesces bursts, respects the cross-session gate and persists Telegram retry timing.
10. While the Big Barrel surface is enabled, the bounded system-event insert is intentionally part of the gameplay transaction; payload validation is pure, duplicate upsert is safe, and there is no Telegram I/O inside that transaction.
11. Raid chat follows the existing `BIG_BARREL_BROTHER_RAID_ENABLED` gate and adds no separate environment/config key. The dev helper cannot register, appear or mutate in production.
12. Migration, focused suites, Big Barrel regressions and `npm run check` pass. Manual Telegram QA is recorded as performed or pending, never inferred.
13. Version/docs/news/context are current; the old draft is archived; no-achievement and expected no-lore-change decisions are explicit.

Implementation record:

- Preflight verified merged PR #176 content on `origin/main` and confirmed `0.3.15` was unallocated before branching.
- The separate chat revision, exact composer, fixed author/lineage rate state, bounded typed events and per-participant dirty/redaction state are persisted under the root `PartySession` lineage.
- `User.currentRaidId` is ignored for authorization; current same-life `PartyParticipant` and the frozen boss roster are canonical.
- Review hardening made rendered/redacted acknowledgements compare-and-set safe, kept cleanup and retention live across a disabled rollout, classified Telegram no-op/permanent/transient failures, preserved media/forward routing, made same-life rejoins unique, routed actor-owned due remort membership through chat terminalization and fixed dev-fill/wait boundaries.
- Final review hardening invalidates every active Big Barrel chat composer in one set-based disabled-startup mutation, separates broken editable references from permanent Telegram targets, repairs clean due rows without letting them hide dirty/redaction delivery, keeps replacement-reference writes parked, limits remort party work to the actor's canonical membership and versions repeated remort leader-transfer event keys.
- Configuration audit removed the redundant `BIG_BARREL_RAID_CHAT_ENABLED` environment/config key and the duplicate repository-factory option. Chat availability now follows the existing Big Barrel production gate, while transactional event writing remains wired for durable cleanup of existing state.
- Manual Telegram inspection exposed that the initial Barrel/`/raid` recruiting card bypassed chat-view composition even though active combat showed chat correctly. The route now renders the authorized recruiting excerpt/button through the shared Telegram gate; a focused routing regression covers the exact entry path.
- Manual Telegram inspection exposed that editing the older ForceReply prompt was too easy to miss and could fail silently after a successful post. Acceptance now sends a fresh confirmation message and only best-effort marks the old composer as used; a focused regression covers the edit-failure path.
- Manual Telegram inspection showed player posts and technical events were too visually similar. The shared presenter now bolds only player names and italicizes complete typed system-event sentences without weakening HTML escaping.
- Manual Telegram inspection showed `/online` describing joined recruiters as already being "in" a group raid while also saying no active raid existed. Recruiting rows now say the gathering is in progress, show `joined/participantCap`, and suppress the contradictory inactive notice.
- Manual Telegram inspection showed player posts were visible to others only after opening or refreshing the chat card. Accepted posts now best-effort send every other current participant a separate escaped blockquote notification immediately, while durable coalesced card delivery remains the recovery path. Knocked-out boss-roster participants explicitly retain active read/write authorization and the raid-chat button.
- No chat reward or achievement was added. `src/content/loreBoard.ts` and lore references were reviewed; no lore change is needed for a private coordination surface.
- Manual Telegram observations found the recruiting-card, presence-label, send-confirmation, immediate-notification, knocked-out-access and chat-formatting gaps recorded above. Full post-fix Telegram QA remains pending and no manual PASS is claimed.
