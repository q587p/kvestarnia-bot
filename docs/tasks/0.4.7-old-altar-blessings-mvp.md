# 0.4.7 — Old Altar Blessings MVP

Status: proposed first old-promise catch-up slice after the initial production
party expedition and guild weekly-goal foundation.

## Goal

Open `🪨 Старий жертовник` from the Korchma Yard, let a current character offer
gold for `Благовоління`, and let an eligible Priest spend mana plus favor on one
short selected-stat blessing.

Canonical source material:

- `docs/tasks/archive/0.2.x-old-altar-blessings-mvp.md`;
- `docs/design/old-altar-blessings.md`;
- `docs/balance/old-altar-blessings-balance.md`;
- `docs/content/old-altar-blessings-copy.uk.md`;
- `docs/qa/old-altar-blessings-qa.md`.

## Activation gate

Before implementation, trace active Priest blessing through `/hero`, solo,
training, duel, PartyBoss and GroupCombat summary/freeze paths. The current
selected-stat bonus must either use one canonical blessing-aware combat summary
contract everywhere, or the activated task must explicitly redefine the rite as
non-combat support. Do not ship copy that implies combat power when a combat path
silently ignores the status.

## Scope

- Keep presence at `location.korchma.yard`; add no new grove location.
- Private gold offering: `13 gold -> 1 Благовоління`, capped at `3` favor from
  gold per current character/remort life per Kyiv day.
- Replay-safe offering and rite ledgers with actor/target/current-life snapshots.
- Level 3+ Priest rite for self or an active same-yard target.
- Selected `strength`, `dexterity`, `intelligence`, `charisma` or `luck` bonus;
  one active Priest blessing, `13` minutes, existing `93`-minute actor-target
  wait, `+1..+3`, mana `[8,12,16]`, favor `[1,2,3]`.
- One transaction for gold/favor grant and one transaction for mana/favor spend
  plus blessing mutation.
- Actor and target gates cover canonical `ActiveCombatLease`, legacy pending/
  active raid, adventure/passage/search, defeated and remort flows. Ordinary
  PartySession recruiting/preparation remains allowed only when no combat lease
  exists; the rite never mutates party state.
- Favor, altar rites, active Priest blessings and actor-target waits bind to both
  current actor/target lives. Remort of either side invalidates or clears the
  applicable status/wait; old callbacks cannot reach the new life.
- Preserve direct `Хто поруч` Priest aid unchanged.

## Non-goals

- Manatka offerings, `Тихий Корінь`, public offerings, resurrection or reward
  multipliers.
- Guild power, new combat formulas, item instances, market/trade or broad
  noncombat-profession infrastructure.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Duplicate, stale and concurrent offering confirms spend/grant once; favor and
   daily cap are scoped to the current remort life.
2. Rite confirm rechecks class, level, location, target presence, active flow,
   mana, favor, existing blessing and repeat wait inside the mutation boundary.
3. The chosen combat/noncombat effect contract is tested on every supported
   summary/freeze path and is truthful in player copy.
4. Restart/remort cannot carry favor, an active altar/direct Priest blessing or
   actor-target wait into another actor/target life; cleanup and stale callbacks
   are tested for remort of either side.
5. No active-combat enum allowlist replaces canonical leases, while documented
   legacy non-lease flow gates and PartySession preparation policy are covered.
6. Direct Priest blessing behavior and unrelated recovery/status behavior remain
   regression-covered.
7. Migration/restore, focused tests, full checks, two-account Telegram QA and
   release-ledger/rollback evidence exist before rollout.

## Manual Telegram QA

Use Priest and non-Priest accounts at the yard. Offer, replay, hit the daily cap,
perform self/target rites, move/busy/remort targets, enter each supported fight
mode and verify the exact advertised stat behavior and expiry.
