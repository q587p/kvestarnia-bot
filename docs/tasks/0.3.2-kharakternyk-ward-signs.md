# 0.3.2 - Kharakternyk Ward Signs

## Goal

Add a narrow Big Barrel Brother lobby support feature for existing `class.kharakternyk`: a level `3+` Kharakternyk can place one ward sign before the raid starts, joined participants can support it, and the sign mitigates Big Barrel broad attacks during the resulting boss fight while its support charges last.

## Scope

- Existing Big Barrel Brother / party-session recruiting lobby and refresh cards.
- One active ward sign per raid lobby/session.
- Placement by eligible `class.kharakternyk` only, costing deterministic `8..11` mana from a base `13` discounted by effective Intelligence plus Luck.
- One support per joined participant before start; the placing Kharakternyk does not also create a support row.
- Support costs deterministic `5..8` mana for every supporter, biased cheaper by effective Intelligence plus Luck.
- Final support count uses only the starting/final raid roster and caps at `7` supporters for an eight-person raid.
- Mitigation formula: `min(95, 25 + 10 * supportCount)`.
- The sign triggers on Big Barrel broad hits while supports remain; `0` supports still gives one activation, while `N > 0` supports gives `N` activations.
- Short Ukrainian lobby, button, blocker, trigger and journal/card copy.

## Non-goals

- No new race, class, location or universal noncombat engine.
- No item crafting, one-use ward charms, shops, gifts, postal transfers, loot, gold, XP, quest progress, public feed row, paid advantage or permanent status buff.
- No mitigation on ordinary attacks.
- No exact hidden support-cost thresholds or formulas in player-facing pre-commit copy.
- No string-parsing Ukrainian combat log lines to detect boss specials; use typed boss action metadata or a narrow domain result flag.

## Acceptance criteria

- Eligible level `3+` Kharakternyk can place a sign for deterministic `8..11` mana in a valid recruiting Big Barrel lobby.
- Placement, duplicate placement callbacks and stale callbacks are replay-safe and cannot spend mana twice.
- Non-Kharakternyks cannot place signs.
- Joined participants can support exactly once before start; non-joined and stale supporters cannot mutate state.
- Support cost is resolved and stored durably before/inside the mutation boundary; duplicate callbacks replay the same cost/result.
- Successful placement/support sends the actor a separate short confirmation with the stored mana spend; duplicate callbacks do not send another spend confirmation.
- Insufficient mana support fails closed with Ukrainian blocker copy and no support row.
- Mana spends update mana regeneration anchors consistently with existing class noncombat mana spends.
- Lobby UI shows support count only, not supporter names.
- If the placing Kharakternyk leaves, remorts or is absent from the starting roster, the sign fails closed and does not carry into combat.
- If a supporter leaves before start, their support row may remain for replay/audit but does not increase final effective support count.
- Mitigation and support charges are frozen at raid start, each broad-hit activation decrements remaining support charges, and refresh/replay/concurrent resolution cannot duplicate an activation.
- Raid card/journal copy clearly shows carried, partially cracked and fully broken sign states, remaining supports and how much mitigation was applied.
- Achievement handling is explicit: add a small rewardless first-use/support hook if it fits the current catalog, or document why achievements are out of scope for this slice.
- Lore Board impact is reviewed and either updated or explicitly documented as unchanged.

## Manual Telegram QA

1. Create or use a Big Barrel eligible `class.kharakternyk`, start a Big Barrel recruiting lobby and tap `🧿 Поставити знак`.
2. Confirm placement spends the deterministic placement cost once and duplicate taps replay safely.
3. Join with one non-Kharakternyk and one Kharakternyk supporter; verify support copy, costs, count-only display and duplicate support behavior.
4. Start the raid and verify broad Big Barrel hits trigger the sign, write the result on the active card and journal, and decrement remaining supports until the sign breaks.
5. Refresh/reopen/replay the raid result and confirm the mitigation does not apply again for already-resolved turns.
6. Verify ordinary boss attacks are unchanged.

## Implementation notes

- Shipped without a schema migration: lobby placement/support metadata lives in the existing `PartyParticipant.snapshotJson` and is revalidated against joined/remort-matching participants at Big Barrel Brother start.
- Placement reserves the session's single ward-sign slot with a `PartySession.version` compare-and-swap guard before spending mana, so simultaneous different placers cannot commit two signs or charge the losing placer.
- Support reserves the participant's single support snapshot with a `PartyParticipant.updatedAt` compare-and-swap guard before spending mana, so simultaneous duplicate support callbacks from the same participant cannot charge mana twice.
- Placement spends deterministic `8..11` mana from effective Intelligence plus Luck and duplicate placement/support callbacks replay the stored state without spending again.
- Support cost is deterministic `5..8` mana from effective Intelligence plus Luck; Kharakternyk supporters no longer get a free class exception.
- Successful placement/support callbacks send a separate actor-only Telegram confirmation using the stored `manaCost`.
- The frozen fight state stores the effective support count, support cap, remaining activations and mitigation, then spends one support activation on each Big Barrel Brother broad retaliation; an unsupported sign still triggers once.
- Big Barrel active cards no longer repeat the auto-defense timer after the viewer has already queued an action for the current turn.
- Big Barrel active cards and journal pages no longer show stale personal cooldown lines after a participant has been knocked out of the raid.
- Achievement hooks are intentionally out of scope for this slice: placement/support are prep metadata with no separate durable event ledger or reward/feed boundary; the decision is documented here and can be revisited if raid-prep achievements get a shared ledger.
- Lore Board was updated for `class.kharakternyk` because the class now has a visible raid-prep trick beyond its combat action.
