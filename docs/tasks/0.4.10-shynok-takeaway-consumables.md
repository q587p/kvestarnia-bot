# 0.4.10 — Shynok Take-away Consumables

Status: proposed after accepted `0.4.3` use catalog.

## Goal

Sell a tiny authored tea/coffee/beer take-away shelf that grants one carried
one-use manatka instead of applying an immediate Shynok drink or food status.

## Scope

- At most three supported catalog entries already understood by `0.4.3`.
- Clear separation between `Випити зараз` and `Взяти з собою` player copy.
- Short server-owned purchase order with current-life, item/content fingerprint,
  price, expiry and terminal result.
- Gold decrement plus one inventory-unit upsert in one replay-safe transaction.
- Catalog/feature/current-life/stale-card blockers and compact purchase history.
- Pending purchase orders are invalidated/cancelled on remort. A completed
  carried stack follows the repository's canonical remort inventory cleanup; any
  active effect follows the explicit `0.4.3` typed-effect remort policy.

## Non-goals

- Broad shop, dynamic stock/pricing, combat-only coffee tricks, food status,
  gifting at checkout, item instances, market or resale coupling.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Duplicate/concurrent confirms spend once and grant exactly one unit; terminal
   replay shows the stored result.
2. Price, feature availability, content fingerprint, character life and gold are
   rechecked at mutation time.
3. Immediate drinks/food and carried items have distinct buttons, copy and state;
   buying take-away never silently applies an effect.
4. Granted items are immediately truthful in `Разові`; pending purchase, owned
   stack and active-effect remort behavior are explicit and independently tested.
5. Economy/balance tests cover prices, repeated purchases and no reward faucet;
   Telegram QA covers old cards, duplicate taps and inventory visibility.

## Relevant files / search terms

- Accepted `0.4.3` catalog, Shynok drink purchase/presenter, purchase-order and
  inventory-upsert patterns, remort cleanup, feature flags and `Разові`.

## Focused tests

- Purchase token parser, catalog/price/current-life drift and terminal replay.
- Concurrent gold spend/inventory grant, insufficient gold and feature-off cases.
- Immediate-drink/food/take-away routing plus pending/completed/effect remort cases.

## Manual Telegram QA

Buy every shelf entry, duplicate/replay/stale cards, change gold/remort/flag, and
verify `Випити зараз` versus `Взяти з собою`, inventory visibility and later use.

## Release surfaces

- Version/changelog/news, task/catalog/balance/QA, Shynok design/copy,
  achievement/Lore decision, release ledger and AI context.
