# 0.1.22 — Monster Abilities & AI

Target version: `0.1.22`.

This task imports the monster ability/loadout proposal after `0.1.21` Combat Action Foundation was merged into `main`. The proposal JSON was treated as authored design input and converted into typed, validated content catalogs.

## Goal

Give every canonical non-training monster a small frozen ability loadout and a fair AI that still uses basic attacks and defense.

## Scope

- Typed content catalogs for 132 monster abilities and 93 monster combat profiles.
- Backward-compatible `CombatState.monsterRuntime` JSON for frozen loadouts, cooldowns, once-per-fight usage, telegraphs, shields and short-lived effects.
- Pure monster AI/effect runtime in `src/domain/combat`, used by persistent fights, timeout auto-actions, skip recovery and failed flee responses.
- Compact player-facing presentation for named monster actions, telegraphs, shields/heals and stored bark IDs.
- Combat simulator action-mix and ability-usage metrics through level 23.

## Non-goals

- No player race/signature/title abilities.
- No item or consumable active abilities.
- No multi-enemy, party, raid or boss-lobby runtime.
- No Yeger matching/progress change.
- No timeout cap, participation gate or auto-victory reward suppression.
- No reward/economy/loot rebalance.
- No new Prisma analytics migration.
- Training doppelganger keeps copied class-skill behavior.

## Behavior Decisions

- New ordinary monster fights freeze selected ability IDs at combat start. Old active fights without `monsterRuntime` keep legacy basic-attack behavior until they finish.
- Explicit authored ability IDs fill identity slots first. Deterministic fallback only fills legal missing slots after difficulty scaling raises the effective level.
- Ordinary monsters cannot use abilities on consecutive monster actions. Boss profiles can chain at most two different abilities before owing a basic/defend action.
- Telegraph abilities spend one monster activation warning the player, then resolve the promised ability on the next legal monster activation if the monster is still alive.
- Class-skill locks are server-authoritative no-ops: no mana, turn, cooldown, monster action, bark or RNG advance when the locked skill is pressed.
- Group-ready target scopes degrade to the current solo fight target set; no Telegram user IDs enter target resolution.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Content validation finds exactly 132 stable ability IDs and 93 current monster profiles with no missing/extra monster IDs.
- New fights persist a frozen runtime block; legacy rows lacking it remain readable and legacy-safe.
- Monster AI is deterministic under injected RNG and falls back to basic attack when no legal ability remains.
- Runtime state round-trips through the Prisma solo combat repository without unsafe blanket casts.
- Fight cards show concise Ukrainian labels/effects without exposing internal weights or effect keys.
- Timeout auto-attack and skip recovery paths call the same monster AI exactly once.
- Yeger progress and reward semantics remain unchanged.
