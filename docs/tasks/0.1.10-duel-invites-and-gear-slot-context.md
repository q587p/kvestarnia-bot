# 0.1.10 — Duel Invites and Gear Slot Context

Date: 12026-06-17
Status: in PR

## Goal

Ship the first safe, rewardless social-combat entry point: a `/duel` invite flow that creates shareable Telegram deep links, records replay-safe results and gives cautious onboarding/resource warnings before a player accepts.

Also make filtered equipment inventory lists clearer by showing the currently equipped item and its effect before replacement candidates.

## Scope

- Add duel challenge persistence, service logic and pure result resolution.
- Add `/duel`, Quest Hub entry points, deep-link payload routing and duel callbacks.
- Generate invite links from configured bot usernames so dev/prod bots can produce the right URL.
- Warn invite recipients who do not have a character yet that duels need a few minutes of onboarding and some basic gear.
- Warn players with partial HP or mana before accepting, while still allowing an explicit accept.
- Show current-slot gear context in weapon, chest and accessory inventory filters.
- Update release surfaces and Phase 2 docs.

## Non-goals

- No rewards, wagers, rating, durability loss or item transfer.
- No full PvP combat engine yet.
- No matchmaking, rematches, tournaments or public leaderboards.
- No Mini App UI.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `/duel` creates an open invite for eligible characters and returns a bot deep link.
- `/start duel_<token>` routes to the invite flow.
- Accept, decline, cancel, expired and replayed challenge states are handled idempotently.
- Missing-character invite recipients get polite onboarding copy instead of a hard failure.
- Partial HP/mana recipients see a warning and can still explicitly accept.
- Filtered equipment inventory views show `Вдягнено` and `Ефект` before candidate items.
- Release docs and player news describe the shipped behavior without promising rewards or full PvP.

## Manual Telegram QA

- Create a duel invite from a character at level 3+.
- Open the invite from a player with no character and confirm the onboarding copy is gentle.
- Accept with full resources and confirm the result card appears once.
- Accept with partial HP or mana and confirm the warning can be bypassed deliberately.
- Cancel/decline/expire an invite and confirm repeated button presses do not reroll state.
- Open inventory filters for weapon, chest and accessory and confirm current gear/effect appears above candidates.
