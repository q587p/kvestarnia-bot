# 0.3.12 — Varenyk-mancer Sated Support

Status: implementation and automated verification complete on `codex/0.3.12-varenyk-mancer-sated-support`. Manual Telegram QA remains pending; merge and deploy are out of scope.

## Goal

Give living level 3+ `class.varenyk-mancer` characters one distinct noncombat support technique, `🍽️ Нагодувати` (`technique.class.varenyk-mancer.sated-support`), which applies `😋 Ситий`: small immediate HP recovery followed by short HP/mana sustain. This does not change Priest healing or blessing.

## Player contract

- The actor may feed themselves from any existing actionable location.
- Another recipient must be active in the existing presence window and share the actor's exact normalized actionable location.
- Actor and recipient must be alive, in their expected remort lives, outside combat and incompatible blocking flows. The authoritative combat lease blocks; a stale Fight/Mimic/Training presence marker left after that lease ended does not.
- The action uses preview → confirm. A short server-owned `CharacterCooldown` preview proof binds token, actor/recipient identities and lives, exact stat/applied rank, exact cost, effective stats, exact attuned equipment row/slot/version identity, Shynok recovery windows and expiry; commit rejects forged, expired or canonically stale plans.
- Canonical passive mana regeneration settles before the exact mana check and spend.
- Public Varenyk open and preview capture one logical `now`. The successful preview response takes actor/recipient resources, natural/effective maxima, full stats, Priest blessing, active equipment ids, exact attuned rows, stat/applied rank and cost from the same transaction that persists those planning snapshots in the preview proof; the preliminary list cannot leak stale mutable values. Passive regeneration is applied once, tuning gear becomes active inclusively at `readyAt`, and an open without Sated recovery remains read-only.
- Rank is deterministic and attunement-aware: `clamp(1 + floor(max(0, INT - 8) / 3) + floor(max(0, CHA + level - 13) / 7), 1, 5)`.
- Rank mana costs are exactly `8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 23`; rank cannot be selected manually. After canonical passive mana settlement, insufficient mana automatically downgrades to the highest affordable rank, while less than 8 mana blocks the action.
- Preview names the recipient and shows the exact cost, immediate HP recovery, delayed HP/mana effect, 13-minute duration and the caster's 93-minute repeat wait for that recipient. Rank remains internal on every player-facing surface. Confirmation can commit only the exact internally stored rank/cost and target activation observed by preview: later regeneration cannot upgrade it, while lost affordability, equipment/attunement/Shynok drift or another winning feed returns insufficient-mana or stale without mutation. A current-life duplicate confirmation replays the durable current receipt before mutable location, presence, resource or blocking-flow gates and never repeats effects.
- A fresh commit spends mana, then applies capped immediate `2 + rank HP` and no immediate mana. Self-feeding receives no mana refund; the first Sated mana recovery is available only after one complete eligible outside-combat minute or the recipient's first committed supported combat turn.
- Full resources do not block activation. A defeated recipient cannot be fed or revived.
- `Ситий` never stacks. A permitted fresh feed replaces the current activation with a new rank and 13-minute timer after settling every eligible minute of the prior activation. The same caster must wait 93 minutes for that recipient unless their pair wait was explicitly shortened or cleared; another Varenyk-mancer has an independent pair wait and may replace the active status. Concurrent confirmations built from the same observed target activation still yield exactly one winner.

## Persistence and recovery

- Reuse `CharacterCooldown` with versioned `resultJson`; add no model or migration. One recipient row owns the current activation/receipt, while actor-owned pair rows preserve each caster's repeat wait for a recipient across replacement by another caster.
- Persist activation id, actor and recipient life identity, rank, exact mana cost, effective-stat snapshot, start/expiry timestamps, lazy-settlement cursor and canonical replay receipt; persist actor/recipient life identity, activation and availability in the pair wait.
- Before any later claim, replacement or combat freeze, settle the previous current-life payload through `min(now, expiresAt)`. Commit recipient claim, actor-pair wait, actor mana spend, immediate HP recovery, status and receipt atomically with guarded Character/Cooldown CAS results. Duplicate, stale, forged and concurrent confirms replay or fail closed without duplicate mutation, notification or achievement. The pair row's `availableAt` is authoritative, so an explicit local shortening permits that caster to refresh.
- The status starts with 13 wall-clock minutes. Outside combat that expiry follows wall time; every fresh durable combat pulse additionally shortens the frozen status expiry by one minute. Each actor-recipient pair wait remains fixed at 93 minutes unless explicitly shortened and may outlive a status that another caster replaced.
- Outside combat, lazy settlement grants capped `+1 HP` and `+1 mana` per complete eligible minute, starting after one full minute. Before expiry the durable cursor preserves its sub-minute remainder and advances even when full; at expiry it grants only complete eligible minutes, retires the final fraction and stores `cursorAt === expiresAt`.
- Hero and Varenyk public reads retry only expected guarded Character/Cooldown settlement races a bounded number of times. Character-first fallback reads validate Character on both sides of the keyed cooldown read, while the historical fast path validates the current cooldown row on both sides of the Character read. A missing row still validates the canonical Character against Hero's resource/anchor/life fingerprint: unchanged absence remains a cheap `null`, while concurrent remort or cleanup returns the authoritative current-life Character. If all three optimistic fallback pairs are unstable, one `Serializable` read-only transaction returns a coherent Character/current-life status pair without mutation. A response therefore linearizes wholly before or after a concurrent settlement, remort or fresh activation instead of mixing resources, anchors, cursor or activation identity. If no winner is proven, recovery remains pending for a later settlement instead of producing a generic Telegram error or fabricated recovery. Unexpected database errors still surface; mutation paths keep their guarded fail-closed CAS contract, and recovery notification state is produced once.
- Only actual combat-lease time is excluded from later recovery. Hero reads during a matching lease do not move the durable cursor or consume the frozen remainder. Pre-lease outside time settles first. The entire public turn-duel acceptance path uses one authoritative active-equipment set at the same logical `now`: tuning rows contribute nothing before the inclusive `readyAt` boundary to passive resource values, warning or confirmation values, natural maxima, stats, set effects, granted actions or DEX/LUCK initiative. Turn acceptance preflight is read-only; after the pending challenge and leases are claimed, the winning repository transaction recomputes that boundary, atomically persists passive/Sated resources and anchors through guarded CAS, and remains final authority for the actor and balanced snapshot. A concurrent loser or duplicate cannot write resources or reroll initiative. Ordinary Fight and Training Doppelganger starts share one repository path that atomically persists complete pre-lease Sated recovery and full-resource anchors to `Character`; a lost Character CAS rolls back the cursor, session and lease, only the stored state owns later combat damage, spend, healing and pulses, and absolute terminal settlement does not add the pre-lease recovery again. Big Barrel start keeps the same pre-lease-only persistence boundary. The exact freeze/start time is stored in each solo, turn-duel and Big Barrel lease. The combat state is the lease-long owner of the original outside remainder; timeout, hard expiry, restart, terminal resolution, malformed/orphan cleanup and Big Barrel remort cancellation atomically claim each matching lease, advance the durable cursor once through the logical release time and delete the lease. Early malformed solo cleanup releases through `min(observedCleanupAt, session.expiresAt)`. Duplicate or losing releases leave the cursor unchanged, a concurrent orphan loser is benign only after the exact lease is already claimed/released, and an old cleanup never rewrites a newer activation; adventure/raid ids alone do not discard recovery. Pulses never resurrect at `0 HP`.
- Recipient remort clears old-life status and makes old-life pair waits inapplicable. Actor remort clears that actor's pair waits but does not cancel a committed status on another recipient.

## Stored combat

- Freeze the active contract into each owning stored combat state at combat start. Direct solo starts, pending-passage first attacks/restarts, Training, turn-duel acceptance and Big Barrel all use the same authoritative freeze contract for their resource domain.
- After the recipient's own committed turn or round, grant at most one capped `+1 HP` / `+1 mana` pulse after action/resource spending and before the following hostile response. In persistent PvE, an enemy alive at the start of an attack/class-skill exchange keeps its canonical restricted basic/offensive final response even when that exchange reduces it to `0 HP`; mutual `0 HP` against the final enemy remains a hero victory, while hero `0 HP` with another enemy alive remains a loss.
- Every fresh durable pulse consumes one additional minute of the status. The shortened expiry is stored in combat state, shown on active cards/journals and atomically written back to the matching current activation when its combat lease releases; replayed pulse identities consume no additional duration.
- Pulse identity includes activation, combat kind, session, canonical turn/round and recipient. Replays, restarts, rejected actions, stale callbacks and losing CAS attempts cannot pulse twice.
- Cover persistent single/multi-enemy PvE, Training Doppelganger, turn-based duels and Big Barrel Brother participants once per round.
- Quick duels receive no synthetic pulse.
- Relevant cards and stored journals use one shared compact effect-bearing formatter. Outside combat it renders `😋 Стан: Ситий ще N хв — +1 HP і +1 мани …`; in combat the same remaining portions render as `ще N ходів`. Durations and resource values are bold, technical rank is never player-facing, and every stored combat journal recovery names the recipient while omitting zero components. Fresh actor/recipient results bold player names, use the established class-support paragraph rhythm and blank-line groups, point to the status in the character card, reuse the shared `💫` mana-spent line and show only immediate HP that actually changed. Preview groups target, exact cost/effects, status/personal wait and the closing line into separate paragraphs. Lazy Sated recovery discovered by Hero is sent once as a separate message, never embedded in the Hero card; an ordinary full-HP passive recovery notice still appears exactly once in the Hero card when produced. Active Shynok drink rows also bold their remaining minutes and numeric modifiers.
- After status expiry, cards stop calling the recipient `Ситий`. Hero shows a wait only for the viewer's own self-feed pair; `Хто поруч` shows the current caster's personal pair wait. When that wait is absent, an active target remains actionable as `оновити Ситого`.
- Fight, multi-enemy Fight, Training Doppelganger, turn duel and Big Barrel active cards/stored journals render the shortened combat-turn count from persisted state; journal recovery never recalculates from current state or duplicates through notices.
