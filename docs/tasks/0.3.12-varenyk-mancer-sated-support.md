# 0.3.12 — Varenyk-mancer Sated Support

Status: implementation and automated verification complete on `codex/0.3.12-varenyk-mancer-sated-support`. Manual Telegram QA remains pending; merge and deploy are out of scope.

## Goal

Give living level 3+ `class.varenyk-mancer` characters one distinct noncombat support technique, `🍽️ Нагодувати` (`technique.class.varenyk-mancer.sated-support`), which applies `😋 Ситий`: small immediate HP/mana recovery followed by short HP/mana sustain. This does not change Priest healing or blessing.

## Player contract

- The actor may feed themselves from any existing actionable location.
- Another recipient must be active in the existing presence window and share the actor's exact normalized actionable location.
- Actor and recipient must be alive, in their expected remort lives, outside combat and incompatible blocking flows.
- The action uses preview → confirm. A short server-owned `CharacterCooldown` preview proof binds token, actor/recipient identities and lives, exact stat/applied rank, exact cost, effective stats, exact attuned equipment row/slot/version identity, Shynok recovery windows and expiry; commit rejects forged, expired or canonically stale plans.
- Canonical passive mana regeneration settles before the exact mana check and spend.
- Rank is deterministic and attunement-aware: `clamp(1 + floor(max(0, INT - 8) / 3) + floor(max(0, CHA + level - 13) / 7), 1, 5)`.
- Rank mana costs are exactly `8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 23`; rank cannot be selected manually. After canonical passive mana settlement, insufficient mana automatically downgrades to the highest affordable rank, while less than 8 mana blocks the action.
- Preview names the recipient and clearly separates the deterministic stat rank from the affordable applied rank, exact cost, immediate recovery, 13-minute duration and 93-minute recipient wait. Confirmation can commit only that exact stored rank/cost: later regeneration cannot upgrade it, while lost affordability or equipment/attunement/Shynok drift returns insufficient-mana or stale without mutation. A current-life duplicate confirmation replays the durable receipt before mutable location, presence, resource or blocking-flow gates and never repeats effects.
- A fresh commit spends mana, then applies capped immediate `2 + rank HP` and `1 mana`; self-feeding follows the same spend-before-restore order.
- Full resources do not block activation. A defeated recipient cannot be fed or revived.
- `Ситий` never stacks, refreshes, extends or changes rank while active, even if a local helper or manual edit shortened/cleared the 93-minute wait. A fresh activation is possible only after the 13-minute status is inactive and the recipient-global wait has genuinely ended.

## Persistence and recovery

- Reuse `CharacterCooldown` with versioned `resultJson`; add no model or migration.
- Persist activation id, actor and recipient life identity, rank, exact mana cost, effective-stat snapshot, start/expiry/wait timestamps, lazy-settlement cursor and canonical replay receipt.
- Before any later claim or combat freeze, settle the previous current-life payload through `min(now, expiresAt)`. Commit recipient claim, actor mana spend, immediate recovery, status/wait and receipt atomically with guarded Character/Cooldown CAS results. Duplicate, stale, forged and concurrent confirms replay or fail closed without duplicate mutation, notification or achievement. The payload's original `availableAt` remains authoritative even if only the row timestamp is shortened.
- The status lasts exactly 13 wall-clock minutes. The recipient-global wait lasts 93 minutes and may outlive status expiry.
- Outside combat, lazy settlement grants capped `+1 HP` and `+1 mana` per complete eligible minute, starting after one full minute. Before expiry the durable cursor preserves its sub-minute remainder and advances even when full; at expiry it grants only complete eligible minutes, retires the final fraction and stores `cursorAt === expiresAt`.
- Only actual combat-lease time is excluded from later recovery. Hero reads during a matching lease do not move the durable cursor or consume the frozen remainder. Pre-lease outside time settles first. The complete public turn-duel acceptance path uses one authoritative equipment set at the same logical `now`: tuning rows are absent from passive resource writes, warnings, confirmation values, natural maxima, stats, set/grant effects, initiative and the final balanced snapshot until the inclusive `readyAt` boundary. Only the winning transactional claim rolls initiative; stale or concurrent callers cannot dictate/reroll the first actor. Turn-duel, solo PvE/Training and Big Barrel starts persist exact pre-lease Sated recovery and full-resource anchors to `Character` with guarded Character/Cooldown CAS before combat state takes ownership; later combat damage, spend, healing and pulses remain combat-local, and base max columns are never rewritten. The exact freeze/start time is stored in each solo, turn-duel and Big Barrel lease. The combat state is the lease-long owner of the original outside remainder; timeout, hard expiry, restart, terminal resolution, malformed/orphan cleanup and Big Barrel remort cancellation atomically claim each matching lease, advance the durable cursor once through the logical release time and delete the lease. Early malformed solo cleanup releases through `min(observedCleanupAt, session.expiresAt)`. Duplicate or losing releases leave the cursor unchanged, a concurrent orphan loser is benign only after the exact lease is already claimed/released, and an old cleanup never rewrites a newer activation; adventure/raid ids alone do not discard recovery. Pulses never resurrect at `0 HP`.
- Recipient remort clears old-life status and wait. Actor remort does not cancel a committed status on another recipient.

## Stored combat

- Freeze the active contract into each owning stored combat state at combat start.
- After the recipient's own committed turn or round, grant at most one capped `+1 HP` / `+1 mana` pulse after action/resource spending and before the following hostile response. In persistent PvE, an enemy alive at the start of an attack/class-skill exchange keeps its canonical restricted basic/offensive final response even when that exchange reduces it to `0 HP`; mutual `0 HP` against the final enemy remains a hero victory, while hero `0 HP` with another enemy alive remains a loss.
- Pulse identity includes activation, combat kind, session, canonical turn/round and recipient. Replays, restarts, rejected actions, stale callbacks and losing CAS attempts cannot pulse twice.
- Cover persistent single/multi-enemy PvE, Training Doppelganger, turn-based duels and Big Barrel Brother participants once per round.
- Quick duels receive no synthetic pulse.
- Cards show a compact `😋 Ситий` row with canonical remaining duration and authoritative post-settlement resources in the same Hero response; an ordinary full-HP recovery notice appears exactly once in that card when produced. An expired duplicate receipt shows only the remaining recipient wait, or fresh availability after both gates end. Historical receipt rows whose status/wait ended and cursor reached expiry remain replayable without forcing the heavyweight settlement transaction on later Hero opens. Show a recovery line only when HP or mana changed.
- After status expiry, cards stop calling the recipient `Ситий` and instead show the canonical `🍽️ Нагодувати знову через …` wait, including the self-feed wait.
