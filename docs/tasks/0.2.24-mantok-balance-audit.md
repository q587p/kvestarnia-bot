# 0.2.24 - Mantok Balance Audit and Rebalance Pass

## Goal

Audit and rebalance reachable manatky after the canonical equipment slot foundation so rarity, price, equipment slot, effects and player-facing item identity make sense together.

This is a balance/content pass on top of `0.2.23`, not a new equipment architecture slice.

## Scope

- Keep the canonical slots from `0.2.23`: `weapon`, `offhand`, `head`, `chest`, `legs`, `accessory` and `tool`.
- Audit authored equipment and generated Loot Expansion v1 equipment variants for slot/effect/value coherence.
- Tune generated Loot Expansion v1 equipment effect mapping so base stats and `+N` enhancement tiers produce visible stat differences.
- Trim very cheap authored trophy equipment that carried too much chest-slot power for its price tier.
- Keep ordinary consumables and medical items from `0.2.22` unchanged.
- Preserve equipped/protected item safety for gifting/postal delivery, Shynok sale, Mantok Chest, Munchkin barter and remort.
- Follow-up: add rewardless equipment achievements for filling all seven canonical equipment slots and for a hidden cumulative `93` successful-equips milestone.
- Follow-up: add `/chronicles` as a direct `/help` command to the existing `📜 Хроніки Квестарні` / `📣 Останні події` feed, without adding it to the Telegram side menu.
- Follow-up: reorder the equipment card/buttons into body-first order and rename the player-facing `weapon` slot label to `Основна рука` without changing the canonical `weapon` storage key.
- Follow-up: add narrow hand-compatibility rules on top of the slot foundation: warriors can equip ordinary weapons into `offhand`, other classes need explicit `offhand` items, and `twohand` items occupy both hand slots with confirmation before auto-clearing a conflicting hand.
- Follow-up: fix remort achievement emission so completed remorts immediately track the remort milestone and the newly selected race/class identity achievements once; replayed remort callbacks stay no-op for achievement progress.

## Non-goals

- No Prisma migration.
- No new equipment slots.
- No broad weapon-style system beyond the small `offhand`/`twohand` hand-compatibility pass.
- No class ability, Priest/Thief, taunt, shield or epic-system changes.
- No duel tournament, crafting, shop, market, trading, Big Barrel, party-boss or Social Games mechanics changes.
- No broad achievement expansion beyond the requested equipment milestones.

## Balance rationale

- Generated Loot Expansion v1 equipment previously flattened many `+1` variants to the same effects as `+0` while raising price. Enhancement tiers now increase at least one visible stat inside each generated practical family without lowering existing bonuses.
- Generated weapon damage now reflects the source package `damage` band, and rare crit-bearing weapons gain a small visible `luck` identity instead of being only expensive `+1` weapons.
- Generated armor now maps to canonical `chest` equipment metadata and scales armor/HP more clearly by enhancement.
- Generated accessory/tool utility remains simple visible stat identity; no hidden procs or invisible power were added.
- Very cheap authored monster equipment remains mostly trophy-scale: the `Клітинка відповідального болю` and `Пляма хоробрості на фартуху` keep small defensive identity but no longer match stronger starter chest gear at junk-tier prices.
- Hand compatibility stays conservative: `class.warrior` may dual-wield ordinary weapons, other classes need an explicit `offhand` tag or slot, and `twohand` items are one equipped item that visually and achievement-wise occupies both hand slots without doubling combat effects.

## Acceptance criteria

- Generated equippable Loot Expansion v1 variants declare canonical equipment slots.
- Generated enhancement tiers are monotonic inside each practical family: a higher enhancement costs more, never loses a visible stat and gains at least one visible stat.
- Very cheap authored equipment stays capped at trophy-scale power.
- Effective-stat aggregation applies tuned generated equipment bonuses.
- Existing protected/equipped item flows remain unchanged.
- Equipment achievements unlock from successful changed equips only: filling all seven canonical slots is visible, while the cumulative `93` successful-equips milestone stays hidden until earned.
- A `twohand` main-hand item counts as both `weapon` and `offhand` for the full-slots achievement; replacing it through either hand requires a confirmation card and leaves the cleared manatka in the bag.
- Confirming a remort emits live achievement events for `remort.completed` and the selected post-remort race/class identity exactly once; the existing `🔎 Перевірити` recalculation remains an idempotent safety net for historical rows.
- Release surfaces describe the rebalance without exposing hidden odds or formulas in player-facing news.

Achievement decision: follow-up adds two rewardless equipment milestones on the existing equip action. `Усі гачки при справі` is visible for filling all seven canonical equipment slots; `Девʼяносто три примірки без протоколу` is hidden and advances through cumulative changed equip events, not through duplicate same-item no-op callbacks.

Latest-events decision: `/chronicles` is only a direct `/help` entry point to the existing latest-events feed. It does not add a Telegram side-menu command, new ledger, feed category, reward, hidden progress path or second achievement.

Slot naming decision: the canonical slot remains technical `weapon`, but player-facing equipment surfaces say `Основна рука` because the slot may later hold non-weapon manatky. The card and slot-filter buttons now read top-to-bottom through body slots before the two hand slots.

Hand-rule decision: this slice keeps the seven canonical slots and adds tags, not architecture. `offhand` marks items that any class may equip in the second hand; warriors may also put ordinary weapons there. `/help` now names that visible warrior rule so the new behavior is not hidden behind trial and error. `twohand` marks a main-hand item as occupying both hands in equipment views and full-slot achievement recalculation. Confirmed twohand replacements automatically clear the conflicting hand row in the same repository transaction and leave that manatka in the bag, while combat stat aggregation still reads the single persisted item once.

Duplicate equip decision: the production equipment repository now returns an atomic `changed` flag from the slot write before `equipment.item_equipped` achievement progress is emitted. Sequential no-op callbacks and rapid duplicate same-item callbacks can replay the equip result without advancing the hidden cumulative `93` progress twice.

Remort achievement decision: remort already persists the selected new identity in `character_remorts.preservedPayloadJson`, and manual achievement recalculation can recover historical race/class rows from that ledger. The live confirmation path now also emits the remort completion event and a `character.created` identity event for the selected new race/class, using the remort id as source and skipping replayed callbacks, so active remort-heavy players do not need manual recalculation to see current-life identity achievements. Already-missed historical remort race/class rows are intentionally handled by the existing idempotent `🔎 Перевірити` repair path rather than an automatic production backfill in this balance PR; if the team wants a broad production sweep, that should be a separate operator/backfill note with target DB review.

Lore decision: `📖 Перекази` was reviewed through the `0.2.23` manatky concept context. No lore entry changed because the in-world concept of manatky is unchanged; this slice only adjusts numbers and generated slot metadata.
