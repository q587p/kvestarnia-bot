# 0.2.24 - Mantok Balance Audit and Rebalance Pass

## Goal

Audit and rebalance reachable manatky after the canonical equipment slot foundation so rarity, price, equipment slot, effects and player-facing item identity make sense together.

This is a balance/content pass on top of `0.2.23`, not a new equipment architecture slice.

## Scope

- Keep the canonical slots from `0.2.23`: `weapon`, `offhand`, `head`, `chest`, `legs`, `accessory` and `tool`.
- Audit authored equipment and generated Loot Expansion v1 equipment variants for slot/effect/value coherence.
- Tune generated Loot Expansion v1 equipment effect mapping so base stats and `+N` enhancement tiers produce visible stat differences.
- Trim very cheap authored trophy equipment that carried too much chest-slot power for its price tier.
- Keep ordinary consumables and medical items from `0.2.22` unchanged.
- Preserve equipped/protected item safety for gifting/postal delivery, Shynok sale, Mantok Chest, Munchkin barter and remort.

## Non-goals

- No Prisma migration.
- No new equipment slots.
- No two-handed/offhand conflict mechanics.
- No class ability, Priest/Thief, taunt, shield or epic-system changes.
- No duel tournament, crafting, shop, market, trading, Big Barrel, party-boss or Social Games mechanics changes.
- No new achievements; this slice only tunes balance/content and adds no new visible player action.

## Balance rationale

- Generated Loot Expansion v1 equipment previously flattened many `+1` variants to the same effects as `+0` while raising price. Enhancement tiers now increase at least one visible stat inside each generated practical family without lowering existing bonuses.
- Generated weapon damage now reflects the source package `damage` band, and rare crit-bearing weapons gain a small visible `luck` identity instead of being only expensive `+1` weapons.
- Generated armor now maps to canonical `chest` equipment metadata and scales armor/HP more clearly by enhancement.
- Generated accessory/tool utility remains simple visible stat identity; no hidden procs or invisible power were added.
- Very cheap authored monster equipment remains mostly trophy-scale: the `Клітинка відповідального болю` and `Пляма хоробрості на фартуху` keep small defensive identity but no longer match stronger starter chest gear at junk-tier prices.

## Acceptance criteria

- Generated equippable Loot Expansion v1 variants declare canonical equipment slots.
- Generated enhancement tiers are monotonic inside each practical family: a higher enhancement costs more, never loses a visible stat and gains at least one visible stat.
- Very cheap authored equipment stays capped at trophy-scale power.
- Effective-stat aggregation applies tuned generated equipment bonuses.
- Existing protected/equipped item flows remain unchanged.
- Release surfaces describe the rebalance without exposing hidden odds or formulas in player-facing news.
