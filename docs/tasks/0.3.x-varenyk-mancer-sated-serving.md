# 0.3.x - Varenyk-mancer Sated Serving

## Goal

Ship a narrow class support technique for `class.varenyk-mancer`: a level 3+ Varenyk-mancer can feed themself or an active same-location adventurer, applying a 13-minute `Ситий` status with small immediate HP/mana recovery and tiny HP/mana ticks over time.

## Scope

- Add a narrow Varenyk-mancer non-combat technique without a universal cooking/crafting engine.
- Surface self-use from the hero/class action surface.
- Surface target-use through `👀 Хто поруч` / same-location presence.
- Allow use from any current location outside blocking active flows.
- Apply a visible timed `Ситий` status for 13 minutes.
- Start a 93-minute recipient food cooldown on successful application.
- Spend Varenyk-mancer mana using Priest-like support tiers: `8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 23` by deterministic serving rank `1..5`.
- Resolve rank from canonical effective actor stats: intelligence primary, charisma/level secondary.
- Restore a small immediate amount, then tick small HP/mana recovery:
  - outside combat: once per minute bucket;
  - in combat: once per target own turn instead of wall-clock minute.
- Store rank, mana cost, stat snapshot and tick/replay state durably enough to prevent rerolls, double spend or double ticks.
- Notify another target privately only after durable success.
- Update Lore Board / player identity docs for the shipped side action.

## Non-goals

- No broad cooking, crafting, shops, market, transfer or itemized food.
- No new location in the MVP.
- No gold, XP, loot, achievements with power, quest/hunt/adventure progress or public feed row.
- No party-wide feast.
- No feeding active combat targets as a new action; only pre-existing `Ситий` status ticks during combat.
- No exact hidden odds in player-facing copy.
- No Prisma schema migration unless the existing status/cooldown model cannot support replay-safe state.

## Acceptance criteria

- Eligible level 3+ Varenyk-mancers can feed self when not in a blocking flow and enough mana is available.
- Eligible Varenyk-mancers can feed active exact-normalized same-location targets that are not blocked, not already `Ситий`, and not under food cooldown.
- Ineligible actors, insufficient mana, blocked flows, stale/remorted callbacks, inactive/moved targets, active-combat targets, already-sated targets and target food cooldowns fail safely without spending mana.
- Successful application spends mana exactly once, applies immediate HP/mana capped by target effective max values, creates one visible `Ситий` status and starts the recipient cooldown.
- Duplicate/replay callbacks show the stored result and do not reroll rank, spend mana again, restart cooldown or apply immediate recovery again.
- Outside-combat ticks restore small HP/mana at most once per minute bucket while the status is active.
- Combat ticks restore small HP/mana at most once per target own combat turn while the status is active, and do not tick on enemy turns.
- Expired `Ситий` statuses no longer affect recovery or presentation.
- Target notifications are best effort and happen only after the stored success exists.
- Ukrainian player-facing copy is compact and does not show exact formula math.

## Manual Telegram QA

- Create a level 3+ Varenyk-mancer with enough mana.
- Self-feed from the hero/class action surface.
- Verify immediate HP/mana capped recovery and visible `Ситий`.
- Open `Хто поруч` with another active same-location character and feed them.
- Verify target private notification.
- Try feeding the same target again before 93 minutes: blocked without mana spend.
- Try feeding an already `Ситий` target: blocked without mana spend.
- Move target away before stale callback: blocked/replay-safe.
- Start a fight while `Ситий` is active and verify one tick per own turn, no enemy-turn ticks.
- Let status expire and verify no further ticks.
