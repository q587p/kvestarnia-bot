# Future Task — Cellar Napkin Exchange

## Goal

Give `item.napkin-of-mouse-diplomacy` a bounded cellar use instead of leaving the stack as a permanently priceless joke trophy. A small bundle of signed napkins should be exchangeable for cheese or another authored cellar benefit through an explicit, replay-safe action.

The first candidate is three napkins for one `item.cellar.fancy-cheese`, subject to balance review. The quantity and result must remain content-configurable so the existing quantity `3` is a starting proposal, not an accidental permanent contract.

## Scope

- Add a cellar-only exchange preview and confirmation for the napkin bundle.
- Show the required quantity, current owned quantity and qualitative result before confirmation; do not silently consume partial stacks.
- Consume exactly the configured napkin quantity and grant exactly one configured cheese/cellar result in one guarded transaction.
- Keep the current napkin item non-sellable/priceless outside this explicit exchange; do not create a general junk-item shop.
- Make the exchange safe for insufficient quantity, stale inventory, repeated callbacks, concurrent claims and already-completed result state.
- Leave room for one later alternate cellar use, such as a mouse-diplomacy permit, cellar access token or small non-combat favor, but do not ship several sinks in the first slice.
- Preserve the existing cellar level gates and presence route; the exchange must not resurrect the retired level 4+ mouse dead-end.

## Non-goals

- no broad item trading, selling or crafting system;
- no automatic conversion of every napkin drop into cheese;
- no free cheese from a stale/replayed callback;
- no random exchange rate or hidden odds in the confirmation card;
- no combat, raid, mana or power bonus unless a separate authored cellar result explicitly defines and balances it;
- no removal of the napkin from existing historical inventories.

## Acceptance criteria

- The exchange is visible only when the player has the configured napkin quantity and is in the valid cellar flow.
- The preview makes the bundle size and result clear without exposing internal callback details.
- A successful claim removes exactly the configured napkins and grants exactly the configured result once.
- Insufficient quantity, stale ownership, wrong location, cooldown/lock state and repeated callbacks do not consume or grant anything.
- Existing napkin inspection, inventory rendering and priceless-item behavior remain compatible.
- Tests cover the default quantity-3 candidate, configuration changes, transaction rollback, replay/concurrency and legacy inventories.
- A visible exchange action gets a small rewardless achievement decision before runtime release, and lore/help text remains truthful.
- Any new cooldown or pending session has a narrow non-production manual-QA helper plus production registration tests.

## Manual Telegram QA

- Obtain fewer than, exactly, and more than the required napkin quantity.
- Open the cellar exchange preview, confirm the result, then press the old callback again.
- Inspect inventory before and after to verify exact stack consumption and one result.
- Try the action outside the cellar, during a lock/cooldown and with stale inventory.
- Confirm the existing mouse and grownup-cellar routes still lead to the correct surface.
