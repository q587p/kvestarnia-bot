## Mode A: Quick Dice Poker

Short duel mode:

- Player vs the existing dice opponent/NPC if the current game has one; otherwise use the existing Doppelganger fallback rather than adding a new tavern entity.
- Each side rolls five fair six-sided dice.
- The player may select any number of dice to reroll once, including none.
- The opponent/NPC gets one reroll using a simple deterministic strategy.
- Compare final hands after rerolls.
- Exact equal hands start one deciding round with the same stake/reward.
- Cap repeated draw loops at 3; after that, refund/return the stake and close the session.

Hand ranking, strongest to weakest:

1. `Покер` — five of a kind.
2. `Каре` — four of a kind.
3. `Фул-хаус` — three of one value plus two of another.
4. `Великий стріт` — 2-3-4-5-6.
5. `Малий стріт` — 1-2-3-4-5.
6. `Трійка` — three of a kind.
7. `Дві пари`.
8. `Пара`.
9. `Старша кістка` — no combination.

Tie-breakers must come from one pure hand evaluator that returns rank and a tie-break tuple:

- Five/four/three of a kind: grouped face value first, then remaining kickers descending.
- Full house: triple value, then pair value.
- Straights: 6-high straight beats 5-high straight.
- Two pairs: higher pair, lower pair, kicker.
- Pair: pair value, then kickers descending.
- High dice: all dice descending.
- Exact same rank and tie-break tuple is a draw.

Opponent reroll heuristic:

- Keep all dice for made straight, full house, four of a kind or five of a kind.
- With three of a kind, keep the triple and reroll two kickers.
- With two pairs, keep both pairs and reroll the kicker.
- With one pair, keep the pair and reroll the other three dice.
- With no pair but four dice already forming an open straight candidate, keep those four and reroll the other die.
- Otherwise keep the highest die and reroll the rest, unless a better existing project helper already fits.

Suggested intro copy:

```text
⚡ Швидкі кості
Кидаємо по 5 костей. Один раз можна перекинути будь-які свої кості.
Перемагає сильніша комбінація.
```

Result messages must always include:


## Mode B: Classic Scorecard Poker

Long solo scorecard mode unless a clean multiplayer/NPC scorecard abstraction already exists:

- 13 turns.
- Each turn uses five fair six-sided dice.
- Each turn allows up to three rolls total: initial roll plus up to two optional rerolls.
- After stopping or reaching the third roll, the player must choose one unused scoring box.
- Each scoring box can be used once.
- After 13 boxes are filled, total the score and grant the existing dice reward based on the project economy/balance rules.
- Show score preview for available boxes after every roll.

Upper section:

- `Одиниці` — sum of all 1s.
- `Двійки` — sum of all 2s.
- `Трійки` — sum of all 3s.
- `Четвірки` — sum of all 4s.
- `Пʼятірки` — sum of all 5s.
- `Шістки` — sum of all 6s.
- Upper bonus: +35 if the upper section total is at least 63.

Lower section:

- `Трійка` — at least three dice of one value; score sum of all dice, otherwise 0.
- `Каре` — at least four dice of one value; score sum of all dice, otherwise 0.
- `Фул-хаус` — exactly three of one value and two of another; score 25, otherwise 0. Five of a kind is not a full house in this simplified version.
- `Малий стріт` — any four sequential faces: 1-2-3-4, 2-3-4-5 or 3-4-5-6; score 30, otherwise 0. Duplicates are ignored for detection.
- `Великий стріт` — 1-2-3-4-5 or 2-3-4-5-6; score 40, otherwise 0.
- `Покер` — five of a kind; score 50, otherwise 0.
- `Шанс` — sum of all dice.

Suggested intro copy:

```text
📜 Табличні кості
13 ходів. У кожному ході можна кинути кості до трьох разів, а потім записати результат в одну вільну клітинку.
Кожну клітинку можна використати лише раз.
```

After each roll, show:

- current turn number, for example `Хід 4/13`;
- roll number, for example `Кидок 2/3`;
- dice values;
- selected dice, if any;
- available actions: reroll selected dice, score a box, rules, cancel;
- compact scorecard summary.

When choosing a score box, show only unused boxes and current score previews, grouped by upper/lower section.

## Focused Tests

Pure evaluator tests:

- `[6,6,6,6,6]` is five of a kind and beats `[5,5,5,5,5]` by tie-breaker.
- `[2,2,2,2,6]` beats `[6,6,6,5,5]` because four of a kind beats full house.
- `[2,3,4,5,6]` beats `[1,2,3,4,5]` because 6-high straight beats 5-high straight.
- `[6,6,6,1,1]` beats `[5,5,5,6,6]` as full house by triple value.
- Two-pair and pair ties compare higher pair first, then lower/kickers.
- Exact equal evaluated hands return draw.

Scorecard tests:

- `[1,1,1,3,4]`: `Одиниці = 3`, `Трійки = 3`, `Шанс = 10`.
- `[2,2,2,5,6]`: `Трійка = 17`, `Каре = 0`.
- `[2,2,5,5,5]`: `Фул-хаус = 25`.
- `[4,4,4,4,4]`: `Покер = 50`, simplified `Фул-хаус = 0`.
- `[1,3,4,5,6]`: `Малий стріт = 30`.
- `[1,2,3,4,5]`: `Великий стріт = 40` and `Малий стріт = 30` if scored there.
- Upper section total of exactly 63 grants +35 bonus.

Flow tests:

- Quick mode: initial roll -> optional reroll -> result -> reward/close.
- Quick mode: exact draw creates deciding round, capped at 3 repeated draws.
- Long mode: cannot score used category.
- Long mode: cannot reroll after 3rd roll.
- Expired/stale callback does not grant or remove rewards incorrectly.
