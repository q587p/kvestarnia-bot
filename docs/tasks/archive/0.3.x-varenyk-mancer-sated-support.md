# 0.3.x - Varenyk-mancer Sated Support

Status: superseded by [`0.3.12-varenyk-mancer-sated-support.md`](../0.3.12-varenyk-mancer-sated-support.md). Retained only as historical planning input; do not implement this draft.

## Goal

Give level 3+ `class.varenyk-mancer` a distinct support technique: feed themselves or one active same-location recipient before trouble and grant a short mixed-resource recovery status.

The committed effect is `😋 Ситий`: a small immediate HP/mana gain, one HP/mana recovery pulse per complete out-of-combat minute, or one pulse on the recipient's own resolved turn while in stored turn-based combat.

This is deliberately different from Priest aid. Priest healing is reactive direct HP recovery; Priest blessing is a temporary stat bonus; `Ситий` is small pre-combat HP/mana sustain.

## Scope

### Action and eligibility

- Add one noncombat technique with technical id `technique.class.varenyk-mancer.sated-support`.
- Suggested player-facing action: `🍽️ Нагодувати`.
- Suggested status: `😋 Ситий`.
- Keep these icons distinct from `🥟 Кипляча начинка` and Priest actions on adjacent cards.
- Only a living level 3+ `class.varenyk-mancer` can use the technique.
- The actor may feed themselves.
- Another recipient must be active through the existing presence window and share the actor's exact normalized actionable location.
- The action is available from any existing location; it does not require the Korchma, Shynok, a kitchen, or a quest.
- Feeding is a noncombat mutation. The actor and another recipient must not have an active combat lease or another blocking flow at commit time.
- Recheck class, level, current remort life, canonical location, target activity, active-flow state, resources, and status/wait state inside the mutation transaction.
- Use a preview/confirm flow. Another recipient does not need to accept; notify them privately only after a fresh durable commit.

### Deterministic rank and cost

- Settle the actor's canonical passive mana regeneration before checking availability or spending mana.
- Use attunement-aware effective Intelligence and Charisma from the current character-summary path.
- Calculate and clamp the rank exactly as follows:

```text
rank = clamp(
  1
  + floor(max(0, effectiveIntelligence - 8) / 3)
  + floor(max(0, effectiveCharisma + level - 13) / 7),
  1,
  5
)
```

- Mana cost is fixed by committed rank:

| Rank | Mana cost |
| --- | ---: |
| 1 | 8 |
| 2 | 12 |
| 3 | 16 |
| 4 | 20 |
| 5 | 23 |

- Show the deterministic rank and exact cost before confirmation.
- Store the rank, cost, level, effective-stat snapshot, and current attunement inputs in the receipt. Later equipment/stat changes do not rerank an existing status.
- Do not offer rank selection or automatically downgrade the rank when mana is insufficient.

### Atomic activation

- Use a recipient-owned `CharacterCooldown` key such as `noncombat.varenyk-mancer.sated` for the current activation and the longer recipient wait.
- Keep this slice schema-free. Store a versioned payload in `resultJson`; do not reuse `CharacterDrinkState`, because Shynok drink effects must remain independent.
- The payload must include at least activation id, rules version, actor and recipient ids/remort counts, rank, exact cost, stat snapshot, `startedAt`, `expiresAt`, `availableAt`, out-of-combat cursor, and the current replay receipt.
- The recipient-global wait is `93` minutes from a successful commit. It blocks every Varenyk-mancer, not only the previous actor-recipient pair.
- `Ситий` lasts exactly `13` wall-clock minutes on `[startedAt, expiresAt)` and does not pause offline or during combat.
- Claim the recipient state and perform the actor mana mutation, immediate recipient recovery, status creation, and receipt write atomically.
- On a fresh success, spend the exact rank cost once, then restore `2 + rank` HP and `1` mana to the recipient, independently capped at canonical effective maxima.
- For self-feeding, require the full cost before commit, spend it, and then apply the `+1` mana recovery. The net stored mana delta may therefore be `cost - 1`.
- Full HP and/or mana does not block feeding because the timed status remains useful. A defeated recipient at `0 HP` cannot be fed or revived.
- One recipient may have only one active `Ситий`. It does not stack, refresh, extend, or get replaced by a stronger rank.
- Concurrent feeders must produce exactly one winner. Losing/stale attempts spend no mana, create no status/wait, and send no notification or achievement.
- Duplicate confirmation replays return the canonical committed receipt without repeating immediate recovery, mana spend, notification, or achievements.
- If the existing `CharacterCooldown` row cannot provide these atomic and replay guarantees, stop and report the blocker before adding a migration or generic effect framework.

### Out-of-combat recovery

- While active and outside combat, grant `+1 HP` and `+1 mana` for each complete eligible minute.
- The first pulse is available only after one full minute.
- Settle lazily on canonical resource reads/mutations; do not add a scheduler, polling scan, process timer, or per-minute Telegram notification.
- Settle only through `min(now, expiresAt)` and cap HP/mana independently.
- Advance the durable cursor even when one or both resources are already full, so capped pulses cannot be banked for later.
- Normal passive regeneration and Shynok recovery effects remain independent; apply `Ситий` as an additive capped pulse without replacing their formulas.
- Restart, repeated reads, duplicate callbacks, and concurrent requests cannot grant the same minute twice.
- Settle completed out-of-combat minutes when a combat lease is acquired. Time under an active combat lease must never be granted later as wall-clock catch-up.
- When combat ends, flees, or expires, resume the out-of-combat cursor from the committed exit/settlement time if `Ситий` is still active.

### Stored turn-based combat recovery

- Freeze the current activation id, recipient life, rank, start/expiry timestamps, and pulse contract into the owning combat state at combat start.
- During combat, replace wall-clock minute pulses with exactly one capped `+1 HP` / `+1 mana` pulse after the recipient's own committed action/resource spend and before the following hostile response where the surface has one.
- The pulse cannot make an otherwise unaffordable selected action affordable retroactively.
- Manual attack/skill, valid item or equipment action, committed timeout/default defence, and a committed terminal win or flee action count as the recipient's resolved turn.
- Stale callbacks, overwritten queued actions, rejected actions, losing compare-and-swap attempts, and a knocked-out participant without a resolved action do not pulse.
- If both resources are full, consume/store the turn identity but omit noisy recovery copy.
- Use a durable tick identity derived from activation id, combat kind, session/reference id, canonical turn/round id, and recipient id. Replay or restart cannot pulse twice.
- Multi-enemy PvE grants one pulse per hero turn, not one pulse per enemy response.
- A Big Barrel Brother round grants each eligible living recipient at most one pulse for that committed round, including timeout/default defence.
- Cover all shipped stored-turn surfaces with the same pure pulse helper and surface-specific persisted snapshots:
  - ordinary persistent single- and multi-enemy PvE;
  - Training Doppelganger;
  - turn-based duels;
  - Big Barrel Brother party-boss rounds.
- Quick duels have no durable own-turn identity and receive no invented combat pulse. Their normal pre/post-combat resource settlement remains unchanged.
- Persist compact pulse details in the matching turn/round summary so cards and journals render committed facts rather than recalculate them.

### Presentation, rollout, and support surfaces

- Expose the action from the existing self/hero and `Хто поруч` class-support surfaces without adding a location.
- Preview shows target, rank, exact mana cost, immediate recovery, 13-minute duration, and recipient-wide wait.
- Success copy shows actual capped HP/mana changes and expiry; another recipient receives one best-effort private notification after commit.
- Hero and relevant combat cards show a compact `😋 Ситий` row with canonical remaining duration.
- Combat cards/journals show a compact recovery line only when at least one resource changed.
- Priest blessing, Shynok drinks, and `Ситий` may coexist and remain mechanically independent.
- Add two rewardless achievements: first fresh successful feeding, and first fresh feeding of another player. Never award an achievement per pulse.
- Update Varenyk-mancer Lore Board copy without exposing exact hidden formulas or turning achievement implementation into player-facing promotion.
- Add `/dev_reset_varenyk_sated` for local QA. It clears the caller's current-life status/wait only.
- Production configuration must not register, list, or mutate through the dev helper, even if a feature-specific dev flag is set.
- Ship the production action behind an explicit off-by-default rollout flag. Tests must prove the disabled path does not expose or mutate the action.

### Remort and expiry

- Recipient remort invalidates and clears both the active status and recipient wait for the old life.
- Actor remort after a committed feeding does not cancel another recipient's valid status/wait: the food was already served.
- Self-feeding resets on remort because actor and recipient are the same character.
- An expired status never grants recovery or appears active, but the 93-minute wait may remain.
- Falling to `0 HP` during the status does not pause expiry and does not allow pulses to resurrect the recipient.

## Non-goals

- No generic food, hunger, fullness, cooking, recipe, ingredient, or profession system.
- No consumable food items, crafting, shop, price, purchase, reward, loot, or economy changes.
- No dedicated kitchen or new location.
- No remote feeding across locations and no accept/decline offer flow.
- No feeding during combat and no replacement for `🥟 Кипляча начинка`.
- No stat bonus, damage bonus, guard, mitigation, overheal, overmana, resurrection, cleanse, or dispel.
- No stacking, status extension, stronger-rank replacement, area feeding, or selected lower rank.
- No Priest heal/blessing rebalance and no credit toward Priest statistics or achievements.
- No generic status-effect engine, regeneration scheduler, background character scan, or broad resource-recovery rewrite.
- No quick-duel hidden-turn simulation.
- No quest, Quest Overview, public Chronicle row, monetization, or unrelated class work.
- No Prisma schema or migration in this slice unless a blocker is reported and a human explicitly approves expanding the task.

## Acceptance criteria

- Only eligible level 3+ Varenyk-mancers can open and commit the action.
- Self and active exact-normalized same-location targets work from every existing location; inactive, remote, blocked, defeated, stale-remort, and stale-location targets fail closed.
- The exact rank formula produces only ranks `1..5`; costs are exactly `8 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 23`.
- Canonical passive mana regeneration and attunement-aware stats are applied before the atomic availability/spend decision.
- Fresh success atomically creates one receipt, one 13-minute status, one recipient-global 93-minute wait, one mana spend, and capped immediate `2 + rank HP` / `1 mana` recovery.
- Concurrent, stale, forged, and duplicate actions never double-spend, double-heal, refresh, notify, or unlock achievements.
- Out-of-combat settlement grants exactly one capped pulse per complete eligible minute, advances through capped pulses, survives restart, and never catches up time spent in combat.
- Every stored combat surface grants at most one capped pulse per recipient own turn/round identity and persists that fact across replay/restart.
- Multi-enemy PvE pulses once per hero turn; Big Barrel pulses once per eligible participant per round; quick duels do not invent pulses.
- `Ситий` coexists with Priest blessing and Shynok recovery without changing either contract.
- Recipient remort clears old-life status/wait; actor remort does not cancel another recipient's committed effect.
- Cards show canonical remaining duration/wait; no per-minute notification spam is introduced.
- Both achievements fire only on matching fresh committed actions.
- The rollout flag and dev helper are unavailable/non-mutating in production when disabled.
- No schema, migration, generic food/status engine, scheduler, economy, quest, or unrelated combat balance change ships.
