# 0.4.8 — Guild Weekly Goal

Status: proposed after measured `0.4.2` party-attack and `0.4.5` guild rollout.

## Goal

Give a small guild one optional weekly reason to form ordinary parties, using the
same expedition/runtime and social/cosmetic-first rewards.

## Scope

- One Kyiv/Holocene weekly period and one server-owned goal definition.
- Eligible completed party expeditions write replay-safe guild contribution
  receipts for current members.
- Compact guild progress/recap and participant-safe contribution wording.
- One capped cosmetic/social completion result and safe Chronicle recognition.
- Late join/leave/kick/remort policy, admin repair and period rollover.
- Funnel/economy monitoring, default-off rollout and kill switch.
- An explicit class-ability parity audit for GroupCombat against every ability
  already available in PartyBoss/Big Barrel. Each ability must either receive
  equivalent typed GroupCombat semantics and tests or a documented raid-only
  reason and a separately scheduled owner.

## Non-goals

- New combat engine, guild boss or guild-only encounter.
- Guild bank, shared items, power buff, wars, leaderboard pressure or FOMO loss.
- Locking non-guild players out of ordinary party expeditions.
- Full guild XP tree; that follows only after observed weekly participation.

## Acceptance criteria

1. One eligible terminal expedition contributes once to one guild/period despite
   callback, settlement, worker and restart retries.
2. Membership at the documented contribution boundary is authoritative. Late
   join/leave/kick/remort cannot duplicate or steal completed contribution.
3. Support participants receive fair credit under the expedition policy; public
   copy does not shame low activity or reveal private timestamps.
4. Completion result is capped, social/cosmetic first and exact-once. It does not
   change combat stats or multiply expedition rewards.
5. Period rollover is timezone-tested and old cards become read-only/replay-safe.
6. Admin repair recomputes from receipts without granting new combat/reward rows.
7. Guild kill switch blocks new weekly writes/UI while ordinary party expedition
   remains available and existing evidence is retained.
8. Metrics show participation/completion without message content or public
   individual rankings; rollout evidence updates the release-state ledger.
9. Every PartyBoss/Big Barrel class ability has an explicit GroupCombat parity
   classification. Implemented parity covers target scope, focus interaction,
   cooldown/resource behavior and presenter/domain regressions.

## Relevant files / search terms

- Guild membership/audit and GroupCombat participant settlement receipts.
- tournament/daily/weekly period helpers and replay-safe reward claims.
- Chronicles, achievements, privacy, release flags and repair commands.

## Focused tests

- Period boundaries and terminal contribution exact-once races.
- Membership join/leave/kick/remort boundaries and admin repair.
- Completion reward replay, kill switch and ordinary expedition isolation.
- Privacy/logging, migration, `npm run check`, `git diff --check`.

## Manual Telegram QA

Use a three-member guild plus one nonmember. Complete expeditions across partial
and full goal progress, repeat callbacks, change membership, cross a local week,
repair/restart and toggle the goal flag. Confirm nonmembers keep ordinary access.

## Release surfaces

- Version/changelog and spoiler-light Ukrainian news.
- Task/QA/design, achievement/lore/Chronicle decisions, release ledger,
  playtesting and compact AI context.
