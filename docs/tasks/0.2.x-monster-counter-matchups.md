# Future Task — Monster Counter-Matchup Profiles

## Goal

Give selected PvE monsters authored reactions to a hero's race, class and combat method. A monster may be unusually aggressive toward one race/class, resist a family of actions, or invite a more suitable counter-helper. The system should make identity and preparation matter without turning a matchup into an invisible hard lock.

The default contract is a soft counter: the affected path becomes worse, while at least one understandable non-magical, magical, class/race, item or escape option remains. Absolute immunity is a rare authored exception that must be telegraphed and have a bypass or retreat path.

## Scope

- Add data-only monster matchup profiles with explicit target dimensions: race, class, action family, damage source and optional encounter context.
- Support bounded profiles such as:
  - race/class-focused aggression or pressure;
  - anti-magic monsters that resist magical damage/effects and are more safely handled with weapon or other non-magical actions;
  - anti-technical monsters that resist weapon/technique-heavy actions and leave magical or other non-technical counters viable;
  - rare, clearly authored immunity or near-immunity with a visible warning and a documented bypass/retreat path.
- Keep the first runtime shape soft by default: prefer damage/accuracy/cost/response modifiers, limited ability locks or altered monster behavior over universal action denial.
- Let a monster use an authored alternative response, such as calling a helper of another race/class, only when the helper contract is separately bounded, idempotent and compatible with the multi-enemy roadmap. Do not couple this task to a full summoner or party system.
- Define how matchup effects interact with class/race abilities, item tags, physical/spell/trick families, monster abilities, remort modifiers and 3+ enemy target selection.
- Show a short player-facing warning or hint when the matchup is discoverable before commitment; do not reveal hidden formulas or exact odds.
- Preserve one encounter reward contract and avoid making the countered path a source of free farming or unavoidable reward denial.

## Non-goals

- no universal race-vs-monster or class-vs-monster rock-paper-scissors matrix;
- no permanent player race/class changes or respec system;
- no silent hard lock where only `Втекти` works;
- no immunity to every action in a family without a readable bypass, preparation or retreat route;
- no full monster faction, diplomacy or helper-party engine;
- no automatic party recruitment or cross-player call in this slice;
- no broad combat rebalance without simulations covering affected and unaffected builds.

## Acceptance criteria

- Matchup profiles are content-validated, deterministic and frozen with the encounter when required by existing combat snapshot rules.
- A focused race/class pressure profile changes the intended target's combat pressure without making unrelated heroes or actions behave differently.
- Anti-magic and anti-technical profiles distinguish magical, weapon/technique and other supported action families through shared domain rules, not presenter-only branches.
- Every authored resistance/immunity has a tested counter path, clear pre-commit warning where discoverable and a bounded fallback if the preferred action is unavailable.
- A monster helper/call outcome, if included, cannot duplicate through stale callbacks and does not silently expand the encounter beyond its configured cap.
- 3+ enemy fights, player flee, monster escape, class/race abilities, item actions, journals and Chronicles preserve the matchup outcome and remain replay-safe.
- Existing monsters without a profile remain behaviorally unchanged.
- Balance simulations cover targeted race/class pressure, anti-magic, anti-technical and neutral profiles across representative builds before production exposure.

## Focused tests

- content validation for legal dimensions, bounded modifiers, immunity bypass requirements and legacy profile absence;
- pure resolver tests for race/class pressure, anti-magic, anti-technical, neutral and fallback paths;
- action-family tests proving class abilities and item tags use the same matchup rules as basic attacks;
- encounter snapshot, stale callback, monster helper and reward-idempotency tests;
- 3+ enemy, monster-escape, player-flee, journal/replay and Chronicles regressions;
- deterministic balance simulations for representative race/class/build combinations.

## Manual Telegram QA

- Preview an encounter with a soft race/class counter and confirm the warning is short, truthful and qualitative.
- Exercise magical, weapon/technique and alternative actions against anti-magic and anti-technical profiles.
- Verify an unavailable preferred path still has a visible counter or retreat route and never leaves the player with a dead-end card.
- In a 3+ encounter, confirm matchup effects stay attached to the correct enemy and do not affect unrelated targets.
- Reload and replay callbacks around warnings, helper calls, monster escape and terminal settlement.
