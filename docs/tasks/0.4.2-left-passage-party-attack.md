# 0.4.2 — Left-Passage Party Attack

Status: implemented in repository behind a default-off production entry flag;
manual Telegram QA, production enablement, merge and deployment remain pending.

## Goal

Turn the exact pending `deep-left` encounter preview at
`PRESENCE_LOCATION_KORCHMA_DEEP_LEVEL1_LEFT` into the first real
production-capable GroupCombat consumer. The player keeps the solo
`⚔️ Атакувати` path and may instead choose `🤝 Покликати в атаку` for a
restart-safe 1–3-person fight against 1–6 enemies.

## Scope

- Atomically reserve the initiating character's exact pending preview, current
  remort life, deterministic seed, normalized location and `PartySession` under
  typed origin `nyz-left-passage-party.v1`; do not overload `periodId`.
- Recruit 1–3 current-life participants for at most three minutes. The current
  leader may start early; the last readiness change starts immediately when
  every joined participant is ready, and the scheduler owns the deadline start.
  Both automatic paths revalidate the authoritative roster atomically under
  `PartySession.version`.
- Revalidate exact location, life, valid persistent resources, active combat,
  search, adventure and expiry at join/start. Never teleport a participant.
  The canonical `adventure.solo-fight` presence marker written by opening this
  preview is compatible with reservation/join/start while any other adventure,
  raid or owned combat lease remains an explicit blocker. Validate resources
  against canonical effective maxima, not raw base HP/mana columns.
- Keep the reserved preview monster as the primary enemy. Start with one enemy
  per participant, then add one deterministic authored backup for every frozen
  participant whose remort count, solo Низ pressure or three-level advantage
  marks them as strong; cap the encounter at six enemies. Freeze the complete
  roster, ability loadouts and difficulty snapshot under `group-combat.v3`.
- Derive each participant's bounded canonical solo Низ pressure with
  `decideThreatEscalation` from history belonging to the participant's frozen
  remort life; legacy history without life metadata applies only to base life.
  Choose the strongest source with roster-order tie-breaking and apply its
  repeated-pressure bonus only to the first backup, capped at level 23. Group
  results never mutate solo pressure history.
- Freeze every remort input; use the strongest remort source with roster-order
  tie-breaking and canonical multi-enemy remort pressure on backups only.
- Reuse the hardened class/race/gear/item actions, deterministic targets and
  timeout guards from `0.4.1`. Freeze only the explicit GroupCombat monster
  ability contract: `royal-scurry`, `cabbage-plate`, `compound-interest`,
  `common-group-rally`, `approved-dam`, `classified-rustle`,
  `return-to-staff`, `smoke-without-approval` and `preapproved-bite`.
  Their authored self/ally/all-player targets, heals, shields, buffs, debuffs,
  damage, cooldowns and once-only markers execute without semantic conversion.
  Unsupported abilities are excluded and fall back to a basic attack; a
  persisted production loadout containing one fails strict validation.
  A separately delivered one-time production
  opening message follows the ordinary-fight title/participant/opponent/tip
  shape and names remort reinforcements or repeated Низ pressure when present;
  the canonical combat card uses the compact ordinary-fight heading,
  cooldown/effect and recap rhythm. One deterministic living monster may speak
  per turn. Player multi-target damage, healing and protection lines state
  their exact target scope and whether damage/healing is per target. Every
  newly defeated monster receives its own in-turn `🧾 Знешкоджено` paragraph.
  An enemy alive when the exchange begins still makes the ordinary final basic
  response after being defeated during participant actions; it cannot use a
  posthumous heal, shield, buff or special. Solo ability/item lines keep their
  icon but omit the redundant actor name and quotes.
  Restart delivery,
  already-active replay and ordinary refresh do not repeat the intro. Authored
  deterministic monster specials, player and enemy contribution totals and a
  rolling last-25-turn resource/cooldown/effect journal remain replay-safe.
  Production combat has no artificial turn-limit defeat while both sides live;
  the 25-turn terminal limit remains exclusive to the rewardless proof.
- Surface the live gathering through `👀 Хто поруч` at the exact location for
  members and non-members. Reuse one preparation presenter for readiness
  markers and the `✅ Готово` / `⏳ Зачекайте` actions; keep
  `⚔️ Почати атаку` available to the current leader.
- Keep exact combat items behind one `🎒 Одноразові манатки` button in both
  GroupCombat and ordinary persistent fights. The submenu exposes only
  currently useful, owned, supported items; opening it spends no item or turn.
- Make `/dev_reset_monster_rest` clear the same consumed-passage completion
  evidence used by left-passage rest. When every nearby candidate is already
  busy in combat, Bard performance copy says that Inspiration must be applied
  before the fight; live mid-combat buff injection remains explicitly deferred.
- Keep full contribution output behind `📊 Статистика` beside `📜 Журнал` on
  the terminal result only. Active combat cards expose neither detail surface;
  stale active journal/statistics callbacks return the canonical fight card.
  The active/terminal battle card remains compact, terminal cards omit
  `🔎 Оновити` after settlement, and stale action/refresh callbacks replace a
  superseded card at the bottom instead of mutating an older message.
- Persist final same-life HP/mana and consumed supplies. Build one bounded
  encounter reward plan from the ordinary persistent-fight XP formula and
  character-level gold band for every frozen enemy; 1×1 therefore matches
  ordinary PvE, while larger groups add enemy budgets before a neutral split
   between participants with a committed manual action. Grant timeout-only
   passengers nothing. Every enemy receives one deterministic ordinary broad-
   loot roll and one ordinary bandage-slot roll; stronger enemy levels improve
   the broad-loot chance, more enemies add opportunities, and the rolls are
   distributed neutrally among eligible manual participants. Authored monster
   loot, eligible Loot Expansion manatky, bandages and the ordinary rare
   `Іскрокамінь` replacement are all possible. A winning result without loot
   says plainly that no manatka dropped.
- After a production victory, persist the same deterministic 13–23-minute
  left-passage discovery window for every participant. During it, no new
  monster preview appears in the left passage; the card shows `🪜 Ярус II`
  above `↩️ Повернутися до Сутеренів`. The second tier is a non-mutating
  construction notice only and cannot be entered in `0.4.2`.
- Validate the complete turn-start action snapshot before start-of-turn effects
  or roster execution. Record every accepted manual action as eligible before
  execution order can end the fight; skipped later actions spend no
  mana/items, tick no cooldowns and apply no effects. Timeout guards remain
  ineligible.
- Settle each participant in one transaction: validate current life, canonical
  plan/receipt and the participant-owned lease; apply resources, XP, gold,
   deterministic per-enemy loot and one privacy-safe Chronicle event; commit the exact immutable
  receipt; then release only that participant's lease. Timeout-only
  participation receives no reward or activity. Settlement may advance a level
  from XP but never lowers a character level already raised after combat start.
- Ship no new left-passage completion achievement or achievement event in
  `0.4.2`. Achievement progression is deferred and is not part of settlement,
  repair, scheduling or rollout. The unavailable Ярус II construction notice
  also grants no achievement because it performs no transition or gameplay
  action.
- Preserve existing generic progression: durable level milestones commit in the
  participant transaction, then fresh manual settlements may emit the existing
  level/item/combat achievement events and separate standard level/achievement
  Telegram notices. Replayed and timeout-only settlements emit none of these;
  no left-passage-specific event or achievement is introduced.
- Keep v1/v2 proof rows readable, repairable and rewardless. Service existing
  combats independently from default-off
  `LEFT_PASSAGE_PARTY_ATTACK_ENABLED`; flag-off blocks new invitations/starts
  while timeout, delivery, repair and settlement continue.
- Fail closed on malformed or semantically untrustworthy production v3 rows.
  Persist an explicit operator-repair state and concise durable reason, retain
  every lease and frozen artifact until a durable production-safe resource
  disposition exists, and exclude the row before every bounded runtime limit.
  Never load it as ordinary combat or downgrade v3 to v1/v2; explicit operator
  inspection remains available without parsing the malformed evidence. Initial
  reads, final reloads and every mutation CAS fence on `repairState = null`;
  quarantine increments the session version without canonicalizing actions,
  participant resources or leases.

## Non-goals

- Guilds, guild-only access or weekly guild progression.
- Big Barrel migration/redesign, public matchmaking, raid finder or group chat.
- Parties above three participants, encounters above six enemies, new authored
  abilities, rare items or a new economy.
- A generic quest engine, broad `FightService` rewrite or cross-mode
  contribution-card rollout.
- Production flag enablement, merge or deployment.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The party button exists only on the exact left-passage pending preview; the
   solo button remains available until the preview is atomically reserved.
   Opening that preview must not make its own party action ineligible.
2. Duplicate creation/start is replay-safe, solo/start races have one winner,
   start consumes the reservation once and cancel/undersized expiry releases or
   safely invalidates it without rerolling the primary.
3. Join/start freeze only the authoritative current 1–3 participant roster.
   Leader transfer, late join, leave and stale due snapshots cannot copy the
   reservation, affect a frozen fight or grant rewards to a departed player.
4. Strongest Низ and remort sources may be non-leaders; ties use frozen roster
   order. Previous-remort and non-base-life legacy wins do not contribute.
   Primary identity/level is unchanged, backups are deterministic from immutable
   reservation/party inputs and all adjustments are explicit in strict state.
5. Timeout auto-guards never count as committed manual actions. Every valid
   manual action accepted for the resolving turn counts even if an earlier
   actor or start-of-turn bleed defeats the final enemy; skipped abilities and
   items consume nothing. Terminal and
   replay cards use persisted damage/heal/guard/control/damage-taken/action
   evidence and do not rank or shame participants.
6. Per-participant settlement retries converge after restart. Pending rows have
   zero attempts/no receipt/no timestamp; completed rows have positive attempts,
   the canonical receipt and timestamp. A failed participant cannot duplicate
   resources, rewards, items or activity, unlock its lease or block another
   participant's settlement. Shape-valid receipt drift enters operator repair
   with the remaining lease and raw evidence retained.
   Exact persisted HP/mana are not truncated to raw base maxima after a valid
   level/equipment-scaled combat, and settlement never lowers a newer live
   character level to an older XP-derived value.
7. Reward totals are one encounter-wide budget made from ordinary persistent-
   fight XP and gold bands for each frozen enemy, then split neutrally across
   eligible manual participants. A 1×1 level-3 fight against a base-level-2,
   effective-level-5 enemy yields exactly 13 XP and 0–3 gold before loot, not
   the former 40 XP / 20 gold shortcut. Loot then uses one ordinary broad roll
   and bandage slot per enemy: effective enemy level scales the broad-roll
   multiplier from `0.75` to `1.5`, enemy count adds independent opportunities,
   and deterministic round-robin distribution can grant authored/Loot
   Expansion manatky, bandages or the ordinary rare `Іскрокамінь` replacement.
   Reward totals and the loot-contract version remain strictly validated.
8. Migration, strict parsers, 1×1 through 3×6 repository coverage, the retained
   2×2/3×3 proof simulator, focused/full checks and manual
   three-account QA are recorded honestly before any production enablement.
9. Readiness auto-start is covered for one, two and three participants. Every
   monster defeat is recorded in the resolving turn; multi-target player
   actions state their scope and per-target values; only one monster remark is
   selected per turn. A defeated enemy that was alive at exchange start makes
   one final basic response, while a start-of-turn defeat does not. Production
   combat continues past turn 25 while both sides live and keeps a rolling
   25-turn journal. Ordinary and group combat expose one reachable,
   non-consuming one-use-item submenu. `📜 Журнал` and `📊 Статистика` appear
   only after the fight is terminal.
10. Every production winner receives one shared deterministic 13–23-minute
    left-passage clear window. Its card exposes the unavailable `Ярус II`
    construction notice and a return to the Сутерени, while other passages
    remain available. The dev monster-rest reset clears this window without
    changing group-combat history.

## Manual Telegram QA

Use three accounts with `NODE_ENV=development`,
`LEFT_PASSAGE_PARTY_ATTACK_ENABLED=true` and
`DEV_GRANT_COMMANDS_ENABLED=true` in the untracked checkout `.env`. Run
`refresh-local-bot.cmd`, verify `status-local-bot.cmd` reports the exact
candidate SHA and confirm the copied runtime `.env` contains the enabled
left-passage flag before opening Telegram. The checkout `.env` alone does not
update a running snapshot.
Cover deep-link and nearby joins, 1/2/3-player early and automatic start,
leader transfer/join/leave races, non-leader strongest pressure/remort, cancel
and expiry, restart during combat/settlement, timeout-only participation,
partial retry and flag-off while an active fight finishes. Confirm no location
changes and record the exact SHA/runtime. Use
`/dev_group_combat_timeout <party-token>` to advance one active timeout boundary
without waiting; the helper is non-production only. Results remain pending until
run.
