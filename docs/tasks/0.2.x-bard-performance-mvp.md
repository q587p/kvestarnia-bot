# 0.2.x — Bard Performance MVP

Status: draft future task. Do not implement until this task is explicitly activated in a fresh version thread after the current branch is merged and `main` is refreshed.

Canonical planning doc: [../NONCOMBAT_TECHNIQUES.md](../NONCOMBAT_TECHNIQUES.md).

## Goal

Ship one narrow non-combat social activity:

> A level 3+ Bard can perform in Shynok on a cooldown. The tavern pays a small daily-capped amount, the action grants small replay-safe role-action XP, and active same-location players may applaud for free or explicitly tip small gold during a short response window.

This proves non-combat technique patterns without creating a universal profession engine.

## Scope

- Shynok entry point for eligible Bards.
- Stable technique id: `technique.class.bard.shynok-performance`.
- Level gate: 3+.
- Recommended cooldown: `93` minutes.
- Recommended live audience window: `13` minutes.
- Freeze canonical effective stats at start.
- Resolve one stored grade from charisma, luck, level and injected bounded RNG.
- Award small house gold once, clipped by a Kyiv-day cap.
- Award small role-action XP once; it must not count as combat, hunt, quest or Korchmar problem progress.
- Snapshot active same-location audience after successful start.
- Audience can respond once with free applause, a voluntary tip, decline or no response.
- Tip options: `1 / 3 / 5 / 13` gold.
- Telegram notifications are best effort and never the authority for game mutation.
- Race/signature flavor may affect text only in this slice.

## Non-Goals

- No universal non-combat/profession engine.
- No Priest, Rogue, Mage, Yeger or race technique runtime.
- No instruments as required items.
- No performance buffs/debuffs.
- No item reward, loot, achievement power, reputation power, public ranking power or quest progress.
- No forced audience charges.
- No public chat spam or global performance feed.
- No generated song text.
- No title mechanics keyed from localized visible text.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `🍻 Шинок` shows a Bard performance action only for eligible Bards in a valid state.
- Non-Bards and under-level characters see a clear unavailable state or no action, according to Shynok UX.
- Active combat, pending Barrel raid, remort transition and wrong-location states block mutation.
- Starting a performance freezes stats, grade, house payout, role-action XP and audience snapshot.
- Refresh/replay never rerolls grade, gold or XP.
- House payout is capped by Kyiv date and may become `0` if the cap is exhausted.
- Tips debit audience and credit performer atomically.
- One audience character can respond at most once per performance.
- Duplicate/stale/expired/remorted/location-mismatch callbacks replay or fail safely without mutation.
- Performer feedback notifications happen only after a real new applause/tip transition.
- Telegram copy is short, Ukrainian, escaped and does not expose exact chance math.

## Manual Telegram QA

Use two accounts in Shynok.

1. Eligible Bard starts a performance.
2. Same-location player receives one private prompt.
3. Player applauds for free.
4. New test performance: player tips `3` gold.
5. Verify both wallets and Bard XP.
6. Repeat the same callback: no second gold or XP mutation.
7. Move audience away before response: controlled stale state.
8. Start combat before response: mutation blocked.
9. Check cooldown text.
10. Check daily house cap.
11. Simulate blocked DM/notification failure: stored performance remains valid.
