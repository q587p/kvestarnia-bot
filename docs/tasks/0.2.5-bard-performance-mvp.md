# 0.2.5 — Bard Performance MVP

Status: implemented in `0.2.5` after `0.2.4` was already claimed.

Canonical planning doc: [../NONCOMBAT_TECHNIQUES.md](../NONCOMBAT_TECHNIQUES.md).

## Goal

Ship one narrow non-combat social activity:

> A level 3+ Bard can perform in Shynok on a cooldown. The tavern pays a small daily-capped amount, and active same-location players may applaud for free or explicitly tip small gold during a short response window.

This proves the first non-combat technique pattern without creating a universal profession engine.

## Scope

- Shynok entry point for eligible Bards only.
- Stable technique id: `technique.class.bard.shynok-performance`.
- Level gate: 3+.
- Cooldown: `93` minutes.
- Live audience window: `13` minutes.
- Freeze canonical effective CHA/LUCK/level and injected RNG result at start.
- House payout is small, replay-safe and capped at `23` gold per Bard per Kyiv day.
- Audience can respond once with free applause, decline or a voluntary tip of `1 / 3 / 5 / 13` gold.
- Tip debit/credit and response completion are one transaction.
- Telegram notifications are best effort and never gameplay authority.
- Local `/dev_reset_bard_performance` speeds manual cooldown/day-cap QA.

## Non-Goals

- No universal non-combat/profession engine.
- No XP, items, buffs, achievements, quest progress, forced charges, instruments, crafting or title-powered mechanics.
- No Priest, Rogue, Mage, Yeger or race technique runtime.
- No public feed or generated song text.

## Acceptance Criteria

- `🍻 Шинок` shows a Bard performance action only for eligible Bards in a valid state.
- Active combat, pending Barrel raid, remort-life drift and wrong-location states block mutation.
- Start freezes stats, grade, house payout, `roleActionXp: 0`, cooldown and audience snapshot.
- Start/live cards label audience as the start snapshot and show the remaining response window separately from the cooldown.
- Refresh/replay never rerolls grade or gold.
- House payout clips by Kyiv date and may become `0` after the daily cap.
- Tips debit audience and credit performer atomically.
- Insufficient-gold tip attempts report the requested tip amount while leaving the stored offered reaction unchanged.
- Audience responses re-check the performer character before mutation: same remort life, still in Shynok, no active combat and no pending Barrel raid.
- One audience character can respond at most once per performance.
- Duplicate/stale/expired/remorted/location-mismatch callbacks replay or fail safely without mutation.
- Performer feedback notifications happen only after a real new applause/tip transition.
- Telegram copy is short, Ukrainian and does not expose exact chance math before commitment.

## Focused Tests

- Domain: deterministic grade, thresholds, payout, daily cap clipping, constants and tip validation.
- Service/repository: class/level/location/combat/raid/remort gates, cooldown, one live performance, frozen snapshot, daily cap, audience filtering, applause, tips, insufficient gold, expiry, duplicate replay and transactional response safety.
- Bot: callback parser limits, Shynok button visibility, dev command catalog/help/presence routes and Ukrainian presenter surfaces.

## Manual Telegram QA

Use two accounts in Shynok:

1. Eligible Bard starts a performance.
2. Same-location player receives one private prompt.
3. Player applauds for free.
4. New test performance: player tips `3` gold.
5. Verify both wallets.
6. Repeat the same callback: no second gold mutation.
7. Try a `13` gold tip with too little gold: copy names the selected amount and lets the audience choose again.
8. Move audience away before response: controlled stale state.
9. Move Bard away, start Bard combat, put Bard into pending Barrel raid or remort Bard before response: mutation blocked.
10. Reopen the Bard performance card while live: it shows response time left, start-snapshot audience and next-performance cooldown.
11. Check cooldown and daily house cap.
12. Simulate blocked DM/notification failure: stored performance remains valid.
