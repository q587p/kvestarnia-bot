# 0.1.24 — Shynok Drinks and Mantok Sales

## Goal

Ship one bounded Shynok economy slice:

- self-service tea, simple beer, fine beer and pepper vodka;
- opt-in drink effects for the existing `🍻 Всім пива` social round;
- safe sale of one, several or all eligible priced manatky for 42% of their combined canonical value.

Use `docs/SHYNOK_DRINKS_AND_MANTOK_SALES.md` as the product and mechanics authority.

Target version: `0.1.24`.

Start from updated `main` after `0.1.23` is merged. Do not stack this work on an unmerged feature branch unless the user explicitly approves it.

Implementation status: completed in branch `codex/0.1.24-shynok-drinks-mantok-sales` with migration `20260623100000_add_shynok_drinks_and_mantok_sales`; follow-up hardening keeps combat drink snapshots transaction-owned, preserves timed recovery windows through lazy expiry/replacement, makes social-round drink replacement an explicit stale-safe confirmation, and replays completed drink/round/sale confirmations from stored results. Follow-up QA should use the manual checklist below plus focused drink/sale/combat tests.

## Scope

### 1. Shynok surface

Add or extend the existing `location.korchma.bar` / `🍻 Шинок` flow with compact actions for:

- self drinks;
- `🍻 Всім пива`;
- manatka sale;
- existing Shynok actions and safe navigation.

All callbacks must revalidate current place and active gameplay locks. Stale callbacks must not teleport the character into the Shynok or mutate economy state.

### 2. Drink catalog and prices

Add stable server-side drink definitions:

| Key | Price | Duration/window | Effect |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `drink.thyme-tea` | 17 gold | 42 minutes | out-of-combat HP/mana recovery ×1.13 |
| `drink.simple-beer` | 13 gold | 23 minutes | recovery ×1.25; accuracy −5 pp |
| `drink.fine-beer` | 42 gold | 42 minutes | recovery ×1.50; accuracy −10 pp |
| `drink.pepper-vodka` | 42 gold | next eligible PvE fight started within 23 minutes | dealt damage ×1.13; received damage ×1.13 for that fight |

Keep numeric mechanics in pure domain/config helpers, not presenters.

### 3. One current drink slot

Persist at most one current timed or queued drink state per character.

- A different or repeated drink requires an explicit replacement/refresh confirmation.
- Do not spend gold on preview.
- Confirmation must be atomic and idempotent.
- Repeated completed callbacks replay stored results.
- Expiry uses server time and may be evaluated lazily.
- A new drink never stacks with the previous drink.

Add a server-owned pending order/token boundary if needed for replacement preview and duplicate callback safety.

### 4. Recovery integration

Tea and beer affect only normal out-of-combat HP/mana recovery.

- No instant refill.
- No max-resource change.
- No in-combat recovery.
- Settle recovery up to purchase time under the previous rules before activating a drink.
- Segment calculations at effect start/end so the multiplier is not applied retroactively.
- Clamp to effective HP/mana maxima.
- Use integer/basis-point arithmetic.

### 5. Combat integration

At eligible persistent solo PvE fight start:

- freeze any active beer accuracy penalty into server-owned combat state;
- atomically consume a non-expired queued pepper-vodka effect;
- freeze vodka outgoing/incoming damage multipliers into combat state;
- preserve the frozen modifier through session replay/restart until terminal state.

Initial exclusions:

- quick and turn-based PvP duels;
- level 1–2 starter mimic combat;
- training doppelganger combat;
- admin/test fights.

Beer affects only actions that perform a real accuracy roll. Vodka modifies positive hero-caused outgoing damage and enemy-caused incoming damage, not healing, shields, mana, self-costs, rewards or unrelated service damage.

Damage rule:

```text
max(1, floor(base_damage * 113 / 100))
```

Legacy combat state without drink metadata must remain readable.

### 6. Social beer rounds

Preserve the existing generosity purchase log and day/week/month leaderboard.

Replace the old launch prices with server-side previewed defaults:

```text
simple = max(93, 13 * recipient_count)
fine   = max(193, 42 * recipient_count)
```

At confirm time:

- use a server-owned recipient snapshot;
- always include the buyer;
- include other active characters inside Kvestarnia under current presence/privacy rules;
- exclude outside/Nyz, active-combat and pending-Barrel-raid characters;
- use a deterministic hard cap of 42 recipients;
- store the exact price and snapshot before spending;
- atomically debit the buyer and create one round purchase plus recipient offers.

The existing completed-today Barrel raid gate remains for `Всім пива`.

Recipient flow:

- offer simple or fine beer for five minutes;
- `Випити` / `Ні, дякую`;
- accept exactly once;
- if another drink is current, the first `Випити` only shows a replacement warning; final replacement confirm revalidates the same offered cup, same recipient, same live drink-state guard and current Shynok access before replacing;
- start duration at acceptance time;
- no refund for declined, expired or undelivered offers;
- private notification is best effort, while the pending offer remains discoverable from the Shynok screen.

Leaderboard spending is counted once from the buyer purchase, not from accepted cups.

Old `KorchmaRoundPurchase` rows must remain valid.

### 7. Mantok sale selector

Add `💰 Продати манатки` in the Shynok.

Eligibility:

- canonical `goldValue > 0`;
- known item content;
- not `priceless`;
- not equipped;
- not protected/story/quest/memorial;
- not reserved by another destructive inventory draft.

Because equipment currently references content `itemId`, protect the whole stack for an equipped item ID.

Support:

- selecting one unit;
- selecting several units across stacks;
- `Усе придатне`;
- clear selection;
- pagination;
- preview and explicit confirm;
- cancel/back.

Keep selection server-side behind a short opaque token. Reuse narrow Mantok Chest selector helpers only when that produces a smaller safe diff; do not force a repository-wide selector refactor.

### 8. Sale formula and transaction

Compute once for the full selected basket:

```text
nominal = sum(unit_gold_value * selected_quantity)
payout = floor(nominal * 42 / 100)
```

Use integer arithmetic.

- Disable confirmation when payout is zero.
- Do not add CHA/LUCK/class/calendar modifiers.
- Preview shows selected quantities, nominal total and exact payout.
- `Продати все` means all eligible units only.

Confirm must run in one DB transaction:

1. validate pending token, owner, status and expiry;
2. reread inventory, equipment, reservations and current item content;
3. recompute eligibility, fingerprint, nominal value and payout;
4. return `stale-selection` without mutation on drift;
5. guarded status claim plus guarded-decrement/delete selected units; any decrement failure rolls back the whole confirmation;
6. increment character gold;
7. mark the sale completed and store replay details.

Repeated confirm returns the stored result without a second inventory or gold mutation.

### 9. Persistence and migration

Add the smallest schema that safely owns:

- current character drink state;
- idempotent self-drink orders;
- social round recipient offers/acceptance;
- pending/completed manatka sale drafts and replay summaries.

Extending `KorchmaRoundPurchase` is allowed and preferred when backward compatible. Equivalent normalized storage is acceptable if it preserves all acceptance criteria.

Add a new migration. Do not modify existing migrations.

### 10. Player-facing copy

All player-facing copy is Ukrainian and follows project spelling/style:

- `Квестарня`, `Шинок`, `пригодник`, `манатки`;
- `«»` quotes;
- `соціяльний` spelling where used;
- short Telegram cards;
- exact prices/effects are allowed on current purchase previews;
- no hidden future rewards or odds.

Use the copy seeds from `docs/SHYNOK_DRINKS_AND_MANTOK_SALES.md`, adapting grammar to the current presenters.

### 11. Observability

Add bounded aggregate/audit fields or logs for:

- drink purchases and replacements;
- vodka consumed vs expired;
- round snapshot/accept/decline/expiry counts;
- sale nominal/payout/unit counts;
- stale sale rejects.

Do not store private message bodies.

## Non-goals

- Coffee or cooldown acceleration/rebound.
- Hangovers, addiction or intoxication levels.
- Drink crafting or drink inventory items.
- Food buff system.
- Any drink power in PvP.
- General shop, buyback, auction, market or player trading.
- Selling equipped, priceless, protected, story, quest or unknown items.
- Item-instance migration.
- Variable 42% rate, bargaining, CHA/LUCK modifiers.
- Mini App.
- Broad inventory architecture rewrite.
- Reward/economy rebalance outside these purchases and sales.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Shynok exposes self drinks, social beer and manatka sale only from a valid Korchma interior state.
2. Tea costs exactly 17 gold and applies only ×1.13 out-of-combat recovery for 42 minutes.
3. Simple/fine beer use 13/42 gold, 23/42 minutes, ×1.25/×1.50 recovery and −5/−10 pp accuracy.
4. Pepper vodka costs 42 gold, waits at most 23 minutes and is consumed by exactly one eligible PvE fight.
5. Only one current drink exists; replacement is explicit and duplicate callbacks cannot double-spend.
6. Recovery calculations are piecewise and non-retroactive.
7. Combat modifiers are frozen server-side and old combat JSON remains readable.
8. PvP, starter and training fights receive no drink power.
9. `Всім пива` previews the exact dynamic price from a stored recipient snapshot.
10. Each round recipient can accept/decline at most once; expiry and duplicate callbacks are safe.
11. Existing generosity leaderboards still aggregate buyer spending and legacy rows remain readable.
12. Sale selection supports one, several and all eligible units with pagination and preview.
13. Payout is exactly `floor(sum(value × qty) × 42 / 100)`.
14. Equipped/protected/priceless/unknown/reserved units cannot be sold.
15. Stale inventory/content/equipment state causes a no-mutation refresh/error.
16. Completed sale callback replay cannot grant gold or consume items twice.
17. Telegram delivery/edit failure does not duplicate committed economy state.
18. New migrations validate and existing migrations are untouched.
19. Relevant docs, compact Codex context, changelog/news, package version and lockfile are updated consistently.
20. Full check and focused tests pass.
