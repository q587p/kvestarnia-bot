# 0.2.3 - Threat Escalation MVP

## Goal

Replace the old eligible ordinary three-win monster-rest block with replay-safe threat escalation: after three consecutive eligible one-enemy ordinary victories, the next eligible ordinary persistent fight starts with exactly two enemies.

## Scope

- Exactly two threat tiers:
  - base: one enemy;
  - escalated: exactly two enemies.
- Ordinary normal persistent fight starts only, including direct `/fight` and Nyz passage attack paths that share the canonical ordinary start.
- The decision is derived from existing durable `solo_combat_sessions` history.
- No Prisma schema or migration change.
- The escalation decision is frozen when the combat session is created.
- Escalated sessions store compact `CombatState.threat` metadata with line id/version, enemy count, reason and eligible win count.
- The stored line renders on intro, active, restart and terminal replay cards.
- Rewards stay the existing single-encounter contract for the whole fight.

## Eligibility Contract

Threat history considers completed ordinary persistent PvE sessions that:

- have `CombatState.source === "normal"`;
- are not starter combat;
- are not the training doppelganger;
- are not Yeger or Adventure fights;
- are not duels;
- are one-enemy encounters, unless they are a stored 0.2.3 escalated two-enemy checkpoint;
- have a canonical terminal result.

Wins only count after stored settlement is completed when settlement metadata exists. Loss, flee and expiry in an eligible one-enemy encounter break the streak. A stored escalated two-enemy terminal encounter is a checkpoint regardless of terminal result, so the next ordinary start returns to one enemy until three new eligible one-enemy wins accumulate.

Dev-forced `/dev_two_enemies` sessions do not carry threat metadata and are ignored by the ordinary threat policy: they neither trigger nor consume escalation.

## Implementation Notes

- `src/domain/combat/threatEscalation.ts` owns the pure streak policy and the 13 stable Ukrainian escalation lines.
- `FightService` maps durable combat history into policy entries and applies the result only for normal ordinary starts without explicit targets, dev bypass or explicit `enemyCount`.
- Escalated starts reuse the existing `enemyCount: 2` foundation path from `0.2.1`.
- The second enemy is selected through current ordinary monster selection and avoids duplicating the primary when the eligible pool permits.
- Existing one-enemy starts omit `CombatState.threat` and keep the one-enemy JSON shape.
- `/dev_reset_monster_rest` remains dev-only and harmless for local cleanup, but the production start denial is no longer used for eligible ordinary fights.

## Manual Telegram QA

Recommended local pass:

1. Complete fewer than three eligible ordinary wins; next fight stays one-enemy.
2. Complete the third consecutive eligible one-enemy win.
3. Start the next ordinary fight; it has exactly two enemies and stable escalation copy.
4. Restart during that fight; enemies and copy restore exactly.
5. Wait for timeout and then kill the primary; second enemy becomes primary.
6. Finish and replay old buttons; reward/settlement occurs once.
7. Start the next ordinary fight; it returns to one enemy.
8. Break a streak with loss/flee/expiry and verify no escalation.
9. Start Yeger, Adventure, training, duel and dev-forced routes; verify exclusions.
10. Verify old monster-rest denial no longer blocks an eligible ordinary start.

## Non-Goals

- No Yeger escalation.
- No Adventure escalation.
- No training, duel or starter escalation.
- No three-or-more enemies.
- No location encounter pools.
- No bystander joins.
- No party or raid runtime.
- No per-enemy reward scaling or `0.75x` reward model.
- No new schema or migration.
