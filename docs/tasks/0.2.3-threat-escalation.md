# 0.2.3 - Threat Escalation MVP

## Goal

Replace the old eligible ordinary three-win monster-rest block with replay-safe threat escalation: after three consecutive eligible one-enemy ordinary victories, the next eligible ordinary persistent fight starts with exactly two enemies.

## Scope

- Exactly two threat tiers:
  - base: one enemy;
  - escalated: exactly two enemies.
- Ordinary normal persistent fight starts only, including direct `/fight` and Nyz passage attack paths that share the canonical ordinary start.
- The decision is derived from existing durable `solo_combat_sessions` history.
- No Prisma schema or migration change.
- The escalation decision is frozen when the combat session is created.
- Escalated sessions store compact `CombatState.threat` metadata with line id/version, enemy count, reason and eligible win count.
- The stored line renders on intro, active, restart and terminal replay cards.
- Rewards stay the existing single-encounter contract for the whole fight.
- After a won escalated two-enemy fight, the next eligible ordinary start is escalated again immediately and boosts only the second enemy by +2 effective levels per consecutive won escalated fight.

## Eligibility Contract

Threat history considers completed ordinary persistent PvE sessions that:

- have `CombatState.source === "normal"`;
- are not starter combat;
- are not the training doppelganger;
- are not Yeger or Adventure fights;
- are not duels;
- are one-enemy encounters, unless they are a stored 0.2.3 escalated two-enemy checkpoint;
- have a canonical terminal result.

Wins only count after stored settlement is completed when settlement metadata exists. Loss, flee and expiry in an eligible one-enemy encounter break the streak. A lost, fled or expired escalated two-enemy terminal encounter resets the cycle to base threat. A won escalated two-enemy terminal encounter keeps the pressure high even while reward settlement is still pending: the next eligible ordinary start is escalated again immediately.

Each consecutive won escalated two-enemy checkpoint strengthens only the next fight's second enemy by another +2 effective levels: +2 after one won pair, +4 after two, and so on. Lost, fled or expired escalated checkpoints reset the cycle without this bonus.

Dev-forced `/dev_two_enemies` sessions carry a compact `CombatState.threatExclusion` marker, not `CombatState.threat`, and are ignored by the ordinary threat policy: they neither trigger nor consume escalation.

## Implementation Notes

- `src/domain/combat/threatEscalation.ts` owns the pure streak policy and the 13 stable Ukrainian escalation lines.
- `FightService` maps durable combat history into policy entries and applies the result only for normal ordinary starts without explicit targets, dev bypass or explicit `enemyCount`.
- Direct starts and Nyz passage attacks decide escalation only when the combat session is created, not when a passage preview is shown.
- Escalated direct starts reuse the existing `enemyCount: 2` foundation path from `0.2.1`; escalated passage starts keep the previewed monster as `enemies[0]` and select only the second enemy through current ordinary monster selection.
- Passage second-enemy selection uses the stored pending encounter seed/difficulty/origin as deterministic input and avoids duplicating the primary when the eligible pool permits.
- Completed threat history scans up to 200 recent completed solo sessions, then maps and orders candidates by canonical `completedAt DESC` before policy filtering. Excluded rows inside that bound do not consume the logical streak window; malformed terminal rows and ordinary two-enemy rows without valid threat metadata reset to base rather than falsely escalating.
- Dev-forced two-enemy rows are the explicit exception to that conservative reset: they persist `threatExclusion.reason = "dev-forced-two-enemies"` so local QA sessions remain invisible to ordinary threat history.
- Escalated two-enemy terminal rows are mapped as eligible checkpoints even when their won settlement is still pending. A won checkpoint immediately keeps the next ordinary start escalated; a lost, fled or expired checkpoint resets to base threat.
- Repeat escalation after consecutive won two-enemy checkpoints keeps the first enemy at the normal selected level and applies a cumulative +2 effective-level bonus per won checkpoint only to the second enemy on both direct starts and Nyz passage attacks.
- Legacy consumed-passage HP recovery is disabled for stored multi-enemy/threat sessions, so an escalated two-enemy passage cannot collapse into a fake one-enemy continuation.
- Persistent fight intros show the full opponent roster once. Nyz passage `Атакувати` callbacks and ordinary edited-callback starts that create a new fight keep this intro before the active combat card. Later active, stale and terminal state cards avoid repeating `Проти вас` and use compact HP rows with short monster labels.
- Persistent fight state cards include the current turn number in the heading as `N хід`, announce newly defeated enemies/final defeated enemies with full monster names, and call out the next target after a two-enemy target switch.
- Persistent fight journal pages render stored two-enemy HP snapshots as per-enemy rows and preserve zero-damage enemy misses in replay text.
- Monster runtime effects now render concrete Ukrainian effect text with remaining actions/charges, and ongoing hero-side tick damage is stored separately as `heroEffectDamage` so fight cards can show it apart from the direct monster response.
- Hero attack/class-skill turns resolve enemy response after hero damage even when the target reaches 0 HP. In multi-enemy fights, enemies living at the start of the hero exchange answer in that same turn; enemies already dead before the turn stay silent.
- Separate post-fight problem-chain progress cards explicitly remind players that a two-enemy problem fight still counts as one problem; one-enemy fight result and progress cards stay quiet.
- The generic main-menu movement refresh is suppressed after `v1:fight:*` callbacks, so passage attack/skill/turn cards do not append a separate `Ви пішли у прямий прохід.` navigation notice after the combat card.
- Existing one-enemy starts omit `CombatState.threat` and keep the one-enemy JSON shape.
- `/dev_reset_monster_rest` remains dev-only and harmless for local cleanup, but the production start denial is no longer used for eligible ordinary fights.

## Manual Telegram QA

Recommended local pass:

1. Complete fewer than three eligible ordinary wins; next fight stays one-enemy.
2. Complete the third consecutive eligible one-enemy win.
3. Start the next ordinary fight; it has exactly two enemies and stable escalation copy.
4. Restart during that fight; enemies and copy restore exactly.
5. Wait for timeout and then kill the primary; second enemy becomes primary.
6. Finish and replay old buttons; reward/settlement occurs once.
7. Start the next ordinary fight after winning the two-enemy fight; it is two-enemy again, with only the second enemy +2 effective levels.
8. Win another escalated two-enemy fight; the next ordinary fight is still two-enemy, with only the second enemy +4 effective levels.
9. Break a streak with loss/flee/expiry in an eligible one-enemy or escalated two-enemy fight and verify the next ordinary start returns to one enemy.
10. Start Yeger, Adventure, training, duel and dev-forced routes; verify exclusions.
11. Verify old monster-rest denial no longer blocks an eligible ordinary start.

## Non-Goals

- No Yeger escalation.
- No Adventure escalation.
- No training, duel or starter escalation.
- No three-or-more enemies.
- No location encounter pools.
- No bystander joins.
- No party or raid runtime.
- No per-enemy reward scaling, increased XP/gold, increased manatka quantity/quality or `0.75x` reward model.
- No new schema or migration.
