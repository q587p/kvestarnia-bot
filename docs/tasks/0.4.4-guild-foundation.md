# 0.4.4 — Guild Foundation

Status: corrected on PR #190; automated release gates pass. Exact-head manual
three-account Telegram QA remains to be recorded.

Design: `docs/design/guilds-and-party-progression.md`

## Goal

Ship a durable, privacy-safe social shell for a small guild while reusing the
ordinary temporary `PartySession` contract and adding no shared economy,
progression or combat power.

## Accepted contract

- Founder eligibility is level 5+, or level 3+ after at least one remort. Joining
  is free and has no progression gate.
- One replaceable creation preview per User lasts 13 minutes. Confirm debits 587
  gold exactly once, consumes a durable rolling seven-day founder cooldown,
  creates a seven-day `forming` charter and leader membership, and terminalizes
  all incoming invitations. The fee is non-refundable.
- The first second distinct User activates the guild. Only activation records
  `guild.created`; the founder and joiner achievement events use that durable
  activation result. Forming expiry closes memberships and invitations.
- Historical names remain stored. A separate reservation key releases at
  forming charter expiry plus 23 hours, or active disband plus 30 days.
- Names are 3–32 graphemes with canonical normalization and mixed-script/basic
  safety checks. Description is 0–93 graphemes. Crest is one of 13 server-owned
  emoji. Active membership caps at 8; exactly one leader and at most two
  officers are enforced.
- Target-bound opt-in codes replace global player-name lookup. Invite TTL is 93
  hours, with one live guild-target pair, at most three incoming invitations,
  at most three new invitations per actor per rolling 13 minutes, and a
  seven-day guild-target decline cooldown.
- `🏰 Ґільдії` in the persistent main menu provides the primary entry to
  charter creation and the private invitation. Creation explains eligibility
  and cost first, then asks for crest, name and optional description in separate
  steps before preview/confirm. Private Telegram share links invoke the
  canonical target-code invitation flow; accept/decline remain explicit
  target-side buttons and notify the inviter once. Commands remain fallback routes.
- Leader may edit crest/description, change roles, kick, offer leadership and
  sole-member disband. Current officers may invite and cancel only their own
  invitations. Leadership changes only after nominee acceptance. A
  multi-member leader cannot leave or disband; there is no inferred successor.
- Profile and role management are button-first: the leader opens `Учасники`,
  selects a stable membership row and then an authorized action. Duplicate
  Character names remain independently selectable. An active member also sees
  `🏰 Ґільдія` on the character card.
- Membership and leadership belong to `User` and survive remort and safe
  Character recreation. Live party/combat contracts still block `/restart`.

## PartySession and privacy boundary

- `/guild_party` is only a five-row picker for an already-existing eligible
  gameplay `PartySession` in recruiting state. It never creates a generic lobby.
- Delivery reuses the ordinary invitation, `joinSource=guild` attribution and
  canonical party-card sender. Ordinary nonmember routes stay available.
- Guild membership/version and party eligibility are revalidated before
  delivery. Guild leave, kick, disband and safe restart do not mutate or own an
  existing `PartySession` or `GroupCombatSession`.
- Roster, invitations and audit expose no exact location, activity/online time,
  Telegram identity or invite token. Candidate, roster and invite pages contain
  at most five rows and callbacks stay within Telegram limits.

## Rollout and non-goals

`GUILD_FOUNDATION_ENABLED` remains false by default. Disabled mode preserves
rows and safe reads, nonleader leave, accepted leadership transfer and
sole-member disband, while blocking formation, acceptance/new invitations,
roles, profile changes and guild-party invitations. `/dev_guild_gold` exists
only outside production.

No bank, shared gold/items, trade, market, guild XP/levels, buffs, weekly goal,
boss, chat, alliance, war, territory, PvP or guild-owned party/combat state is
part of 0.4.4. Quests do not change. `📖 Перекази` adds one current-customs
entry that explains the shipped guild shell without promising a bank, shared
party or combat ownership. The rewardless create/join achievements remain in
the achievement catalog and activation/join hooks; no duplicate definitions
are added for the guided UI.

## Release-critical verification

- Real concurrent SQLite repository calls cover equivalent/different name
  confirms, cooldown CAS, cross-guild accepts, final roster slot and officer cap.
- Creation tests cover replacement, expiry, retention cleanup, insufficient
  gold, duplicate/stale confirm, activation exactly once and 23-hour/30-day name
  release.
- Lifecycle tests put more than the 23-row cleanup batch ahead of a relevant
  charter and prove acceptance, new invitation, hub and mutation paths
  terminalize that specific overdue guild exactly once while an unexpired
  forming guild remains usable. Target-membership checks terminalize the
  target User's specific expired forming guild behind the same backlog.
- Name-reservation tests put more than two cleanup batches ahead of the exact
  owner and prove a due charter plus hold releases for a same-name confirm,
  while a still-live hold stays unavailable without gold or cooldown debit.
- Role tests cover profile permissions, officer ownership, demotion/kick/leave
  cancellation invalidation, guild-wide hub expiry, leader-versus-officer
  cancellation controls, offered/accepted transfer and leader exit rules.
- Adapter tests cover flag-off recovery, production dev-helper absence, blocked
  Telegram delivery with `/guild` recovery, inert disabled legacy guild-party
  callbacks, stale guild-party audiences and the ordinary canonical party-card
  message reference, guided ForceReply creation/invitation steps, invalid crest
  recovery, separate name/description prompts, share deep links, profile/member
  menus, active-character navigation and exact-once inviter response notices.
- Hub tests cover one combined five-row member/invitation stream, stable current
  page callbacks, invitation overflow without repeated members, exact-name role
  actions and membership-backed selectors for duplicate names.
- Real remort and `/restart` tests preserve User membership, block live party or
  combat deletion, allow expired non-automatic recruiting residue to stop
  blocking recreation, and keep unrelated party/combat rows across guild
  lifecycle and migration rollback/restore.

## Production-enable blocker

An abandoned multi-member leader has no presence-derived automatic successor.
Before target enablement, the release operator owns a runbook decision: either
keep the flag off or use an audited operator-only recovery with an explicitly
approved inactivity threshold and nominee/consent procedure. No such operator
mutation is shipped in 0.4.4.
