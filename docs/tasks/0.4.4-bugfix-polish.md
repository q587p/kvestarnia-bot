# 0.4.4 — Bugfix & Polish

Status: merged through PR `#191` at `67bd02cdd1e9c73e9f8fbe8ebdcffb7ac279af4c` and observed on the production runtime. Formal three-account Telegram QA remains unrecorded; production feedback exposed a post-merge SQLite delivery-contention defect now owned by the unnumbered GroupCombat delivery hotfix.

## Goal

Polish three already-shipped player paths without adding a new progression system: make GroupCombat targeting explicit and compact, make left-passage attack invitations safe to open from anywhere, and mark owned equipment whose canonical requirements are not met.

## Scope

- GroupCombat cards expose one Attack, Defend and one button per available class/race/gear ability. Attack and explicit single-target abilities commit directly when exactly one canonical target remains and open a target-only picker for two or more targets; auto-target abilities commit directly. `↩️ До дій` returns without changing combat state.
- Enemy focus follows the Big Barrel contract: the living party leader is the initial target, then the participant who dealt the most actual damage in the previous resolved turn leads enemy attention, with stable roster-order ties. Active cards and each newly stored journal turn mark `🎯 ціль ворогів`, and the recap announces focus changes; legacy journal snapshots without focus evidence remain unmarked. Additive `enemyFocusVersion: 1` state evidence separates this semantic from legacy cumulative threat: an active v2/v3 state without the marker resumes through the living leader once, then persists exact current-turn damage for normal next-turn focus.
- Presentation callbacks are bounded, distinct from canonical action callbacks and backward-compatible with legacy action parsing. Reply-menu pickers are temporary and cleaned up after selection/back.
- The exact left-passage payload is `nyz_left_attack_<token>`. It is bound to the canonical origin and relocates only an otherwise eligible joiner atomically with party membership. Legacy `party_` links retain the same safe behavior for a recognized left-passage gathering.
- Left-passage recruiting reuses the invite-card flow with deterministic rotating Ukrainian copy, a visible canonical URL, leader/count/readiness/deadline, and `🔗 Покликати до атаки`; the compact preparation card omits the raw URL and future reward details.
- Owned equippable items show `🔎 🔒` when the shared canonical level/class/race/title evaluator rejects them. Equipped `✅` wins. Detail remains reachable and shows the same reasons without an Equip action. Inventory loads character/equipment once and evaluates rows in memory.
- Reconcile merged PRs `#188` (`0.4.3`) and `#189` (local-runtime supervision/log retention). Privacy-safe combat debug instrumentation remains deferred.

## Non-goals

- No ґільдія runtime, schema, migration, feature flag or work from PR `#190`.
- No combat damage, AI ability, reward, timer, roster-size or singleton-target behavior changes beyond the explicit GroupCombat enemy-focus rule; no new items, achievements, lore promises or production instrumentation.
- No change to solo left-passage fighting.

## Acceptance criteria

- Picker opening/back is read-only; final taps use the canonical action path and retain stale/duplicate/timeout validation.
- Wrong-origin, malformed, expired, full, dead, stale-life, invalid-resource, active combat/search/rest/adventure/raid and other-party joins do not relocate the player.
- Active/completed GroupCombat links route participants to their canonical card/result.
- Missing bot username degrades without emitting a broken URL.
- List/detail/preview/equip agree on requirement state, including Loot Expansion and title buckets, with no per-row repository calls.
- `Щоби відгукнутися` is absent from active runtime, tests and docs.

## Second-Codex follow-up

- Social routing and combat-lock recognition cover every supported GroupCombat callback generation (`v1`–`v5`); a production-shaped middleware/module regression proves inline v5 target-menu and Back are each handled once without combat mutation.
- Left-passage creation plus deferred recruiting-card refresh/redaction use the origin-aware URL builder. Big Barrel and generic party URLs remain unchanged.
- Canonical equipment requirements precede two-hand and attunement confirmation paths in preview and commit, including confirmed callbacks; combined regressions cover list locks and concrete detail reasons.
- The party-combat evolution sequence now records this release as Bugfix & Polish and shifts the accepted follow-up plan through `0.4.13`.
- One canonical Left Passage predicate binds both `originKind` and the exact left-passage location across deep links, GroupCombat replay, session URLs, controls and presentation. A matching kind at any other location is not a Left Passage session and exposes no combat/session details through `nyz_left_attack_*`.
- Canonical preparation rendering rechecks GroupCombat after a successful origin-bound join. If combat started in that interval, it delivers the participant's current active combat card once instead of replying with a stale preparation card; terminal results retain their canonical rendering.
- The GroupCombat replacement/final-action and replacement/timeout integration races accept either linearization winner, including `replacement.state === "resolved"`, while asserting exactly one resolver and canonical stored recap evidence.
- Left-passage preparation keeps `⚔️ Почати атаку` as the final visible row after invite controls, answers new creation with `Ватагу відкрито.`, and uses nominative numeric deadline forms after the colon (`1 хвилина`, `2 хвилини`, `5 хвилин`).
- The unchanged PartyBoss due-timeout integration case isolates its target from older active fixtures before asserting the repository's bounded default due page; this removes order-dependent overflow without widening the production batch.
- Newly created and relationally rebuilt GroupCombat states persist `enemyFocusVersion: 1`. Active v2/v3 state without that additive marker treats old cumulative threat as non-authoritative, presents and targets the living leader for the first post-upgrade turn, then records exact resolved-turn damage; proof/production restart regressions cover zero and tied damage plus deterministic roster-order fallback.
- In a multi-enemy GroupCombat, a single-target ability recap names the enemy that actually lost HP. Mixed support/damage actions such as `✨ Суворе благословення` no longer report an unlabeled damage number that can be mistaken for damage to another low-HP enemy; monster shield absorption remains accumulated under the existing `🛡️ відвернена шкода` statistic. The named-target recap keeps the measured 25-turn 3x6 production journal at `64,827/65,536` bytes (`709` bytes of headroom), while the 24-case hardening simulation peaks at `20,518/65,536` bytes.

## Post-merge SQLite delivery hotfix

- Production evidence on merged head `67bd02cd` showed `P1008` socket timeouts, expired `P2028` transactions and stale first-turn/later-turn cards while GroupCombat published all participant cards concurrently against SQLite.
- GroupCombat now publishes the initiating participant's canonical card first, then processes the remaining roster serially. A failed participant remains isolated and does not prevent later participants from receiving their current card.
- Pending-delivery scans defer a recently attempted row for 13 seconds, reducing recovery write pressure without changing combat state, turn timing, rewards, balance, schema, migrations or rollout flags.
- Regression coverage uses a production-shaped three-participant last-ready sequence, proves that only the initiating participant owns the first in-flight publication claim, verifies visible current-turn cards in actor-first order, preserves partial `P1008` recovery and covers the retry cutoff in the Prisma repository.
