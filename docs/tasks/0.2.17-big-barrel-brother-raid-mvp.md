# 0.2.17 - Big Barrel Brother Raid MVP

## Goal

Ship the first feature-flagged Big Barrel Brother route for level `8+` characters, or remorted level `3+` characters, on top of the temporary party foundation and one-boss runtime.

## Naming and allusion

Use `Big Barrel Brother` as the English planning and technical name to preserve the Big Brother surveillance allusion from Orwell's *Nineteen Eighty-Four* as summarized in <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Brother_(Nineteen_Eighty-Four)>. Keep the Ukrainian player-facing boss name `Старший Брат Бочки`, following the established Ukrainian rendering `Старший Брат` summarized in <https://uk.wikipedia.org/wiki/Старший_Брат>. Do not rename Ukrainian player-facing copy away from that form.

## Scope

- Add `BIG_BARREL_BROTHER_RAID_ENABLED`.
- Keep non-remorted level `1-7`, remorted level `1-2`, and pre-existing legacy pending Barrel rows on the legacy Barrel route.
- Create Big Barrel Brother recruiting parties from `/raid` or the explicit Barrel card action only while the flag is enabled for non-remorted level `8+` characters or remorted level `3+` characters.
- Keep ordinary Barrel place entry/current-location resume on the familiar Barrel card first, with a separate player button to opt into Big Barrel Brother recruiting.
- Reuse `PartySession`, `PartyParticipant`, `party_boss_sessions`, `party_boss_actions` and `ActiveCombatLease(kind = "party-boss")`.
- Start Big parties with `rulesVersion = big-barrel-brother-v1`, `bossKey = big-barrel-brother`, frozen roster/resources/boss HP and one shared boss target.
- Let each living participant submit one action per turn; duplicate/stale callbacks replay canonical state.
- Timeout resolution fills missing actions with deterministic defend.
- Victory claims the canonical `tavern.friday-barrel-raid` success key for meaningful participants in the frozen Barrel period, with stored XP/gold/resource outcome and the existing Barrel item grants.
- Big party start blocks with a generic ineligible result when any joined participant is not currently eligible for the frozen Barrel period; settlement also rechecks level, remort life and existing success before writing rewards.
- Production timeout resolution may fill missing actions only when `turnExpiresAt <= now`; the early force-timeout path is reserved for dev helpers.
- Failure grants no success, gold or items in this slice.
- Terminal resolution releases party-boss leases and live party membership keys.

## Non-goals

- No ґільдія systems, permanent parties, matchmaking, public roster, market, trade, crafting, auction or Mini App work.
- No broad combat rewrite, targetable adds, combat items, one-use manatky, healing role, resurrection, full threat/aggro system or loot-master flow.
- The only targeting follow-up in this slice is a narrow Big Barrel Brother focus rule: ordinary retaliations first hit the party leader, then the living participant who dealt the most damage in the previous round; every fourth turn keeps a broad hit against all living participants until a future explicit threat system replaces this temporary cadence.
- No hidden runtime cap, enrage timer, turn-number terminal pressure or 13-round auto-loss.
- No exact reward/odds/formula preview before commitment.
- No affinity spotlight/trophy subsystem yet; rewards intentionally reuse the existing Barrel grant set.

## Acceptance criteria

- Non-remorted level 7 and remorted level 2 legacy Barrel behavior remains unchanged.
- Non-remorted level 8+ and remorted level 3+ open Big Barrel Brother recruiting only behind `BIG_BARREL_BROTHER_RAID_ENABLED`.
- Explicit `/raid` and `🍺 У рейд на бочку` starts create/open Big Barrel Brother recruiting; plain Barrel entry/current-location resume keep the old Barrel card visible instead of auto-creating a party.
- Big Barrel Brother recruiting cards keep an active `https://t.me/<bot>?start=party_<token>` link while recruiting is live when `BOT_USERNAME` is configured.
- Deep links and nearby raid buttons join the exact same party token.
- Deep links opened after the party starts or finishes show the active boss card or stored result instead of an expired recruiting fallback.
- Join/leave actions best-effort refresh other stored recruiting cards, including the leader's original card.
- `👀 Хто поруч` at the Barrel lists live Big Barrel Brother recruiting parties with participants and one join button per leader when multiple groups are open.
- Recruiting parties automatically start the Big Barrel Brother fight when their recruiting deadline is due, even below full roster size.
- Starting freezes roster, period, boss scale and participant resources.
- Big Barrel Brother freezes boss level from the current party leader, not from the roster average, so lower-level joiners do not lower a high-level starter's boss tier.
- Every living participant can submit exactly one action per turn.
- Private active battle cards follow ordinary combat shape: turn heading, viewer HP/mana, boss HP, participant target marker, concrete action buttons, 23-second turn hint, and no live journal button.
- Private raid keyboards use concrete class/race ability labels and the same availability semantics as ordinary combat.
- The battle journal opens only after terminal state, is paginated one stored round per page, and includes action descriptions plus boss target/focus-switch notes.
- Timeout fills missing actions as defend only through all-actions resolution, production due-timeout after the deadline, or dev-helper force-timeout.
- Runtime ends only when boss HP reaches `0`, all active participants are unable to continue, or lifecycle cleanup cancels/invalidates the raid.
- A fight still active after the 13-round simulation horizon remains active in runtime.
- Deterministic Big Barrel Brother balance probes by 13-round horizon: solo baseline `0/400` wins (`0%`), prepared 3-player entry party `181/400` wins (`45.25%`), full same-level level-8 party `350/400` wins (`87.5%`), full same-level level-13 party `345/400` wins (`86.25%`), and level-13 leader with seven lower-level joiners `0/400` wins (`0%`) in the checked matrix.
- Start rejects a Big party if a joined participant fails the current eligibility gate, already completed the same frozen Barrel period, has a remort-life mismatch, or is in another active combat lease; the shared result does not name the private reason.
- Settlement fail-closes before Big rewards/success if the participant no longer passes the current eligibility gate, no longer remains in the same remort life, or already has the frozen-period `tavern.friday-barrel-raid` row.
- Victory writes the existing Barrel success gate once per meaningful participant; replay does not duplicate rewards.
- Failure does not write Barrel success.
- Terminal and replay paths release active combat leases/live party keys.
- Local `/dev_raid_reset` clears the current Barrel pending timer/completion gate for same-period QA without granting rewards.
- Local `/dev_raid_win` sets an active Big Barrel Brother boss to `0 HP` for manual QA; the next normal party-boss action or timeout resolution settles the raid through the standard victory path, and boss-zero plus all-participants-zero states resolve as a player win rather than a draw/loss.

2. Confirm non-remorted level 8+ and remorted level 3+ open legacy Barrel when the Big Barrel Brother flag is disabled.
3. Enable the flag locally, reopen `🛢️ Бочка` on an eligible account, and verify the familiar Barrel card appears first with `🍺 У рейд на бочку` rather than auto-creating a party.
4. Tap `🍺 У рейд на бочку`; verify the Big Barrel Brother recruiting card appears, includes the active invite link when `BOT_USERNAME` is configured, and starts with the extra Старший Брат Бочки intervention copy.
5. Open Big Barrel Brother recruiting through `/raid`; verify it reaches the same recruiting surface and keeps the active invite link.
6. Join the same Big Barrel Brother party by deep link and by `👀 Хто поруч`; verify the leader's original recruiting card updates to show the new participant.
7. With one or more recruiting parties open at the Barrel, open `👀 Хто поруч`; verify each group is listed with participant names and, when multiple groups exist, separate join buttons by leader name.
8. Let recruiting time expire without manual leader start; verify the fight starts automatically regardless of whether the roster is full.
9. Start the raid manually as leader; verify private cards show only the viewer's HP/mana, show `23 с`, mark the boss target on participants, and do not show `📜 Журнал` while active.
10. Submit and replay actions from multiple participants.
11. Try a joined under-level or already-completed participant; verify start blocks with a generic raid-office message and no boss session/lease.
12. Verify ordinary boss retaliations first hit the party leader, then follow the previous round's highest damage contributor; verify turn 4 hits all living participants.
12a. Finish or force a terminal state, then open `📜 Журнал`; verify pages show one round at a time, action descriptions, boss target and next-focus notes.
12b. Open the original `https://t.me/<bot>?start=party_<token>` invite after terminal state; verify it shows the stored raid result, not an expired recruiting message.
13. Start one full same-level raid and one level-13-leader raid with lower-level joiners; verify the boss card freezes the boss at the leader's level and the lower-level roster feels materially more dangerous.
14. With dev helpers disabled, replay or forge a timeout callback before the deadline; verify the turn stays active. Then wait past the deadline and verify missing actions defend.
15. With dev helpers enabled, use the dev timeout control and verify missing actions defend before the deadline.
16. Continue past rounds 7 and 13 in a controlled high-HP scenario and verify no automatic loss by round number.
17. Finish a win; replay result/actions and confirm rewards/success do not duplicate.
18. Use `/dev_raid_reset` locally when the same account needs another Barrel/Big Barrel Brother attempt in the same raid period without waiting for the next `:23` period boundary.
18a. During an active Big Barrel Brother fight with dev helpers enabled, run `/dev_raid_win`, then submit any remaining action or press the dev timeout control; verify the next resolution is a victory. Repeat once with all participant HP manually forced to `0` and confirm boss `0 HP` still wins for players.
19. Finish a loss and confirm no Barrel success/beer gate is written.
20. Remort/invalid participant smoke on disposable characters.
