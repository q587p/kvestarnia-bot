# 0.2.x - Player Help Against a Monster

## Goal

Let a player with an eligible active monster fight call one or two nearby players for help, turning the encounter into a small shared fight of exactly 2–3 participants total.

This is a future task. It is not the Big Barrel Brother raid, a public group hunt, or a full party/raid engine.

## Scope

- Add a private, opt-in `🆘 Покликати на підмогу` action on eligible ordinary monster fights.
- Offer only active, same-location players who are visible through the existing nearby/presence surface and pass server-side eligibility checks.
- Allow one owner plus at most two accepted helpers; the roster becomes immutable when the shared fight starts.
- Send helpers a private invitation with accept/decline/ignore and a short expiry. Never add a player silently.
- Reject invitations and accepts for players in combat, a pending/active raid, a duel, a blocked quest/fight flow, a different location, a different remort life, or an incompatible encounter type.
- Reuse the existing combat action and journal shape where possible: each living participant gets one action per round, missing actions use the documented safe default, and the shared state stores the frozen monster and participant snapshots.
- Keep player help separate from the system `help` battle intervention level delta. Joining a helper must not silently lower the monster level.
- Keep the first slice reward-safe: no automatic multiplication of monster loot, no uncapped XP/gold farm, and an explicit decision for whether helpers receive participation-only recognition or a bounded shared reward.
- Make invitation, accept, roster freeze, round actions, settlement, cancellation and replay idempotent and stale-safe.

## Non-goals

- No more than three total participants.
- No public matchmaking, guilds, permanent parties, party chat, voice or group channel.
- No Big Barrel Brother integration, raid roles, threat table, positioning grid or broad party-buff framework.
- No arbitrary joining of an already terminal fight or stealing another player's encounter.
- No automatic help, forced targeting, PvP, player-versus-player damage or player-to-monster assistance without the owner's opt-in.
- No new reward source until contribution, AFK and abuse rules are specified and simulated.

## Acceptance criteria

- Only an eligible fight owner sees the call-for-help action, and the call can be cancelled before acceptance.
- An invite identifies the monster encounter and owner without exposing unnecessary private character/location data.
- A helper can accept only once, the roster cannot exceed three, and duplicate/stale/expired accepts do not mutate the fight twice.
- Starting the shared fight freezes the roster, remort life, participant resources and monster state; later presence changes do not rewrite it.
- Every participant sees only the actions and resource rows they are allowed to see, and all active/result/journal cards replay the same stored rounds.
- A participant leaving, becoming invalid or timing out has deterministic handling and cannot strand the remaining players or duplicate settlement.
- Rewards and recognition are settled exactly once, with explicit caps and no helper-created reward multiplication.
- Existing solo fights, tutorial/story/fixed-reward encounters, duels, raids and other blocked flows remain unchanged.
- The social-help source is compatible with the future `BattleInterventionSourceType = "player"` extension without forcing it into the first implementation.

## Focused tests

- Eligibility and visibility tests for owner, nearby helper, wrong location, active combat, raid, duel and remort mismatch.
- Invite accept/decline/expiry/cancel and duplicate/stale callback tests.
- Exact 2- and 3-participant roster-cap tests; the fourth player is rejected without mutation.
- Frozen-roster, participant-leave/timeout and shared-round replay tests.
- Reward/recognition idempotency, AFK handling and no-multiplied-loot balance tests.
- Regression tests proving ordinary solo, tutorial, fixed-reward, duel and Big Barrel flows do not gain the help button.
- `npm run check`.

## Manual Telegram QA

Manual QA status: not run in this planning package.

When implemented: start an eligible monster fight, invite one nearby player, accept from the second account, verify both receive the shared card, add a third player, reject a fourth, resolve several rounds, replay the journal/result, and verify stale/expired invitations do not change the fight.
